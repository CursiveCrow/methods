COURSE WEB SITES AND COURSE NOTES

  • Lectures 4 and 5 for CSC2409S 1973 The notes for my Lecture 4 introduce Method I, a precursor for clause learning for SAT solvers, and my Lecture 5 introduces the propositional pigeonhole clauses (attributed to Karp), together with empirical results on the number of resolvents needed by Method I to generate a resolution refutation. The title of that course was "Logic and Mechanical Proof Procedures".

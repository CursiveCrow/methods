L10383:     normal derivation.

L10385:     is to the left of the minor premise(s), if there are any minor premises. We
L10386:     use a main induction on the cutrank n of V, with a subinduction on m, the
L10387:     sum of lengths of all critical cuts (= cut segments) in V.

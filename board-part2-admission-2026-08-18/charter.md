# Board charter: Part II admission review

- Artifact: the live reachable Part II body and proof-annex graph rooted at
  `handbook/parts/p02.tex` and `handbook/annex/p02.tex`, including exact
  restoration wrappers and the separately attributed editorial supplements.
- Purpose: determine whether Part II is mathematically correct, source-faithful,
  complete for its declared scope, and ready to lose its reader-visible OPEN
  notices before the final book build.
- Mode: audit.
- Requested by: the governing objective requires three independent reviews for
  every reader-facing topic and zero unresolved source, content, or register
  obligations before final publication.
- Authorship disclosure: chair-authored and inherited/editorial mixed; reviewers
  did not author the current Part II files.
- Invariants:
  - I1: exact inherited artifacts and their internal order remain unchanged;
  - I2: editorial additions are separately attributed and may survive only when
    correct, source-supported, nonduplicative, and complete for their claims;
  - I3: definitions precede use and every theorem/rule has exact types,
    quantifiers, hypotheses, side conditions, and proof status;
  - I4: no OPEN, unadmitted, owed, pointer-only, stub, unsupported alias, or
    unresolved register claim may remain in an admitted reader section;
  - I5: source, topic, static, build, visual, and user acceptance are separate;
    this board can settle only its review mandates.
- Criteria:
  - C1 source fidelity and one-route/no-duplicate correspondence;
  - C2 mathematical correctness and proof-status accuracy;
  - C3 completeness of each declared Part II topic and cross-part dependency;
  - C4 labels, citations, notation, index entries, and annex links;
  - C5 explicit disposition for every reader-visible OPEN/owed statement.
- Severity taxonomy: blocker, must-fix, should-fix, consider, discard,
  unresolved-user.
- Source rule: file claims cite exact file:line locations; external claims cite
  fetchable primary or authoritative sources; unverified claims cannot block.
- Consensus rule: rough consensus; blind r1 plus at most three focused rounds.
- Roster:
  - source-fidelity: exact authority/restoration/citation/variant correspondence;
  - topic-specialist: formal-logic definitions, rules, theorems, and proofs;
  - adversarial-publication: omissions, aliases, navigation, OPEN debt, and
    likely render failures.
- Board type: live; exact deployment identifiers are unavailable to the chair.
- Workspace: `board-part2-admission-2026-08-18/`.

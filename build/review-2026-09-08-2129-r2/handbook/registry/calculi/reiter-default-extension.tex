\calculussheet{sheet:reiter-default-extension}{Reiter Default-Extension Construction}{Data: a classical default theory $(W,D)$; defaults have prerequisite/justification/conclusion form; for each candidate theory $E$, the construction produces the least grounded closure $\Gamma_E$; skeptical and credulous consequence quantify over the resulting extensions; equality is the equality of the classical base language.}
\noindent\emph{Controlling source:} \cite{Reiter1980}.

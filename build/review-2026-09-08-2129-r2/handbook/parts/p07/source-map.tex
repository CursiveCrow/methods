\section*{Reader source map for Part VII}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Reader source map for Part VII}
\label{sec:p07-source-map}

The nineteen source intervals in this part follow manuscript lines
11354--12248 in their original order.  They contain the calculus definitions,
cut-elimination and normalization material, and the Craig and Beth statements.
Those two statements now link to complete proofs in the annex: Craig includes
functions, constants, equality, and free-variable control; Beth includes a
uniform finite supporting subtheory.  Other results retain their individually
stated proof status.

The registered-calculus comparisons and translations, Maehara proof,
direct relational cut-free completeness and interpolation proofs,
Curry--Howard categorical extension, lambda- and type-theory chapter,
propositional cut assignment, ordinal calibrations, functional-interpretation
survey, generalized-calculus examples, proof-complexity chapter, figures,
rule sheets, and generated register are editorial additions.  Where such an
addition descends from an older historical draft, its file header identifies
the retained range and the substantive delta; the derivative is not presented
as restored source.

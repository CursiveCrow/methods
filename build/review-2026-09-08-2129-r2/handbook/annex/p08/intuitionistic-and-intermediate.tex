\begin{annexitem}{annex:p8.ipl.1}{thm:p08-ipl-persistence}{Persistence of intuitionistic forcing}
\begin{proof}
Induct on \(A\): atoms use \(V\)-persistence, \(\bottom\) is vacuous, and
\(\land,\lor\) use induction.  If \(w\leq v\leq u\), then
\(w\forces B\to C\) and \(u\forces B\) give \(u\forces C\), hence
\(v\forces B\to C\).
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p8.ipl.2}{entry:p08-ipl}{Soundness, strong completeness, and the finite rooted countermodel clause}
\sketchnote{The prime-extension lemma, the failed-implication extension
lemma, the full truth-lemma induction, and the filtration/countermodel-bound
verification are omitted; the cited sources supply those inputs for the exact
calculus and semantics fixed in the card.}
Soundness is an induction on the finite
\hyperref[sheet:lj]{\texttt{sheet:lj}} derivation; persistence supplies the
implication-rule step.

For completeness, extend a premise set that does not derive \(A\) to a prime
theory \(T\): \(T\) is consistent; every \(B\) with
\(T\derives_{\mathsf{IPL}}B\) belongs to \(T\); and whenever
\(T\derives_{\mathsf{IPL}}B\lor C\), it contains \(B\) or \(C\).
Deductive closure makes the displayed derivability condition equivalent to
\(B\lor C\in T\); primeness is relative to derivability from \(T\), not from
the empty set.  Order prime theories by inclusion and force \(p\) at \(T\)
exactly when \(p\in T\).  Structural induction gives the truth lemma
\(T\forces B\) exactly when \(B\in T\); its implication step is the omitted
failed-implication extension lemma.  The resulting world contains every
premise and omits \(A\).

When \(\Gamma\) is finite, filter through the finite set of subformulas of
\(\Gamma\cup\{A\}\).  The omitted filtration verification preserves forcing
of those formulas and yields the stated finite rooted countermodel.
\end{annexitem}

\section{Further reading}

The following annotated route supplies provenance and larger treatments; it
does not upgrade the
status of any result in the preceding chapters.
\begin{itemize}
  \item SAT algorithms, clause learning, and proof logging:
        \par\parencite{DavisLogemannLoveland1962,MarquesSilvaSakallah1999}
        \par\parencite{MarquesSilvaLynceMalik2009,WetzlerHeuleHunt2014}
  \item First-order resolution, refinements, and saturation:
        \par\parencite{Robinson1965,WosRobinsonCarson1965}
        \par\parencite{Slagle1967,BachmairGanzinger2001}
  \item SMT architectures, combination, and standard interfaces:
        \par\parencite{NelsonOppen1979,NieuwenhuisOliverasTinelli2006}
        \par\parencite{BarrettSebastianiSeshiaTinelli2009,SMTLIB2025,TPTP}
  \item Practical automated reasoning and trust boundaries:
        \par\parencite{Harrison2009,Pollack1998}
  \item LCF, AUTOMATH, pure type systems, and inductive constructions:
        \par\parencite{GordonMilnerWadsworth1979,DeBruijn1970}
        \par\parencite{Barendregt1992,PaulinMohring2015}
\end{itemize}

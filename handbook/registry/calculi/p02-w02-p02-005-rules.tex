\calculussheet{sheet:p02-w02-p02-005-rules}{Barwise's Gentzen-type systems \(\mathbf G\) and \(\mathbf G^{+}\)}{Language: a first-order language \(L\) with equality, as in \cref{def:p02-w02-p02-005-bw-system-h}; sequent shape: \(\Gamma\vdash\Delta\) with \(\Gamma,\Delta\) finite sets of formulas and \(\Gamma,\varphi\) for \(\Gamma\cup\{\varphi\}\) (\cref{def:p02-w02-p02-005-bw-sequents}); negation: primitive, with rules \((\neg\vdash)\) and \((\vdash\neg)\); equality: the equality axioms and the equality rule of \cref{def:p02-w02-p02-005-bw-system-g}.  \(\mathbf G\) has every axiom and rule below except cut; \(\mathbf G^{+}\) adds cut.  The last display is not a rule of either system but Barwise's \(\mathbf G^{+}\) derivation for his third derived claim (Barwise, pp.~39--40).}

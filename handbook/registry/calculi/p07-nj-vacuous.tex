% Standard empty-discharge variant. The separate sheet:nj remains nonvacuous.
\calculussheet{sheet:p07-nj-vacuous}{Intuitionistic Natural Deduction $\mathsf{NJ}_{\mathrm v}$}{Finite rooted derivation trees over $\bot,\land,\lor,\to,\forall,\exists$, with $\neg A\defeq A\to\bot$. The finite set $\Gamma$ supports the actual open assumption occurrences; labels specify the occurrences discharged by an application. The discharge classes of $\to I$, both branches of $\lor E$, and the minor derivation of $\exists E$ may be empty. All displayed eigenvariable conditions and total capture-avoiding substitution remain binding. Bound-variable alpha-equivalence is respected; free variables are not renamed. There are no RAA, primitive-negation, equality or induction rules.}

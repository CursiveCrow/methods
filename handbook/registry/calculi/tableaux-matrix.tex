A branch is \emph{saturated} when every retained non-atomic labelled
occurrence has its expansion mark and the component labels chosen for
that expansion occur on the branch.  A labelled nullary connective is
non-atomic for this purpose: its unique empty tuple is used when its
value is correct, and an empty preimage closes the branch otherwise.
An open saturated branch has no atomic label clash.  Distinct occurrences
remain distinct even when their formula strings agree; an expansion mark
belongs to its occurrence, not to all occurrences of that formula.

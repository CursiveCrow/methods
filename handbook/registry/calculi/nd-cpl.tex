\calculussheet{sheet:nd-cpl}{Classical Propositional Natural Deduction ($\mathsf{ND}_{\mathsf{CPL}}$)}{Language: $\mathsf{Fm}_{\mathsf{CPL}}$ with primitives $\bot$, $\neg$, $\land$, $\lor$, $\to$; derivations: finite rooted trees of formula occurrences whose leaves are assumption occurrences and whose other nodes are applications of the twelve rules displayed after the assumption display; discharge labels are metalinguistic natural numbers, and an assumption occurrence is open iff no rule application on its path to the root discharges it; sequent shape: single-conclusion judgments $\Gamma\vdash\varphi$ with $\Gamma$ a finite set of formulas, where $\Gamma\vdash\varphi$ holds iff some derivation has root $\varphi$ and every open assumption occurrence in it is an occurrence of a member of $\Gamma$; members of $\Gamma$ need not occur, and since a derivation has finitely many leaves, derivability from an arbitrary set of formulas is derivability from a finite subset of it; reading of the displays: each display is one step on witnessing derivations, not a closure condition on the relation alone: every premise of an instance stands for a chosen derivation witnessing it, one application of the named rule is placed beneath the roots of these derivations, and the result witnesses the conclusion; the assumption display is the one-node derivation $\varphi$, whose single open occurrence is of a member of $\Gamma\cup\{\varphi\}$, and there is no separate weakening rule; discharge: for a premise displayed as $\Delta\cup\{\varphi\}\vdash\chi$ of a discharging rule, the derivation witnessing it must contain at least one open occurrence of $\varphi$; the application carries a label fresh from every label of the premise derivations, all open occurrences of $\varphi$ in that derivation are first marked with this label by convention~(ii), and the application discharges exactly them, so every open occurrence that remains is of a member of the displayed conclusion context; vacuous discharge is not licensed: a witness with no open occurrence of $\varphi$ gives no instance of the display, and that witness already shows $\Delta\vdash\chi$; label conventions, neither of which is an inference step: (i)~applying one injective map to all assumption labels of a derivation and to the matching labels on its rule applications preserves the derivation and the occurrences each application discharges, and when copies of derivations are grafted onto open assumptions (Lemma~\ref{lem:nd-substitution}) each copy is first renamed into a label set disjoint from every label of the receiving derivation and of every other copy; (ii)~merging relabeling: marking any set of open occurrences of one formula with a common label fresh from every label of the derivation preserves the derivation and the occurrences each application discharges, because no application carries that label, and this is how a newly added final inference discharges several open occurrences at once (as in the proof of Theorem~\ref{thm:prop-completeness}, which uses $\bot E$ when no open occurrence of the negated goal remains and otherwise this relabeling followed by RAA); there is no repetition rule: a repeated formula is the node already present; negation: primitive, with its own rules alongside primitive $\bot$, and RAA supplying classical strength; equality: none (propositional language).}


\begin{definition}[Intuitionistic semantic consequence]
\[
  \Gamma\entails_{\IPL}A
\]
iff, for every intuitionistic Kripke structure \(\mathcal M\) and every
world \(w\), forcing all members of \(\Gamma\) at \(w\) implies forcing \(A\)
at \(w\).
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Soundness and strong completeness of \(\IPL\)]
For every formula set \(\Gamma\) and formula \(A\),
\[
  \Gamma\derives_{\IPL}A
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \Gamma\entails_{\IPL}A.
\]
Moreover, if \(\Gamma\notderives_{\IPL}A\) for finite \(\Gamma\), there is a
finite rooted Kripke structure with a world forcing every member of
\(\Gamma\) and not forcing \(A\).
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Complete proof.}
The soundness proof checks every propositional LJ rule and persistence in
Annex~\ref{annex:p8.ipl.complete.soundness}. The complete prime-extension,
failed-implication extension and canonical truth-lemma arguments establish
Theorem~\ref{thm:p08-ipl-strong-completeness-full} for arbitrary premise sets
in classical ZFC. The separately stated enumerated construction works in
classical ZF when a formula enumeration is supplied. The filtration proof
includes the implication case, and
Theorem~\ref{thm:p08-ipl-finite-rooted-countermodel} constructs the finite
rooted countermodel for each finite nonderiving input. These are proofs for
the same common-world consequence relation, not an inference from classical
validity or a countability assumption on every premise set.

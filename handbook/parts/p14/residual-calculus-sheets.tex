\subsubsection{Catalog boundary}

These residual displays and the preceding calculus chapters preserve the
registered rule surfaces without identifying distinct presentations. A body claim may cite a sheet only when
its calculus identifier, rule list, side conditions, semantics, and status
agree with that registry file. Adding a genuinely different calculus therefore
requires a new attributed registry record; it may not be created by silently
renaming or merging one of these presentations. The surrounding source record
for each sheet supplies its controlling citation; general background begins
with \cite{Prawitz1965,RobinsonVoronkov2001,BlackburnDeRijkeVenema2001}.

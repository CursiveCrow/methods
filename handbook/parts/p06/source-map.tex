\section*{Reader source map for Part VI}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Reader source map for Part VI}
\label{sec:p06-source-map}

The twenty-three source intervals in this part follow the
set-theory manuscript in its original order.  The axiom table,
ordinal and cardinal reference chapters, Choice calibrations, forcing
apparatus, infinitary combinatorics, large-cardinal and absoluteness survey,
descriptive set theory, determinacy, figures, and generated register are
separately authored additions.
The constructibility definition includes its empty base, and the
relativization lemma and proof use the declared index bounds and that base
case directly.  Source attributions and proof statuses identify the
dependencies of each result.
The cardinal-arithmetic chapter includes possible cofinalities, elementary
ultrafilter-product proofs, the cited PCF maximum theorem, and Shelah's
bound at \(\aleph_\omega\).

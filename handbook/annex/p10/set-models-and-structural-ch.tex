\subsection{Material models and structural continuum statements}
\label{sec:annex-p10-set-models-structural-ch}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p10-set-model-topos}{thm:p10-set-model-topos}{The coded category of sets}
\begin{proof}
The domain of \(M\) is a set.
Satisfaction of each fixed function-definition formula in this
set-sized structure is an ordinary metatheoretic relation.
Thus the indicated objects and typed function codes form actual
sets of objects and arrows.
The ZF theorems about identities and composition give unique
codes in \(M\) and verify the category equations.
No external evaluation by well-founded membership recursion is used.

The empty set and a singleton, as coded in \(M\), are initial
and terminal. Products are the coded sets of ordered pairs,
and a pullback is the coded subset on which the two maps agree.
The usual pairing maps give their universal properties for
every coded cone.
Disjoint tagged unions supply finite coproducts.
The quotient by the equivalence relation generated by the
two sides of a parallel pair supplies a coequalizer.
These constructions and their universal maps are ZF
set constructions within \(M\); their universal assertions
range over exactly the arrows of \(\mathcal C_M\).

The exponential from \(x\) to \(y\) is the set of functions
from \(x\) to \(y\) computed in \(M\).
Evaluation is coded there.
Currying and uncurrying coded functions are inverse constructions,
which proves the exponential universal property in this category.
This assertion does not include functions existing only outside \(M\).

A coded map is monic exactly when \(M\) regards it as injective.
Injectivity gives cancellation; failure of injectivity gives
two distinct coded elements and hence two singleton-domain
arrows witnessing failure of cancellation.
Every such monomorphism is isomorphic to its coded image inclusion.
The two-element set with its true point classifies these
inclusions by their characteristic functions.
Every coded subset has its coded complement, so the resulting
topos is Boolean.
The empty and singleton objects are not isomorphic.

A coded map is epic exactly when \(M\) regards it as surjective.
For a surjection, equality after precomposition gives equality
at every target element and hence equality of the two coded maps.
For a nonsurjection, the characteristic function of its image
and the constant-true function agree after precomposition
but differ at a missing target element.
This proves the converse.
Choice in \(M\) gives a coded section of every surjection,
so every epimorphism of the category splits.

The singleton is a generator.
If two coded functions differ, function extensionality in \(M\)
supplies a coded input where their values differ.
The corresponding singleton-domain arrow distinguishes them.
This is well-pointedness.

Finally, for every coded endomap and coded initial point,
the recursion theorem in \(M\) supplies a unique coded map
from its \(\omega\) commuting with successor and having that
initial value. This is precisely the natural numbers object
universal property among the arrows of \(\mathcal C_M\).
If \(M\) is nonstandard, its \(\omega\) need not be the
external natural-number set. The universal property here
quantifies over the coded objects and arrows, as required.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p10-set-model-ch}{thm:p10-set-model-ch}{The two meanings of continuum agree in this model}
\begin{proof}
By the preceding proof, monomorphisms and epimorphisms are
exactly the injections and surjections recognized by \(M\).
The internal object of epimorphisms is the coded subset of
the coded function set consisting of surjections.
It is initial exactly when \(M\) says it has no members.
Equivalently there is no coded surjection.
This equivalence uses the particular well-pointed set model;
it is not imposed on an arbitrary sheaf topos.

For the nonempty objects involved in a comparison above
\(\omega\), Choice in \(M\) makes injection and surjection
comparisons the usual cardinal comparisons in \(M\).
Hence a monomorphism in one direction together with no
epimorphism in that direction means strict inequality of
the corresponding \(M\)-cardinals.

The exponential \(2^\omega\) is \(M\)'s set of
two-valued functions on its natural numbers.
Characteristic functions give, in \(M\), a bijection with
its power set of \(\omega\).
If \(M\models\mathsf{CH}\), its continuum cardinal is
its least uncountable cardinal, and no intermediate
object-cardinal exists.
Conversely, if \(M\models\neg\mathsf{CH}\), Cantor's
theorem and Choice in \(M\) give
\[
 \aleph_0^M<\aleph_1^M<(2^{\aleph_0})^M.
\]
The coded set \(\aleph_1^M\) is then an intermediate object
of \(\mathcal C_M\).
These implications prove the equivalence.
No external power-set or external cardinal computation
is substituted for the ones interpreted in \(M\).
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p10-structural-ch-consistency}{thm:p10-structural-ch-consistency}{The explicit consistency comparison}
\begin{proof}
Assume consistency of \(\mathsf{ZF}\).
The constructible inner-model derivation and its finite
proof translation give consistency of
\(\mathsf{ZFC}+\mathsf{GCH}+(V=L)\), hence of
\(\mathsf{ZFC}+\mathsf{CH}\).
First-order completeness supplies a set-sized model.
It supplies no transitivity or well-foundedness assumption.
Applying \cref{thm:p10-set-model-topos,thm:p10-set-model-ch}
gives the required structural model satisfying the
categorical continuum assertion.

The same premise gives consistency of \(\mathsf{ZFC}\).
The independently proved Boolean-valued implication
\cref{thm:p06-bfc-consistency} gives consistency of
\(\mathsf{ZFC}+\neg\mathsf{CH}\).
A set-sized model of that theory, followed by the same
coded-category construction, gives the structural model
with the opposite continuum statement.
This argument uses the supplied membership-model proof
for the negative material conclusion, not an unproved
reverse interpretation of arbitrary topoi.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

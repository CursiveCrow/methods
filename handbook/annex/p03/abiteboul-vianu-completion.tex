\begin{annexitem}{annex:p03.av.1}{thm:p03-av-type-order}{An ordered refinement of tuple types}
\begin{proof}
Fix the vocabulary and the variable list \(\bar x\). There are
finitely many atomic formulas whose terms are these variables or the
vocabulary's constants. Fix a list of them. Comparing their truth
vectors lexicographically, with true before false, gives a quantifier-free
formula \(\operatorname{AtLess}(\bar x,\bar y)\). Its incomparable
pairs are exactly the pairs with the same atomic type.

For a temporary \(2k\)-ary relation \(D\), put
\[
 E_D(\bar x,\bar y)=
       \neg D(\bar x,\bar y)\land\neg D(\bar y,\bar x),
 \qquad
 M_i(\bar x,\bar z)=
       \exists u\,E_D(\bar x[i\leftarrow u],\bar z).
\]
Tuple notation expands into a fixed finite list of variables or
terms, with fresh bound variables at each substitution. Define
\[
 \operatorname{Same}_i(\bar x,\bar y)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \forall\bar z\bigl(M_i(\bar x,\bar z)\leftrightarrow
                         M_i(\bar y,\bar z)\bigr).
\]
Let \(\operatorname{Diff}_i(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z)\) say that
exactly one of the two profile memberships holds, and put
\[
\begin{split}
 \operatorname{MinDiff}_i(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z)
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad&
 \operatorname{Diff}_i(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z)\ \land\\
 &\forall\bar w\bigl(D(\bar w,\bar z)\to
             \neg\operatorname{Diff}_i(\bar x,\bar y,\bar w)\bigr).
\end{split}
\]
The formula \(\operatorname{Split}_i(\bar x,\bar y)\) is
\[
 \bigwedge_{j<i}\operatorname{Same}_j(\bar x,\bar y)
 \ \land\
 \exists\bar z\bigl(
   \operatorname{MinDiff}_i(\bar x,\bar y,\bar z)
   \land M_i(\bar x,\bar z)\land\neg M_i(\bar y,\bar z)\bigr).
\]
Empty conjunctions are true. Take the inflationary fixed point, from
\(D^0=\varnothing\), of the first-order formula
\[
 \operatorname{AtLess}(\bar x,\bar y)
 \ \lor\
 \left(E_D(\bar x,\bar y)\land
                 \bigvee_{i=1}^k\operatorname{Split}_i(\bar x,\bar y)\right).
 \tag{O}
\]
This is a single IFP definition of arity \(2k\). It is not
asserted positive in \(D\).

We verify every stage. At stage zero, \(E_D\) is universal.
Nonemptiness of \(A\) makes every \(M_i\) true, so there is
no split. The first stage is precisely the strict order of atomic
types. Suppose a later stage orders its equivalence classes linearly.
For a tuple and coordinate \(i\), \(M_i\) records the set of
old classes reachable by changing that coordinate. Its truth is
constant on each old class in the \(\bar z\) argument. If two
profiles differ, their symmetric difference has a least old class.
The minimum-difference formula identifies exactly that class, without
selecting one of its representatives.

Inside each old equivalence class, formula (O) compares the list of
coordinate profiles lexicographically: first the least coordinate
with different profiles, then the least old class on which those
profiles differ, with membership before nonmembership. This is an
ordinary lexicographic order on finite lists of finite bit vectors.
It is irreflexive and transitive; two lists are incomparable exactly
when they agree. Between distinct old classes the inflationary union
retains the old order, and the split clause adds nothing in the reverse
direction. Thus the next stage again orders its classes linearly,
refining but never reversing the preceding partition. If the order
changes, at least one class splits. Finiteness therefore gives a
stationary stage, and at that stage equal classes have identical
replacement profiles for every coordinate.

It remains to identify the classes; this does not follow just from
termination. First, equality of first-order \(k\)-variable types
has the replacement back-and-forth property on a finite structure.
If \(\bar a\) and \(\bar b\) have the same type but a
replacement \(\bar a[i\leftarrow u]\) has no equivalent
replacement of \(\bar b\), then for each \(v\in A\)
choose a \(k\)-variable formula true at
\(\bar a[i\leftarrow u]\) and false at
\(\bar b[i\leftarrow v]\). Negate a separating formula if
necessary. Their finite conjunction, existentially quantified at
coordinate \(i\), distinguishes \(\bar a\) from
\(\bar b\), a contradiction. The reverse direction is identical.
This is the finite back-and-forth argument underlying
\cref{thm:p03-w03-p03-056-eternal}.

By induction on the refinement stages, two tuples of the same type
stay together. They have the same atomic type, and back-and-forth
makes their sets of old replacement classes equal at every coordinate.
Conversely, at the stationary stage equality of profiles supplies a
matching replacement in the same final class, in either direction.
Atomic truth is already preserved. Induction on a first-order
\(k\)-variable formula now proves agreement: atoms use the initial
partition, Boolean operations preserve agreement, and an existential
witness is transferred by the matching replacement. The final
incomparability relation is therefore exactly equality of types.

Finally \cref{thm:p03-w03-p03-060-gurevich-shelah} translates this
IFP formula into an LFP formula with the same free variables and the
same interpretation on every finite structure. Call it \(D_k\).
Its construction depends only on the finite vocabulary and \(k\),
not on the particular structure. The preceding argument proves all
the asserted properties of its associated \(E_k\).
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p03.av.2}{thm:p03-av-pullback}{The quotient and its logical pullback}
\begin{proof}
The unary atomic labels are invariant under type equivalence.
For the replacement relation we need a stronger fact than the
existence of one pair of suitable representatives. If
\(S_i([\bar a],[\bar b])\), then every representative of
\([\bar a]\) has a coordinate-\(i\) replacement in
\([\bar b]\). Indeed, compare that representative to one
witnessing the edge and apply the back-and-forth property proved in
\cref{annex:p03.av.1}. Consequently
\(\exists u E_k(\bar a[i\leftarrow u],\bar b)\) is invariant
under changing either displayed representative.

Coordinate permutations preserve types by bijective renaming of all
variables of a formula, bound as well as free. Copying coordinate
\(j\) into coordinate \(i\) also preserves types. Apply
back-and-forth to the move setting \(a_i=a_j\). The matching
tuple must satisfy \(x_i=x_j\), so its new entry is necessarily
\(b_j\). For \(i=j\) the operation is the identity.
Likewise a move assigning a constant \(c\) to coordinate
\(i\) has the unique matching value \(c^{\mathbf A}\),
by the atom \(x_i=c\).

These operations generate every map \(\theta\) in the definition.
For each variable index occurring in its image, choose one target
coordinate receiving that variable. A permutation puts the requisite
old values in these chosen coordinates. Copy their values into the
other coordinates requiring them, and then fill the constant-valued
coordinates. No chosen variable-value coordinate is overwritten
before it has been used. If \(\theta\) fixes a specified collection
of coordinates, choose those coordinates as their own representatives
and extend the permutation fixing them; none of the subsequent updates
changes them. Thus \(\bar a E_k\bar b\) implies
\(\bar a_\theta E_k\bar b_\theta\). Each \(P_\theta\)
is the graph of a well-defined total function on classes, defined on
representatives by \(E_k(\bar x_\theta,\bar y)\).

The order is well defined by \cref{thm:p03-av-type-order}, and
there are at most \(|A|^k\) classes. Since \(A\) is nonempty,
there is at least one, and a finite linear order has a least class.
Its representative tuples satisfy the LFP formula
\[
 \operatorname{Min}_k(\bar z)
       \quad\defeq\quad\forall\bar w\,\neg D_k(\bar w,\bar z).
\]
Together these are the promised uniform definitions.

Here is the pullback construction, including its fixed points. For
each quotient variable introduce a fresh \(k\)-tuple of variables.
Temporarily replace every occurrence of the quotient constant
\(c_\ast\) by one further tuple \(\bar z\). Replace equality
by \(E_k\), the other atoms by their definitions above, and
quantification over one quotient element by quantification over its
representative tuple. Rename all inserted bound variables and relation
binders to avoid capture. Induction gives the usual truth equivalence
for Boolean operations and quantifiers, because the map
\(A^k\to A^k/E_k\) is surjective and every interpreted atom
is invariant under representatives.

For a target fixed-point relation of arity \(r\), introduce a
fresh relation of arity \(kr\). If \(S\) is a relation on
quotient elements, let its pullback consist of all tuples of
representatives of members of \(S\). The induction hypothesis
for the defining matrix gives
\[
 F^{\leftarrow}(S^{\leftarrow})
             =F(S)^{\leftarrow}.
\]
The translated matrix is positive in the new relation whenever the
old one is positive: the definitions of the quotient relations and
of \(E_k\) contain no occurrence of that relation. Starting from
the empty relation, induction on the finite stages proves equality
of the pulled-back least-fixed-point stages. They remain saturated
under replacement of any representative. Hence the stationary least
fixed points agree. For a greatest fixed point the same argument starts
from the whole relation and proceeds downward. Nested fixed points
are covered by the structural induction, with all their ordinary
parameters fixed at the corresponding tuples. No choice of one
representative per class is made.

The resulting translation \(\eta^I(\bar z)\) has the desired
truth value whenever \(\bar z\) represents the least class.
For a sentence put
\[
 \eta^{\leftarrow}\quad\defeq\quad
 \exists\bar z\bigl(\operatorname{Min}_k(\bar z)
                              \land\eta^I(\bar z)\bigr).
\]
There is such a tuple and all such tuples give the same truth value.
This is an LFP sentence in the original vocabulary and proves the
asserted equivalence, including the constant proviso.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p03.av.3}{thm:p03-av-quotient-space}{Polynomial space in the type structure}
\begin{proof}
First rename every bound relation variable to its own fresh symbol.
Rename every bound first-order variable, including each coordinate
bound inside a fixed-point operator, to a name unused anywhere else
outside its scope. For
\([\mathbf{pfp}\,R\bar x\,.\,\psi](\bar t)\), the binding
of \(\bar x\) is in \(\psi\), not in the argument terms
\(\bar t\); those terms retain their external meanings. The
renaming conventions of \cref{def:p03-w03-p03-059-lfp} preserve
truth. Choose \(k\geq1\) large enough for all variable names
in the resulting finite syntax, and list them among
\(x_1,\ldots,x_k\). Thus \(k\) is fixed with the formula.
Let \(M\) be the number of elements of its ordered type structure.

\emph{Parameter fibres.} For a class \(a\) and a set
\(P\subseteq\{1,\ldots,k\}\) of coordinates to be held
fixed, let \(V(a,P)\) be the image of \(a\) under the
composition of the relations \(S_i\), once for each coordinate
outside \(P\), in a fixed order. If there are no such coordinates,
the composition is the identity. For any representative \(\bar a\)
of \(a\), this is exactly
\[
 \{[\bar b]: b_i=a_i\text{ for every }i\in P\}.
 \tag{F}
\]
One inclusion follows by lifting each edge of the path from the
current representative, using the uniform replacement property in
\cref{annex:p03.av.2}. The lifted path never changes a held
coordinate. Conversely, any tuple with those coordinates fixed is
obtained by replacing the other coordinates one at a time. Consequently
(F) holds for every representative of \(a\), not just a
specially chosen one. The set \(V(a,P)\) can be computed using
\(M\)-bit vectors and at most \(k\) relation-image operations.

\emph{Relation environments.} In an evaluation of a matrix, each
currently bound relation variable \(R\) has a designated coordinate
list \(I_R\), one coordinate per argument, and a set \(P_R\)
of captured parameter coordinates, disjoint from \(I_R\).
Its value is represented by a bit vector \(B_R\) on
\(V(a_R,P_R)\), where \(a_R\) is the class at which its
binder was entered. The invariant is this: for every assignment
agreeing with the captured parameter values, its class belongs to
\(B_R\) exactly when the tuple in coordinates \(I_R\)
belongs to the actual relation value of \(R\). Bits outside the
fibre are zero. Thus the representation is constant on types and is
independent of unused coordinates within the parameter fibre.

At a new binder, the captured support is the union of its matrix's
free first-order coordinates outside its bound tuple and the supports
of all its free relation variables other than the newly bound one.
The latter addition is essential. An outer relation can carry a
parameter even when that parameter's name is not printed in an inner
matrix. These supports are obtained along the finite lexical nesting
of binders; they do not require solving a new fixed-point equation.
Global freshness makes the new bound tuple disjoint from them.
Likewise a newly encountered first-order quantifier does not bind
any captured support coordinate of a currently active relation.

Evaluation starts with no such environment for a sentence: the
original vocabulary relations are part of the structure, have empty
captured support, and are already represented by its atomic labels. During a recursive call,
the current class has a representative agreeing with all captured
parameter values of the active environment. The following construction
preserves this coherence condition.

\emph{Atoms, Boolean operations, and quantifiers.} A base atom is
read from its unary type label. For \(R(\bar t)\), apply the
coordinate map that places the values of \(\bar t\) into
\(I_R\) and leaves the other coordinates unchanged. Terms here
are variables or constants. This map fixes \(P_R\), so the
resulting class lies in the fibre of \(R\). Its bit in \(B_R\)
is precisely the required truth value. This lookup changes no
assignment used for evaluating other subformulas. Boolean operations
use their ordinary truth functions. For \(\exists x_i\psi\),
search the \(S_i\)-successors of the current class and evaluate
\(\psi\) there; universal quantification uses the corresponding
universal test. The uniform edge property provides actual witnesses
from each current representative, and the bound coordinate is outside
the captured supports. Therefore these calls retain coherent parameters.

\emph{A fixed-point call.} Suppose the matrix of a binder for
\(R\) has bound coordinate list \(I\). Determine its support
\(P\) as above and compute \(V(a,P)\) from the current
class \(a\). Start with the empty bit vector for \(R\).
For each class \(b\in V(a,P)\), evaluate the matrix at \(b\)
using the current relation vector and the active outer environment.
These evaluations produce the next vector. A structural induction
on the matrix proves that this is exactly the next stage of the
actual update operator at the fixed parameter values. To spell out
the inductive invariant, choose an actual assignment representing
\(a\). By (F), every tested \(b\) has a representative
with the same captured parameters; these include the supports of
all outer relations. The smaller-formula induction therefore applies.
Its truth value depends only on the designated tuple values, the
fixed parameters, and the current relation interpretations. This
proves both the correctness of the new vector and its independence
of unused coordinates. Equality of classes makes it independent of
the chosen representative as well.

Every tuple of values for \(I\) extends to an assignment with the
captured parameters, since \(I\cap P=\varnothing\) and the
universe is nonempty. Hence this representation is injective on
actual relation values occurring at the fixed parameters: two such
values agree if and only if their bit vectors agree. In particular,
a stationary vector transition is exactly a stationary relation
transition. The argument also covers a nullary bound relation: its
vector is either empty or the whole nonempty parameter fibre.

There are at most \(2^M\) possible vectors. Compare each new
vector with its predecessor. If they agree, return that partial
fixed point. If none of the first \(2^M\) transitions is
stationary, two of the \(2^M+1\) encountered states repeat.
Determinism then gives a nontrivial cycle, which can never subsequently
reach a stationary state; return the empty vector, as required by
\cref{def:p03-w03-p03-059-pfp}. Finally test the argument tuple
by the coordinate-map lookup already described. An \(M+1\)-bit
counter suffices for this bound. The induction on formula syntax
allows nested fixed points; their computations are completed and
released before the enclosing matrix evaluation continues.

This proof does not merge different parameter fibres into one
partial-fixed-point iteration. Such a merger would be invalid: a
cycle in one fibre could prevent a stationary value in another from
being returned. Each call instead holds its own captured parameters
fixed, including those inherited through outer relation variables.
Thus the semantic induction covers the full parameterized syntax,
not merely the parameter-free clause of an infinitary translation.

\emph{Space and arbitrary ordered inputs.} A table, a fibre, and
an iteration counter each use \(O(M)\) bits. Coordinate sets
and formula positions have sizes bounded with the fixed formula;
class indices use \(O(\log M)\) bits. The maximum number of
simultaneously active tables is bounded by the fixed syntactic nesting
depth. Relation lookup and construction of a fibre can be performed
by scans of the input tables. Hence the total work space is polynomial
in \(M\), even when \(|A|\) is much larger than \(M\).
No enumeration of the original universe occurs in this algorithm.

To obtain a decision problem on all finite ordered structures of the
quotient vocabulary, use the same algorithm with fixed defaults on
malformed auxiliary relations: for example, use the least available
successor when a purported coordinate map has several outputs, and
return false for a lookup with no output. Bit vectors and iteration
bounds are still finite and of the same sizes. For a sentence start
at the least element. On genuine type structures this gives the
sentence's truth value, independent of the initial assignment.
Require the distinguished constant to name that least element;
reject other inputs. This convention is isomorphism invariant.

Use explicit relation-table encodings as in
\cref{def:p04-captures-complexity}, including the order and the
constant's value. The latter can equivalently be stored as a
singleton unary table; conversion between that table and an index
is polynomial time. Encoded length is at least \(M\); checking
the header, table lengths, and order is polynomial in that length.
A header claiming tables larger than the input is rejected before
allocating those tables. Thus the preceding algorithm is a genuine
polynomial-space decider on encoded inputs, not just a promise
procedure on correctly formed invariants. It defines the required
class \(Q_\varphi\). This completes the correctness and space
proofs.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p03.av.4}{thm:p03-w03-p03-059-abiteboul-vianu}{The unordered equivalence}
\begin{proof}
Assume first \(\mathsf P=\mathsf{PSPACE}\), and fix a PFP
sentence \(\varphi\) in any of the stated finite vocabularies.
By \cref{thm:p03-av-quotient-space}, its truth on
\(\mathbf A\) is decided by the polynomial-space property
\(Q_\varphi\) of \(\operatorname{Typ}_k(\mathbf A)\), for a
fixed \(k\). The hypothesis gives a polynomial-time decider
for that property.

Apply the proved ordered capture theorem
\cref{thm:p09-immerman1986-capture}. Its constant proviso is
satisfied by \(c_\ast\). A non-strict input order, if used in
that presentation, is the strict order together with equality.
The source binder \((\bar t:\mu R(\bar x))\psi\), with
first-order positive matrix, is precisely
\([\mathbf{lfp}\,R\bar x\,.\,\psi](\bar t)\) of
\cref{def:p03-w03-p03-059-lfp}. The capture proof includes
small nonempty structures and explicit decoding of the input tables.
Consequently it supplies an LFP sentence \(\eta\) defining
\(Q_\varphi\) on these ordered structures. By
\cref{thm:p03-av-pullback}, its pullback \(\eta^{\leftarrow}\)
is an LFP sentence of the original vocabulary, and
\[
 \mathbf A\models\varphi
 \ \Longleftrightarrow\
 \operatorname{Typ}_k(\mathbf A)\models\eta
 \ \Longleftrightarrow\
 \mathbf A\models\eta^{\leftarrow}.
\]
No order on \(A\) has been chosen or added to that vocabulary.
The auxiliary order is the uniformly definable order of tuple types.

For a formula \(\varphi(u_1,\ldots,u_d)\), introduce fresh
unary predicates \(P_1,\ldots,P_d\) and apply the sentence
case to
\[
 \exists u_1\cdots\exists u_d
       \left(\bigwedge_{i=1}^dP_i(u_i)\land
                                      \varphi(u_1,\ldots,u_d)\right).
\]
In the expansion with \(P_i=\{a_i\}\), this sentence is
true exactly when \(\varphi(\bar a)\) is true. Obtain its
LFP equivalent, rename bound first-order variables away from the
original free variables and bound relation variables away from the
new predicates, and replace each free \(P_i(t)\)-atom by
\(t=u_i\).
The result is an LFP formula in the original vocabulary with free
variables among \(u_1,\ldots,u_d\), equivalent to \(\varphi\)
at every tuple. The substitution preserves positivity of all bound
relation variables and respects the parameter convention. Repeated
values among the \(a_i\) cause no problem. Thus the implication
holds for the query-level equivalence, not just for sentences.

For completeness, the unconditional inclusion LFP in PFP uses
finite monotone iteration. Starting from the empty relation, a
positive least-fixed-point operator reaches its least fixed point,
which is therefore also its partial fixed point. A greatest fixed
point is the complement of the least fixed point of the dual operator
\(S\mapsto A^r\setminus F(A^r\setminus S)\). Applying these
constructions recursively to subformulas preserves each parameter
assignment and gives \cref{thm:p03-w03-p03-059-lfp-in-pfp}.
Conversely, if LFP and
PFP are equivalent on all finite structures, they are equivalent
on vocabularies containing a distinguished order. The proved ordered
equivalence \cref{thm:p03-w03-p03-059-ptime-pspace} then yields
\(\mathsf P=\mathsf{PSPACE}\). Its word-structure argument
covers arbitrary languages, with the empty word handled separately.
This proves both directions without assuming that unordered LFP
captures all polynomial-time properties.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

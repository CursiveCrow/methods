\begin{annexitem}{annex:p2.fol.calc.4}{thm:p02-equality-laws}{Derived equality laws}
\begin{proof}
Reflexivity and formula replacement are the two registered equality rules.
Choose \(z\notin
\operatorname{Var}(r)\cup\operatorname{Var}(t)\cup\operatorname{Var}(u)\).
For symmetry, take \(r=r\) and apply replacement to \(r=t\) with
\(\varphi(z)\defeq z=r\), obtaining \(t=r\).  For transitivity, apply
replacement to \(t=u\) and \(r=t\) with
\(\varphi(z)\defeq r=z\), obtaining \(r=u\).  Total capture-avoiding
substitution makes both displayed instances legitimate.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p2.fol.calc.5}{thm:p02-hilbert-fol-deduction}{Hilbert-FOL deduction theorem}
\begin{proof}
The reverse implication appends \(\varphi\) as a premise and applies modus
ponens; this is \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-025-discharge}(2).  For the forward
implication, induct through a dependency-annotated derivation from
\(\Gamma\cup\{\varphi\}\), proving
\(\Gamma\derives_{\mathsf H_{\mathsf{FOL}}}\varphi\to\chi_i\) with support
contained in the line's support less \(\varphi\).  Premises from \(\Gamma\)
and axioms use A1 and modus ponens; the line \(\varphi\) uses the five-line
empty-support identity derivation of \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-025-identity};
modus ponens uses A2.

Suppose \(\chi_i=\forall x\,\psi\) comes by generalization.  If the
dependency set for \(\psi\) contains \(\varphi\), the rule condition gives
\(x\notin\FV(\varphi)\).  Generalize the induction result
\(\varphi\to\psi\), whose support omits \(\varphi\), then use the \(\forall2\)
schema to obtain \(\varphi\to\forall x\psi\).  If \(\varphi\) is absent from
that dependency set, \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-025-support}(2) rederives the line
from \(\Gamma\) with the same support; generalize \(\psi\) and use A1 to infer
\(\varphi\to\forall x\psi\).

Suppose instead that \(\chi_i=\exists x\,\theta\to\psi\) follows by
existential elimination from \((\theta\to\psi,D)\).  If
\(\varphi\notin D\), \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-025-support}(2) rederives that
line from \(\Gamma\); apply existential elimination there and then A1.  If
\(\varphi\in D\), the rule condition gives \(x\notin\FV(\varphi)\) as well as
\(x\notin\FV(\psi)\).  From the induction result
\(\varphi\to(\theta\to\psi)\), the nineteen-line empty-support permutation
derivation of \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-025-permutation} and modus ponens give
\(\theta\to(\varphi\to\psi)\).  Existential elimination yields
\(\exists x\,\theta\to(\varphi\to\psi)\), and the same lemma with the two
antecedents interchanged gives \(\varphi\to(\exists x\,\theta\to\psi)\).
Every case is written line by line, with its support, in
Annex~\ref{annex:p02-w02-p02-025-discharge}.

  Thus every annotated line is covered, and the
  last one proves the claim without unrestricted generalization.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p02-w02-p02-014-fol-tableau-adequacy}{thm:p02-fol-tableau-adequacy}{First-order tableau adequacy}
\begin{proof}
The tableaux of \cref{def:p02-fol-tableau} are those of the calculus
\(\mathsf T\) of \cref{def:p02-w02-p02-014-fol-setting}, with the bookkeeping
of \cref{def:p02-w02-p02-014-fol-branch}; a fair saturated open branch in
the sense of \cref{def:p02-fol-tableau} is an open branch, finite or
limit, on which every available task is done, that is, an open saturated
branch in the sense of \cref{def:p02-w02-p02-014-fol-branch}.  The first
sentence of the theorem is \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-014-fol-completeness}(1):
soundness of closure is \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-014-fol-soundness}(3), and
the converse is the halting of the fair systematic procedure on
unsatisfiable input, \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-014-fol-semidecision}(3).  For a
fair saturated open branch \(B\), \(\operatorname{fm}(B)\) is an equality-free
Hintikka set by \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-014-saturated-hintikka}, its quotient
Herbrand structure is \(\mathcal M_{B^{+}_{\mathrm{at}}}\) by
\cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-014-congruence}, and
\(\mathcal M_{B^{+}_{\mathrm{at}}}\entails B\) is
\cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-014-herbrand-truth}.  The reduct and expansion
clauses are \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-014-fol-extraction}.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

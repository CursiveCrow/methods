\begin{hbconvention}{def:p08-imll-empty-tensor}{Empty antecedent in the IMLL semantics}
In the exact commutative-residuated-pomonoid interpretation immediately above,
allow \(n=0\) and set
\[
  \bigotimes_{i=1}^{0}\llbracket A_i\rrbracket\defeq 1,
\]
where \(1\) is the monoidal unit.  Thus the semantic reading covers the empty
antecedent admitted by \texttt{sheet:imll}, including
\(\Rightarrow\mathbf 1\).  This is an editorial completion of the missing
empty-product clause; it changes no inherited byte and introduces no second
semantic structure.
\end{hbconvention}

\noindent\textit{Editorial group convention.}
In the preceding exact language declaration and throughout the later
epistemic apparatus, ``group'' means a nonempty finite subset of the agent
index set.  The empty-group identities
\(E_{\varnothing}A=\top\) and
\(C_{\varnothing}A\leftrightarrow A\) are mathematically coherent under the
handbook's empty-conjunction and length-zero-path conventions, but they are
outside the language and calculus fixed here.

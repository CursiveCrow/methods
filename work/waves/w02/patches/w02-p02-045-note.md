## R-011 (CNF half of the normal-form theorem)

Host: handbook/restoration/inherited-01946-02186.tex lines 4-58 (clause, form_CPL, CNF/DNF
definitions), 60-95 (lem:prop-clause-satisfaction), 117-208 (thm:prop-normal-forms).

Earlier state: the CNF half (lines 157-207) was a dualized valuation enumeration; it met the
CNF definition in the degenerate cases but contained no induction on formulas, so the
acceptance clause "The CNF proof is a complete induction" was not met literally.

Patch entry 6 (replace host lines 157-208, i.e. the CNF half through end of proof, plus a
worked example after the proof): structural recursion (justified by
thm:prop-unique-readability, line 109 of inherited-01661-01816.tex; recursion theorem in
inherited-01264-01308.tex) defines a pair of finite clause sets K+(phi), K-(phi) with
K+(p)={{p}}, K-(p)={{neg p}}; K+(bot)={empty clause}, K-(bot)=empty set; neg swaps;
and: K+ union, K- pairwise-union product; or: K+ product, K- union; ->: K+ = K-(psi) x
K+(chi), K- = K+(psi) union K-(chi). Simultaneous structural induction proves (i) K+ is
satisfied exactly by models of phi, (ii) K- exactly by countermodels, (iii) only variables
of phi occur. Output C_phi = top if K+ is empty, else N_1 over the clauses listed by
increasing sum of 2^code(L) (injective by binary expansion). Degenerate cases written:
top, bottom, variable-free (via iii), valid with variables (p_0 or neg p_0 gives one
tautologous clause), unsatisfiable (p_0 and neg p_0). Worked example
(neg(p_1 -> p_2) or p_3) gives ((p_1 or p_3) and (neg p_2 or p_3)). Limitation sentence:
pairwise unions multiply clause counts; the existing Tseitin pointer follows.
The exponential lower bound for theta_n is packet w02-p02-007's
thm:p02-w02-p02-007-cnf-size (not yet input by any driver), so it is not restated or
cross-referenced here.

Checks: standalone pdflatex compile clean (book, amsmath, amssymb, amsthm, cleveref);
find_fused_spaces clean; recursion verified in Python on 3000 random formulas over three
variables (K+ satisfied iff true, K- satisfied iff false on all 8 valuations).
Symbols needing registry rows (lead): \mathcal K^{+}, \mathcal K^{-} (clause sets of a
formula and of its negation) and \otimes (pairwise-union product of clause sets), all
proof-local, scope sec:prop-syntax-semantics-04. Existing \otimes rows are scoped to P8,
P15, P17 and P12 sections, so there is no overlap in this scope.

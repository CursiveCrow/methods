Definition~def:ultrafilter-lemma (Ultrafilter Lemma) asserted without citation or hypothesis that the principle is strictly weaker over ZF than the Axiom of Choice. The corrected sentence states that it is strictly weaker over ZF than the Axiom of Choice relative to the consistency of ZF, cites Halpern and Levy (HalpernLevy1971, the theorem that Con(ZF) implies Con(ZF+BPI+not AC)), and points to the statused theorem form, Theorem thm:p01-w02-p01-009-ultrafilter-lemma. Justification: that theorem (parts/p01/orders-ordinals-cardinality-and-choice-w02-p01-009.tex, proved in annex:p01-w02-p01-009-lemma) gives part 2 (ZF proves AC implies the lemma) and part 4 (if ZF is consistent, ZF does not prove the lemma implies AC, from HalpernLevy1971 via thm:p01-bpi-choice-separation), and its closing sentence carries exactly the added consistency qualification.

## SRC-02665-02809

In the proof sketch of the completeness of the sequent calculus (`thm:prop-lk-completeness`), the paragraph on the natural-deduction-to-sequent translation subroutine said that the rule-by-rule translation trees were omitted. It also said that the argument does not establish cut elimination. The corrected sentence points to the trees. They are now given, together with the exact count of contractions and weakenings, in `def:p02-w02b-p02-009-nd-to-lk`, and `thm:p02-w02b-p02-009-nd-to-lk` proves them correct (annex `annex:p02-w02b-p02-009-nd-to-lk`). The sentence also states that the translation uses Cut, which is true: the translation trees for the elimination rules, the discharging rules and lor E end in Cut steps. It keeps the disclaimer about cut elimination and points to `thm:p02-w02b-p02-009-cut-admissibility`, where cut elimination is proved. All three labels are defined in `handbook/parts/p02/natural-deduction-and-classical-sequent-calculi-w02b-p02-009.tex`, and `handbook/parts/p02.tex` and `handbook/annex/p02.tex` both route that file. The mathematics of the proof is unchanged. The closing sentence of the proof is not changed: it still says "the individual translation trees of the subroutine remain omitted" as the reason for the sketch status. The request leaves the status decision to the lead, who must now either raise the status or reword that sentence.

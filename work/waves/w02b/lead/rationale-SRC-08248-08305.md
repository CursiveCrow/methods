## SRC-08248-08305

The Trakhtenbrot theorem in Section "The classical decision problem" was followed only by a deferral to the literature ("See \cite{Trakhtenbrot1950} for a complete proof."), although the handbook now proves the theorem on the route. The corrected text says that a complete proof, by a reduction from the self-application domain set through finite encodings of accepting computations, is Corollary~\ref{thm:p02-w02-p02-036-trakhtenbrot} in Section~\ref{sec:p02-w02-p02-036-finite-validity}, and credits the result to Trakhtenbrot \cite{Trakhtenbrot1950}. Justification: the routed corollary thm:p02-w02-p02-036-trakhtenbrot (handbook/parts/p02/definitions-interpolation-and-decidable-fragments-w02-p02-036.tex, input by handbook/parts/p02/first-order-logic.tex) states the same result for every finite relational signature containing a binary relation symbol (finite satisfiability computably enumerable and undecidable; finite validity co-computably enumerable and not computably enumerable), and is proved in annex:p02-w02-p02-036-trakhtenbrot (handbook/annex/p02/definitions-interpolation-and-decidable-fragments-w02-p02-036.tex, input by handbook/annex/p02.tex) by reduction from the self-application set K. The theorem statement and its status line are unchanged.

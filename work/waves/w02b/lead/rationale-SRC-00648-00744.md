## SRC-00648-00744

The status line of the well-ordering theorem in the choice-principles section still read "cited theorem", although the theorem is proved in full on the handbook route. The status now reads "complete proof" with a reference to \cref{thm:p01-w02-p01-008-well-ordering}, whose annex proof (annex:p01-w02-p01-008-well-ordering) derives a well-order of an arbitrary set from a choice function on its nonempty subsets; the theorem file is input by handbook/parts/p01.tex and handbook/annex/p01-route.tex.

The status line of the corollary on cardinal assignment (cor:cardinal-assignment) also read "cited theorem". It now reads "complete proof" with a reference to \cref{thm:p01-w02-p01-008-cardinal-assignment}, whose items 1 to 4 prove existence and uniqueness of the cardinal bijective with a set, agreement of the cardinal order with injection comparison, and totality with finite maxima; these are exactly the statement of the corollary and the conventions in the paragraph that follows it.

The paragraph after the corollary sent readers to an external text ("See \cite{Jech2003} for complete proofs") for the well-ordering theorem and cardinal assignment. It now says that complete proofs are given in \cref{sec:p01-w02-p01-008-choice}, the routed section containing both proofs (subsections sec:p01-w02-p01-008-wot and sec:p01-w02-p01-008-cardinals). The later citation of Jech2003 for the finite-coding theorem is kept, so the bibliography key stays in use.

The status line of the finite-coding theorem (thm:infinite-cardinal-finite-coding) read "cited theorem". It now reads "complete proof" with a reference to \cref{thm:p01-w02-p01-011-finite-coding}, which restates the four clauses with the same hypotheses and is proved in annex:p01-w02-p01-011-finite-coding from the routed lemmas on ordinals, finite sets, infinite ordinals, the canonical order on pairs, representation and the square theorem.

In the proof of the syntax-counting corollary, the step "every expression generated by a finitary grammar is a finite string over the grammar's alphabet" was followed by a forward pointer to "the string chapters below". The pointer is replaced by a reference to \cref{thm:p01-w02-p01-011-grammar-strings}, whose item 2 proves that the set generated by a finitary grammar over an alphabet is contained in the set of finite strings over that alphabet.

The closing paragraph cited only external proofs of the finite-coding theorem, and the inherited corollary proof used the bound of the countable set of adjoined symbols by the cardinal without justifying it. A sentence is added stating that complete proofs that use only the definitions of the chapter and the Axiom of Choice are given in \cref{sec:p01-w02-p01-011-finite-coding} and \cref{sec:p01-w02-p01-011-syntax-counting}, and that there the bound on the adjoined symbols is derived from the inclusion of the natural numbers in the infinite cardinal. This matches the annex proof annex:p01-w02-p01-011-syntax-counting, step 1 of item 2, which uses item 1 of thm:p01-w02-p01-011-infinite-ordinals to obtain that inclusion.

## SRC-01592-01631

The error-taxonomy longtable in section sec:writing-foundations-07 had a single repeated head and no caption, no first-page head distinct from continuation heads, and no continuation foot, so a reader reaching a page break mid-table had no signal that the table continued and no title on the continuation page. The table now opens with the caption "Error taxonomy" on the first head, repeats the column heads verbatim under the caption "Error taxonomy (continued)" on every later page, prints "continued on next page" at each page break, and carries an empty last foot so the table's own closing rule ends the final page. This is a presentation-only change: no row, cell, or claim is altered. It follows the longtable continuation apparatus already used for multi-page tables in handbook/parts/p02/first-order-depth.tex and handbook/annex/p02/propositional-rejection-and-refutation.tex.

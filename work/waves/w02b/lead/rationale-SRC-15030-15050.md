## SRC-15030-15050

The theorem-status longtable in section `sec:research-workflow-01` lacked the longtable continuation apparatus required of every multi-page table: it had no caption, a single head serving both first and continuation pages, and no continuation foot, so a page break inside the table would give the reader no indication that the table continues. The corrected head gives the first page the caption "Table for theorem status" (closed by \endfirsthead), repeats the column-header rows verbatim under the caption "Table for theorem status (continued)" on later pages (\endhead), adds a right-aligned "continued on next page" foot across all four columns (\endfoot), and an empty \endlastfoot so the table body's closing \bottomrule stands on the final page. This is a typographic correction only; no row content, mathematics or claim changed, and the pattern is the one already used in handbook/restoration/inherited-14231-14270.tex and the other restored longtables.

## SRC-12415-12536

The argument after the theorem "No effective complete calculus for full validity" was headed "Proof sketch" although a complete proof now exists on the route. The heading now reads "Proof" and a new first sentence states that the complete proof, including the effectiveness of the reduction and the categoricity equivalence displayed below, is \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-040-no-effective-calculus}. Justification: that routed theorem (proved at annex:p02-w02-p02-040-no-effective-calculus) proves all five clauses: the reduction is primitive recursive, the equivalence holds, TA and the full validities are not computably enumerable, and no effective complete calculus exists.

The same argument ended by saying that the reduction and its effectiveness were "omitted steps" left to \cite{BarwiseFeferman1985,Shapiro1991}, and that the computability proof was not given. The corrected sentence says that the reduction, its effectiveness and the computability step through \cref{thm:p04-ce-sigma-one} are proved in \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-040-no-effective-calculus}. Justification: clause 1 of the routed theorem proves effectiveness, and clause 3 proves the computability step through thm:p04-ce-sigma-one and thm:tarski-undefinability. The later paragraph still cites BarwiseFeferman1985 and Shapiro1991 for the effectiveness qualification.

The theorem "Categoricity of the full second-order reals" was labelled "Status: cited theorem". It now reads "Status: proved in \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-040-reals-categorical}". Justification: the routed theorem, proved at annex:p02-w02-p02-040-reals-categorical, constructs the cut map, proves that it is an isomorphism of L_R-structures onto the target, and proves that every complete ordered field is uncountable. The note after that theorem records one assumption: identifying every model with R presupposes that the ambient R is a complete ordered field.

The paragraph after that theorem sent the reader to \cite{Shapiro1991} for the categoricity proof and the list of ordered-field axioms, and to \cite{Jech2003} for the uncountability of R. The corrected paragraph says that the axioms are listed in \cref{def:p02-w02-p02-040-ordered-field} (axioms F1-F10 and O1-O5), and that the cut-map categoricity proof and the uncountability proof are given in \cref{thm:p02-w02-p02-040-reals-categorical}. It keeps "compare \cite{Shapiro1991}". With this change, the following paragraph on the failure of downward Lowenheim-Skolem rests on a theorem proved on the route.

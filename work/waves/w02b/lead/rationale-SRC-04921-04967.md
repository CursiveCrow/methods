## SRC-04921-04967

Hypothesis of thm:fol-compactness (w02-p02-054 entry 6, R-027). The inherited statement assumed the Axiom of Choice for every signature, although the countable case needs no choice principle. The corrected hypothesis assumes AC only when the set of function and relation symbols of L is not countable in the sense of sec:set-prerequisites-05, and states that no choice principle is assumed when that set is countable, finite signatures included. Justification: the countable case of clause 1 is proved without choice by the next edit through cor:fol-completeness-countable (handbook/restoration/inherited-04713-04920.tex), and clause 2 cites thm:fol-completeness, whose hypothesis is scoped the same way by the routed request for SRC-04713-04920.

Proof of clause 1 of thm:fol-compactness (entry 7). The inherited proof obtained the model only by the Henkin term-structure construction, which depends on AC through lem:henkin-lindenbaum. The corrected proof keeps that route when the symbol set of L is not countable and, when it is countable, argues without choice: if no L-structure and assignment satisfied T, then T entails bottom, and cor:fol-completeness-countable (with phi = bottom) gives a derivation of bottom from T, contradicting the consistency already established; the satisfying structure is then a model of T because its members are sentences, by lem:fol-coincidence (handbook/restoration/inherited-03519-03680.tex:41).

Hypothesis of the corollary on finite structures in the empty signature (entry 8). The inherited corollary assumed AC although the empty signature is countable. The corrected statement says that no choice principle is assumed. Justification: the proof, as corrected in the next edit, uses only the countable case of clause 1 of thm:fol-compactness, which is choice-free.

Proof of the same corollary (entry 9). The inherited proof cited compactness without noting which case applied. The corrected proof observes that the empty signature is countable, since the empty set injects into N (sec:set-prerequisites-05), so the choice-free countable case of clause 1 of thm:fol-compactness yields a model of the union.

# Source-template and related-evidence construction decisions

Date: 9 September 2026. These are lead-editor scope decisions for the coverage
map. They preserve exact historical rows and all substantive obligations;
independent source/policy and publication acceptance remain later gates.

## SRC-037: whole-set, part and apparatus templates

The producer applied thirty slots to every descriptor, including nonchapter
descriptors. The complete slot vocabulary and the twelve extra requests were
read from tmp/edition-2026-catalog-src037/identity-obligation-receipt.json.
The 582 remaining rows consist of nineteen nonchapter descriptors multiplied
by thirty slots, plus SRC-037-T1559 through T1570.

Whole-set, volume and part descriptors aggregate the already enumerated full
chapter treatments. Map their actual mathematical content, notation, definitions,
rules, hypotheses, statements, proofs, boundaries, examples, exercises,
qualifications and source attributions to those exact chapter components.
An aggregate descriptor does not add a second independent copy of every chapter.
It also does not permit any chapter to be omitted.

Preserve real prefaces, contributors, second-reader distinctions, copyright,
front matter and indexes as the corresponding source apparatus. The generic
template does not prove that a copyright page contains a theorem or exercise.
Mark such mismatched field expectations explicitly inapplicable to that
descriptor, preserving their original text and linking the apparatus unit.
Do not relabel actual mathematical text as metadata.

Page images, renderer runs, exports, acquisition logs and page verification are
source/production evidence requirements. They are not extra intellectual
treatments. The published handbook is an independently worded complete
reconstruction with preserved mathematical content and variants, not a printed
facsimile of every source cover or page. Preserve semantic notation faithfully;
the old request for literal visual copying of every expository glyph is
superseded by the approved edition's reconstruction policy.

The twelve extra rows have these exact dispositions:

- T1559: full two-volume source acquisition and complete mapped chapter coverage
  remain required; access metadata cannot substitute for an absent body.
- T1560: cover identity/source apparatus, not an additional mathematical unit or
  a requirement to print the source cover in the handbook.
- T1561–1562: exact volume-1/volume-2 contents and chapter accounting remain owed.
- T1563: record the actual access, license and any reproduction permissions
  needed for the use actually made. A free URL is not a fabricated permission
  to republish a complete source's protected prose.
- T1564: preserve all mathematical glyph meaning and source conventions; the
  universal literal-facsimile prescription is superseded as described above.
- T1565: preserve every identified draft/final manifestation. Required version
  comparisons remain actual source work; no equivalence follows from a title.
- T1566: preserve the mismatched MPG artifact and its verified wrong-target
  identity. It is evidence of a failed intended acquisition; it does not add
  an unrelated graphics-paper treatment to the reasoning manuscript.
- T1567–1568: the two incomplete Maryland PostScript bodies cannot provide
  successful complete renders. Record the known truncation and failed-source
  outcome, preserve recoverable content and acquire the exact intended complete
  source as the concrete remaining task. Do not invent a successful past render
  or leave an impossible renderer success as an undefined subject obligation.
- T1569–1570: successful certificate-valid access is an acquisition condition
  for the exact intended Fermüller source. Preserve failed requests as evidence;
  an author page and the linked technical body have different roles.

Apply the four shared raw-record dispositions by actual record role. A policy
or acquisition record can map to an apparatus/evidence obligation without being
an extra theorem. Record applicability and the exact component relation rather
than forcing every slot to claim the same kind of completion.

## SRC-038: remaining H103 rows

For the exact twenty-five rows identified by the source mapper, attach real
whole-body, title, copyright, preface, contents, references, index, figure/table,
caption, note, appendix and cover obligations to their bounded content or
apparatus units. Publisher summaries and index-access routes are distinct from
the mathematical body. Keep edition identity and completeness checks explicit.

Generic erratum or supplement slots are acquisition/correction contingencies
for the identified source. They do not establish that an additional work
exists. Enumerate actual discovered corrections or supplements and map them
where applicable; preserve a bounded negative search outcome where none is
found. Newly discovered corrections reopen the affected existing treatment.
Do not invent an appendix, erratum or supplement to fill a template.

The absent historical Crossref request invocation is an irreducible evidence
gap when the saved corpus cannot supply it. Record that historical outcome
honestly, alongside the retained response and source identity. A new request
can provide new evidence but cannot reconstruct a missing old event. This is
not an unresolved mathematical scope candidate. Preserve the MPI copy's
actual access/license evidence separately from source-body and writing status.

The mapper still owes complete classification of the twelve mixed HTML
captures and the actual unfilled HAR chapter inventories. This policy does
not close those evidence and content-scope tasks by declaration.

## SRC-044: related monograph evidence

The intentionally retained 101-page The Productions of Time is a related
temporal-semantics source, not the publisher-final Temporality chapter.
Keep the exact artifact, its technical text and that relationship. It is
background evidence for the selected chapter; retaining it during acquisition
does not automatically impose a complete additional monograph reconstruction.
Every specifically used claim must receive an exact bounded dependency and
source extent. Any explicit preexisting requirement naming further content
still controls and cannot be removed by this decision.

The Partee article and the two discourse-representation editions remain
separately identified source presentations. Do not merge them, the monograph
or the final chapter based on their related subjects.

The source mapper records these decisions against the exact raw IDs and units.

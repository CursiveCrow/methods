# Coverage completion for a CRC-style handbook of logic and reasoning

Authority: the user's 9 September 2026 clarification that The Methods of
Reason is a handbook of logic and reasoning, similar to the CRC Handbook of
Chemistry and Physics, followed by the instruction to continue the scoping
and coverage map.

The reference organizes logical systems, forms of inference, reasoning
methods, definitions, assumptions, rules, algorithms, results, required
proofs, worked examples, counterexamples, comparisons and precise sources.
It supports consultation by subject and by reasoning task. Its broad coverage
includes the deductive, inductive, statistical, Bayesian, abductive, causal,
defeasible, analogical, practical and strategic methods already approved.

The earlier wording of this note expanded "handbook of thinking" into general
design, attention, memory, creativity and cultural or ethical thinking. That
was an agent interpretation, not an explicit scope decision by the user, and
is superseded here. New material from those areas requires an identified
contribution to logic or reasoning. Already approved empirical and
philosophical reasoning obligations remain preserved.

## This construction phase

Complete the existing coverage map and record the additional finite obligations
identified by the breadth analysis. Preserve all existing substantive
requirements and current drafts. Broad manuscript authoring remains paused
until the map is complete. Source reading, source acquisition, comparison with
actual LaTeX, and construction-level reconciliation are part of the map task.
Consolidated manuscript reviews follow the full writing and correction batch.

Every candidate is classified against both the current manuscript and its
existing planned units:

- Absent from the catalog: a required method or perspective needs a new unit.
- Catalogued but missing from the text: complete the existing unit and retain
  its identity.
- Insufficient scope: add the exact missing components to the identified unit,
  or create a separately attributed treatment if the presentation differs.
- Present: retain the actual coverage; a new heading is not needed merely
  because another terminology is possible.

A new obligation must identify a reader's question, the missing capability,
its specific components, a worked case and a failure case, prerequisites,
source identity and selected extent, destination and success/failure
conditions. Evidence for absence states which catalog and manuscript paths
were checked. A source title or a generic list of desirable subjects does not
close a gap. Exact identified sources may still require body acquisition;
that source work remains visible alongside the missing writing.

## Gap-analysis assignments

The practical assignment checks explicit inference, representation, search
and planning methods. The scientific assignment checks quantitative,
model-based and evidential reasoning methods. Additional comparisons may
identify missing formal or informal systems, argument evaluation, testimony,
collective inference and historically distinct reasoning traditions. Every
addition must specify what conclusion its method warrants and under which
conditions, or which empirical or philosophical claim about reasoning it
establishes. General productivity, design or cognitive-development coverage
does not enter merely because it involves thought.

Each assignment compares its findings with the already mapped Oxford Handbook
of Thinking and Reasoning and Handbook of Rationality chapters, the 26 new
reasoning assignments, the 12 mandatory families, the original requirements
and actual current LaTeX. Those benchmarks are inputs to the comparison, not
an assertion that their contents exhaust thinking.

The root integrates overlaps and publishes one gap register. Priority states
whether an addition supplies a core thinking capability, an important
extension, or a specialized reference treatment. It does not erase retained
requirements or provide a manuscript completion percentage.

## Construction and publication remain distinct

The existing source cutoff of 7 September 2026 remains in force. No paid
acquisitions are authorized. Applied cases demonstrate inference principles;
they do not create obligations to write complete textbooks in every applied
discipline. Comparative historical coverage preserves distinct source
traditions and avoids presenting a modern translation or formal reconstruction
as an unchanged original system.

Construction completion means that the finite obligations and their relations
are accounted for. Source acquisition, writing, mathematical or empirical
validity, independent admission, reader usability and final publication have
their own remaining work and evidence. Mapping cannot count them as completed.

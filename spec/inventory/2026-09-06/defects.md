## Scope and interpretation

All 88 original IDs are retained: 42 rigor rows, 26 standards rows and 20 production rows. Historical `v1_lines` are coordinates in an older source state, not current LaTeX line numbers. The root's current mathematical-environment inventory was used only to locate candidate active files; matching current passages were then read where identified. No completion conclusion comes from an environment heading or a historical commit alone.

Current states have these meanings:

- `remediation_evidence_present`: actual current text supplies evidence of the proposed repair, but broader acceptance remains unverified unless expressly stated.
- `confirmed_remaining`: the specifically mapped current passage still lacks the requested local presentation.
- `historical_claim_unverified`: the original claim/fix is recorded, but its present truth or complete acceptance has not been established.
- `policy_conflict`: the proposed old fix or disposition conflicts with a later governing correction/source-preservation instruction.
- `mapping_unverified`: the old coordinate has not been mapped reliably enough to a current acceptance determination.

## Superseded or conflicting proposed fixes

### Direct mathematical correction policy

The current `handbook/CONVENTIONS.md:3–12` requires mathematical errors to be corrected in active reader definitions, statements and proofs, including restoration files, while frozen originals and research provenance remain archival. Its later content-policy amendment at `:14–19` does not authorize retirement or normalization of source logical content. The active front matter at `handbook/frontmatter.tex:45–51` likewise says that corrections appear where the reader uses them. Neither old source immutability nor appended reader instructions override that policy.

### Notation: S-002, S-003, S-020 and S-021

The original S-002 proposal bans domain bars globally; S-003 assigns one global role to equivalence glyphs. These bans conflict with `handbook/frontmatter.tex:97–105`: every attributed source has its own notation scope and keeps its published syntax, while newly added editorial glyphs must have one role inside their declared scope. Meaning and scope must be checked, but source-specific domain and equivalence notation must not be mechanically rewritten under these old rulings. The same issue affects the global glyph/macro demands in S-020/S-021 and L-016. The editorial `forces/nforces` pair already exists at `handbook/handbook.sty:200–201`.

### Status/rule normalization: S-001, S-007, S-008, S-014, S-023, L-003, L-006 and L-014

The current front matter applies status keys to editorial results without replacing inherited proof presentation. The style explicitly supports source-scoped `proofplan` at `handbook/handbook.sty:489–503`. Thus the old demand that every source result use exactly one new status syntax, that every source rule display originate from the registry, or that every inherited definition adopt one new template is not present authority. Current editorial defects still require checking; preserving source syntax does not excuse mathematical errors. The record distinguishes these two questions.

### Retired exercises: S-024 and S-025

The old OBSOLETE dispositions depend on retiring exercises and qualifying problems. The active P12 route now imports `scope-SRC-15620-15637` and `scope-SRC-15638-16742` at `handbook/parts/p12.tex:41–42`. The actual text contains the additional problems and the Exercise Feedback chapter with solutions/source labels. Therefore retirement cannot currently dispose of these rows. The appropriate remaining task is to reconcile original source-required problem/solution coverage, not infer either completion or a new solution obligation solely from the old open/obsolete fields. S-026's feedback-notation issue likewise requires current source-scope mapping.

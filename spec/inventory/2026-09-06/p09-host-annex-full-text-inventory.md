## Confirmed CSL finding, counted once

**NEW-P9-CSL-INTERVAL-CLOSURE** is the same finding reported in the preceding small-file inventory, not an additional duplicate.

At [1365–1369](C:/Dev/the-methods-of-reason/handbook/parts/p09/logic-in-computer-science.tex:1365), the host says that replacing a CSL interval by its closure preserves path probability. Use a singleton absorbing state s, rate R(s,s)=0 and proposition p true. Then:

- `(neg top) U^(0,1] p` has probability 0: every positive witness requires neg top at time zero.
- `(neg top) U^[0,1] p` has probability 1: witness zero is permitted and its earlier-time condition is empty.

The operative until clause is at [1377–1380](C:/Dev/the-methods-of-reason/handbook/parts/p09/logic-in-computer-science.tex:1377), and the absorbing/final-path conventions are at 1331–1345.

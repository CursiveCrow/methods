## 1. Intended result and authority

Complete one integrated reference of reasoning and logic for graduate readers,
researchers and practitioners. A reader must be able to find an appropriate
method from a reasoning problem, look up its precise assumptions and limits,
work through an example, and follow it to a complete attributed treatment.
The CRC analogy governs organization, precision, comparisons and lookup.

This document makes the existing [edition execution plan](EDITION-2026-EXECUTION-PLAN.md)
dispatchable. It implements the [edition contract](EDITION-2026-CONTRACT.md);
it does not replace that contract, change requirement IDs, admit manuscript
content or change the coverage cutoff of 7 September 2026.

The deliverable is `handbook/main.pdf` together with its maintained LaTeX,
bibliography, indexes and reproducible production instructions. All 615
original requirements and all inherited substantive material remain accounted
for. Correct errors in the active manuscript and preserve archival evidence.
There is no page ceiling or permission to shorten an owed proof to meet a date.

“Complete” means complete against a finite, reviewed first-edition catalog
covering the domains below. Catalog omissions discovered before release become
edition work. The catalog cannot be closed merely by naming topics or excluding
inconvenient source presentations. Later publications belong to later editions.

## 3. Coverage catalog and unit of work

A catalog unit is an identified intellectual treatment: a definition and its
use, calculus and metatheorems, theorem/proof group, algorithm and correctness
argument, reasoning method, comparative argument, or empirical topic. Every
unit must record:

- Stable unit ID; original requirement IDs and inherited/source units served.
- Subject, source identity/version and exact required extent.
- Required definitions, claims, proof or evidence components, examples and limits.
- Prerequisites, active body/annex destinations and lookup destinations.
- Named owner, status, specific acceptance cases, evidence and blocker if any.

Each required component receives its own disposition. A unit can serve several
requirements, and a requirement can require several units. Every relation must
be explicit. Counts of labels, pages, source captures or review notes cannot
stand in for these mappings.

Reuse the existing ledger, catalog, packet and provenance mechanisms. The lead
will choose and pin the edition catalog path and schema during CAT-01 after
checking actual consumers. This plan does not invent a competing unbound store.

### CAT-01 — Reconcile and freeze the edition catalog

Owner: lead editor, with bounded source/catalog subassignments. Input: current
requirement ledger, inherited inventories, historical catalog and identified
handbook contents. Dependency: edition policy review.

Output: one reviewed edition catalog and a total reconciliation of historical
records to substantive units, repeated captures/aliases, non-content metadata,
or unresolved candidates. Deterministic batches may classify known metadata

Materially different versions or presentations remain distinguishable.

Pass: all 615 requirements and inherited substantive units have complete
component mappings; every historical record has a traceable disposition;
domain coverage and prerequisite review find no unresolved scope candidate;
validators use the same catalog identity and count. Fail: silent exclusions,
unreviewed version merges, metadata counted as writing, dangling obligations,
or validators bound to different catalogs.

existing obligations are already unambiguous may proceed while catalog work
continues; their IDs must subsequently be reconciled.

served. Include a lead when it supplies a required core method, a materially
different guarantee/assumption regime, a necessary prerequisite, or a
source-specific treatment already owed. Record an alias only after checking
the actual content/version. A new peripheral application may be nonselected
with a reviewed reason explaining why the inference principles remain covered.
An old publication's discovery alone does not automatically require a new
whole-book transcription. Existing substantive obligations cannot be excluded
by this rule. The lead and three independent review roles approve the finite
family/component list and record the freeze identity.

CAT-01 also produces a crosswalk for every topic in each selected benchmark

Give each benchmark an explicit role: organization/lookup, subject coverage,

## 4. Subagent distribution and operating rules

Use one lead and at most three active subagents. Roles rotate by wave;
the table does not authorize more than three simultaneous subagents.

| Seat | Authoring wave | Review wave |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lead | Own catalog, dependencies, shared integration, assignment queue and acceptance decisions. | Freeze scope, resolve findings and record acceptance only after required evidence. |
| A | One proof/correction or foundational chapter package. | Fresh source-fidelity reviewer who did not author the assigned unit. |
| B | One independent existing or new subject package in disjoint files. | Fresh mathematical/topic reviewer who did not author the assigned unit. |
| C | Source acquisition, third writing package, or apparatus with a concrete downstream need. | Fresh adversarial publication reviewer who did not author the assigned unit. |

Keep at least two authoring seats on prose, mathematics or worked treatments
when dependencies permit. Review waves temporarily use all three seats for
independent review of a frozen batch. Reviewers file blind before comparing
findings. An unresolved finding cannot pass by majority vote.

The lead owns `handbook/main.tex`, shared part and annex drivers,
`handbook/handbook.sty`, canonical bibliography and symbols, generated navigation,
catalog bindings and terminal records. A specialist may receive exclusive
temporary ownership of a named shared module. Two writers never own the same
file simultaneously. A dispatch must name exact files, even when this plan
names a larger directory as the eventual home.

Work blocks last at most 45 minutes. A checkpoint by 15 minutes must name an
actual saved result, finding, verified acquisition or exact obstacle. At block
end the agent returns a completed unit or precise continuation and stops.
The lead checks active agents at each checkpoint, interrupts an overrun, and
redispatches only a bounded task. No agent retains an unowned days-long task.

After two blocks without substantive progress, split or redirect the task to
its actual missing dependency. After two failed repair cycles on one finding,
assign the source question, lemma or policy conflict that prevents resolution.
Do not repeat an unchanged prompt. Time limits never lower acceptance standards.

### Mandatory dispatch contract

Every dispatch contains the unit/requirement IDs, one concrete output, exact
owned files, actual sources and passage ranges, prerequisites, required
statements/cases, pass/fail conditions, and a return deadline/checkpoint. State
that other agents share the workspace and their edits must be preserved.

The return contains changed files and hashes; definitions/proofs/examples
supplied; actual source extent read; conditions passed, failed or unresolved;
remaining work; and the precise next handoff. Reviews also name read intervals,
the frozen dependency identities and every finding's source location. Report
observable reviewer configuration honestly; never invent hidden model versions
to satisfy an admission field.

## 9. Entry, proof and comparison standard

Each applicable reference entry supplies purpose; inputs and conclusions;
definitions, types and assumptions; rules/procedure; results and justification;
a worked example; a limitation/failure case; relationships and variants;
and exact sources/full-treatment destinations. Mark an inapplicable field
with a reason rather than inventing a formal structure for every subject.

Every promised proof must be complete at its assigned scope, with definitions
before use and each direction/case supplied. A theorem whose full proof is
owed cannot close as “cited” or “sketched.” Where the actual requirement is
an attributed boundary statement, preserve that status accurately. Philosophical
arguments and empirical findings use their appropriate evidence standards.

A normative treatment additionally states the evaluative aim, normative
premises, scope, inference to its recommendation and significant competing
positions/objections. It passes when the recommendation follows conditional
on those declared standards. It fails when a preference axiom becomes an
empirical law, an observed regularity becomes an unsupported prescription,
or a contested normative conclusion is stated as an unconditional theorem.

Comparisons state the question being compared, definitions, assumptions,
known relationships, counterexamples and source variants. They must not
suggest that algorithms, probability updates and informal argument schemes
offer interchangeable guarantees. Source-based treatments use independent
exposition while retaining the required mathematical content and attribution.

Assign the following seven comparison units in the existing edition catalog.
Each has named source treatments, a shared worked question where appropriate,
paired definitions and assumptions, and a separating case. Reusing an example
never permits changing its data or premises silently between methods.

## 11. Review, admission and release

Every unit passes these six separately recorded gates at the scope required by
the existing admission mechanism:

1. Source fidelity: correct identity, version, required extent, attribution and qualifications.
2. Topic validity: exact claims follow from valid proofs or the stated empirical/argumentative evidence.
3. Adversarial publication review: completeness, dependencies, comparisons, examples and reader usability withstand challenge.
4. Static checks: applicable structure, labels, citations, notation, routes and provenance checks pass.
5. Build: the scoped content and dependencies compile with accurate references and no unresolved relevant diagnostics.
6. Visual production: inspected output has legible mathematics, glyphs, tables and figures without lost or clipped content.

Reviewers read the actual manuscript LaTeX for reasoning/content review and the
identified primary sources for authority. Rendered manuscript pages serve the
later production gate. Use independent mathematical calculations or explicit
countermodels when they test a claim. Focused software checks are appropriate
for actual changes to style, validators or generators; prose does not require
a mirrored software test suite.

Freeze maintained content/dependencies, obtain the six gates, then admit the
unit. Generate navigation from admitted projections afterward. Internal scoped
builds may use labelled construction views to inspect dependencies; those views
do not confer admission. Generated lookup files remain downstream of content
identity, avoiding a requirement that final publication precede unit admission.

For pre-admission navigation and notation findings, the lead may generate
clearly labelled candidate symbol/navigation projections from frozen candidate
inputs into isolated construction outputs. Review their actual routes and
content for scoped static, usability, build and visual checks. They carry no
admission authority and cannot replace canonical published views. Following
admission, regenerate canonical projections through the validated admission
path and verify that the reviewed content, anchors and destinations survive.
Final integrated reference/production checks then cover those canonical views.
This explicit sequence prevents navigation-dependent reviews from requiring
an already admitted unit in order to admit that same unit.

Changing content or a relevant assumption reopens the affected reviews.
Pagination-only changes require relevant production rechecks. Preserve valid
evidence for unchanged scopes. Publication review findings remain open until
the actual integrated repair, including dependent navigation, has been checked.

### Final success and failure conditions

Release succeeds only when all of the following hold at one frozen revision:

- The edition catalog is reconciled and has no unresolved scope candidate.
- Every catalog unit is admitted; all 615 original requirements have valid terminal dispositions.
- All inherited substantive material is retained or corrected with verified provenance.
- There are zero required source, proof, coverage, correction or review gaps.
- All nineteen parts and required apparatus are integrated; reader tasks pass.
- PUB-01 copyediting, production instructions and distribution manifest pass.
- Strict whole-book checks pass and a clean integrated build succeeds.
- Every page of the exact final PDF has recorded visual inspection; repairs have been reinspected.
- The readiness record pins the exact artifact and has zero non-user residuals.
- The user gives final editorial acceptance against that exact artifact.

Any failed condition keeps the book unreleased. Do not assign acceptance to
the user, infer it from silence, or substitute a clean compile for content review.

conditions repaired, remaining unit counts by part/status, required sources
still unavailable, active owners and next dispatches. State the catalog version
and denominator for any percentage. Before catalog freeze, do not claim an
overall percentage. After freeze, report counts of accepted units alongside
source blockers and larger unfinished proof/chapter packages; equal unit counts

Estimate effort only after the first representative completed packages in each

explicit; do not promise an unconditional publication date while required

handoff saved content, exact remaining tasks and ownership so work can resume
without redoing the inventory or leaving unattended agents running.

## 13. Execution waves and milestone exits

The main sequence is catalog and current repairs, foundational units,
dependent development, integrated reference tools, and final publication.
Catalog reconciliation and source acquisition continue alongside writing.
The unresolved full catalog does not block an already unambiguous,
adequately sourced unit from being written and reviewed.

### Maintain both existing-content and new-reasoning progress

After the NOW-01 intake and NOW-02 review cycle, use the following repeating
authoring allocation. These are rotating responsibilities, not permanent
agents or permission for more than three concurrent subagents.

| A: existing manuscript specialist | NOW-03's next actual PA proof-builder dependency after the checker review, or NOW-04's Kamp III.2 Lemma 2. | Rotate through the remaining LOG, MOD, COMP, SET, PROOF, NONCLASS, CS and ALG units by prerequisite and confirmed defect priority. |

All three subagent seats switch to fresh independent review contexts for a
frozen batch, then return to authoring. A review batch must fit its reading

### What each assignment returns

Each author returns the actual saved LaTeX and an itemized component result:
definitions, claims, proof cases or evidence, worked example, limitation,
references and outstanding components. A review returns PASS, FAIL or
UNRESOLVED per assigned condition with the exact text/source location.
UNRESOLVED is not a pass. The lead reconciles these results with the existing
records before dispatching the next unit.

At every completed wave, report actual new/accepted content, repaired
findings, blocked units, active owners and the next bounded assignments.
Once the catalog is fixed and representative units have completed authoring,
review and repair, estimate remaining work by stream using those observed
costs. Until then, give milestone standing and concrete counts at their stated
scope rather than inventing a publication date or overall percentage.

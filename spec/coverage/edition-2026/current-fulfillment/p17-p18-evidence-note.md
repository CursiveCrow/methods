## Scope and disposition definitions

`authored_unreviewed` means the whole named bounded definition, method,
argument or comparison is evidenced in actually read current reader text,
with the actual supplied formal annex where relevant. It records writing
extent, not independent correctness or complete closure of every linked
source facet. A source's stated survey/cited boundary is not fabricated into
a missing experiment or proof.

`partial` requires substantive current treatment plus a specifically named
remaining allocated source, argument, formal-system instantiation or comparison.
`missing` requires bounded manuscript evidence that the named allocated
contribution has no established reader treatment. `unassessed` records
located but unread or not-wholly-assessed candidates, including a broader
scope not established by a read finite foundation. Headings, filenames,
theorem status strings and old canonical content flags do not confer credit.

No completion percentage, completed-chapter conclusion or reduction of scope
is calculated. The 615 original requirements and all selected source/native
version obligations remain unchanged.

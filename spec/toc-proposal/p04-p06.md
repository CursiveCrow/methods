### Scope and placement note for integration

This proposal uses the completed construction map and its active unit shards,
the exact original N-a and N-x goals, and current LaTeX chapter/section
declarations. P4 has 15 chapters, P5 has 11 and P6 has 15. The sections include
the advanced source-specific obligations from SRC-001 through SRC-006,
SRC-030, SRC-044 through SRC-046 and SRC-059, together with the mapped
historical treatments. Individual source presentations, original papers,
revised books and substantive companion proofs remain within the relevant
sections; their full mapped scope is not reduced to the TOC wording.

Several source shards retain proposed destinations from an older part-number
scheme: SRC-001 model theory points to p04, set theory to p05 and recursion
theory to p06; SRC-056 HoTT also points to p04. Those paths do not control this
reader-facing subject organization. Model theory belongs with current P3;
HoTT, proof calculi, functional interpretation and realizability have their
primary formal treatments in P7; proof-assistant implementation belongs in
P12. This proposal leaves all current files and map identities unchanged.

The finite/infinite automata interface and descriptive complexity have full
source-specific treatments shared with P9. P4 provides the computational
objects, language methods and complexity organization; root should give P9
the primary logic/automata translations and model-checking procedures without
duplicating their proofs. Formal learning and learnability have neighboring
statistical and empirical coverage; the P4 sections cover only the already
mapped formal identification and language-learning methods. First-order
arithmetical proof theory and provably recursive hierarchies connect P5 with
P7; their source-specific proof constructions remain intact under whichever
single primary destination root selects.

# Coverage and placement notes for the P1–P3 contents proposal

The proposed segment is `p01-p03.md`. It has 8 chapters in P1, 15 in P2 and
18 in P3. The enlarged P3 follows actual scope: the complete Hodges chapter
allocations, the selected *Model-Theoretic Logics* chapters and the finite-model
source treatments require substantially more than the original short
classification-theory overview. No graduate prerequisites, learning outcomes,
review processes, tests or production milestones become reader chapters.

The proposal uses the edition contract and the clarified CRC-style purpose in
`spec/decisions/handbook-of-thinking-scope-2026-09-09.md`. Its construction
baseline is the completed root map dated 9 September 2026 at 16:21:25 UTC,
including the canonical requirement crosswalk and completion queue. I read

P1–P3 chapter/section declarations, and extracted the actual source-unit

## Coverage groupings

- P1 preserves the mathematical substrate, cardinal and choice conditions,
  finite coding, structural induction/recursion, logical levels, consequence,
  formalization, proof methods, counterexamples and notation/binding content.
  The existing definition/proof inspection language is expressed as usable
  definition and argument methods, rather than a manuscript-audit chapter.
- P2 houses all propositional and first-order syntax, calculi, normal forms,
  completeness, compactness, resolution and fragment comparisons. It includes
  `ED26-P02-syllogistic-0001`: both modern S and S-dagger calculi, their
  soundness/completeness proofs, empty-term countermodels and distinction from
  Aristotle's historical system. Full and Henkin second-/higher-order
  semantics retain their different metatheorems and matched rule systems.
- P3 preserves N-a-001–014, N-a-059–062 and N-a-065, the model-theory depth
  rows and existing finite/computable-model material. The twelve Hodges
  chapters and algebraic examples are grouped by their methods, retaining
  interpretations, imaginaries, resplendence, Horn products, Feferman–Vaught,
  Boolean powers, cohomology of expansions and relative categoricity.
- The selected SRC-049 chapters are explicitly housed in the chapters on
  cardinal transfer, infinitary languages, generalized/game quantification,
  abstract logics and topological/Borel/measure/category model theory. The
  SRC-050 finite expressibility and random-structure sections have their own
  two reference chapters. Generalized-quantifier linguistic semantics from
  SRC-044-0019 is included with the formal quantifier methods.

Named sections preserve materially different constructions: the three
countable-categoricity presentations, Keisler versus Shelah, original
Herbrand/Gentzen routes, the o-minimal source presentations and distinct
full/general semantics. Other source variants remain independently attributed
within their subject sections. A repeated source does not require a repeated
chapter or receive duplicate completion credit. Required proofs belong with
their statements and linked annex treatments; the contents do not impose a
new proof-compression boundary.

## Cross-part dependencies retained once

P1 states the set-theoretic substrate and its assumptions; P6 houses the full
set-theoretic developments and independence constructions. P2 supplies the
classical calculi and model-construction tools used by P3. P3 gives the full
model-theoretic preservation and Craig–Robinson–Beth arguments, while P2
retains their usable first-order entries. Algebraic free-object and Stone
duality proofs connect to P10. Cut elimination, type theory, labelled
deduction and constructive proof interpretations connect to P7. Detailed
decidable-fragment, descriptive-complexity and algorithmic developments link
to P4/P9/P12. P3's measure/category logics are not identified with P15
statistical inference. No historical or philosophical source obligation is
removed by these organizational links.

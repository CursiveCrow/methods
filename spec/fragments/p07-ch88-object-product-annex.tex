\begin{annexitem}{annex:p7.ch88.object-landing}{thm:p07-ch88-object-landing}{Actual small-type typing and finite template shape}
\begin{proof}
The source substitution theorem applied to the actual beta premises
gives \(\Gamma\vdash C:[N/x]P\). Since \(C\) is an object product,
source generation strips its final conversions and exposes its object-
product formation premise. Reapplying that common rule gives an actual
\(\Gamma\vdash C:\ast\). Forward transport and exact CC uniqueness
compare these two actual source typings, yielding
\(T([N/x]P)=_\beta\ast\). Literal-star rigidity makes
\([N/x]P\) literally star. Object substitution preserves class and
does not remove a logical product head, so \(P\) itself is literally
star. The original body premise is consequently \(M:\ast\), and
common abstraction/application give the redex type star. The source
small-type certificate came from source generation, not from target
typing alone. The same argument applies to a product right landing.

Raw substitution retains the root of a product, lambda or application.
It changes a variable root only at the selected variable. Thus a literal
product or lambda result implies that its template has that same head
or is the selected variable, whose image is the argument. Apply this
to both landing substitutions. A mixed case would make a product or
lambda template beta-equal to the selected variable by raw equality
transport of \(M\cong M'\). Confluence would reduce that headed
expression to the normal variable, but compatible beta never removes
a product or lambda head. Contradiction. This proves the two finite
cases without normalizing an arbitrary raw input.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p7.ch88.object-asymmetric}{thm:p07-ch88-object-asymmetric}{Only the first substitution needs typing}
\begin{proof}
Induct on the supplied typing/validity proof, with all suffixes. The
first substituted environment is valid by source single substitution.
At the selected projection use \(N\cong N'\) thinned to it. Every
other projection has the same raw variable image; single substitution
provides its left typing, and full reflexivity gives its equality.
Validity star uses context reflexivity. Extension conclusions are star
in that same valid first environment.

For a product, apply induction to its actual smaller annotation
certificate and its body premise in the extended suffix. The result
is exactly product congruence with the body equality in the first
annotation's context. For a lambda use these domain/body inductions
and the LEFT body typing provided by single substitution. For
application use the two child inductions and the LEFT application
typing from single substitution. These are precisely the full guards.
Final typing conversion leaves the subject unchanged, so use its
typing premise induction. All inserted right expressions are scoped
objects, so the raw substitution clauses remain legal. No right
argument typing or right substituted environment was used.

If the template itself is an object product or lambda, strip its
final typing conversions before this construction. Its common
constructor rule yields one outer product or lambda congruence node;
all recursively constructed equalities are its annotation/body
premises. Both resulting raw heads remain that constructor. This
proves the final head assertion as a construction property, not a
presumed endpoint-typing result.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p7.ch88.object-commute}{thm:p07-ch88-object-commute}{The exact replacement of a single guarded diamond}
\begin{proof}
Both beta body premises provide actual context certificates for
\(A,A'\). The primitive middle lambda congruence provides
\(\Gamma\vdash A\cong A'\) and
\(\Gamma[x:A]\vdash M\cong M'\). Symmetry and source conversion
cast the actual right argument \(N':A'\) to \(N':A\).
Source context conversion transports the right beta body typing
\(\Gamma[x:A']\vdash M':P'\) to the first context
\(\Gamma[x:A]\vdash M':P'\). Neither fact is obtained merely
from the middle application equality's untyped right endpoint.

Single source substitution by the actual typed \(N:A\) in the body
equality gives \(C_0\cong[N/x]M'\). Equal substitution in the now
actually typed right body, with \(N\cong N'\), gives
\([N/x]M'\cong[N'/x]M'=C_1\). This is the asserted source proof.
For products the preceding landing lemma provides actual star typings.
For lambdas the arbitrary original body types suffice.

Both original redexes have actual principal typings by common
abstraction/application, and the contracta by source substitution.
The already independently proved full annotated source normalization
therefore makes their beta heights finite. Each root contraction is
an available step, so
\(\eta_\beta(R_i)\geq1+\eta_\beta(C_i)\) for each \(i\).
Adding gives the strict endpoint decrease. The replacement's middle
term is not one of these endpoints. No assertion that its height is
smaller, or that this is a global proof measure, has been made.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p7.ch88.object-normalize}{thm:p07-ch88-object-normalize}{Recursive calls on original subproofs only}
\begin{proof}
Induct on \(\operatorname{ht}(d)\) of the original equality proof,
simultaneously for product and lambda heads. A headed base is copied.
For symmetry normalize its ORIGINAL child and symmetrize the headed
output. For transitivity normalize its two ORIGINAL children and
compose; the stipulated intermediate has the same head. Both calls
have smaller original height, independently of output proof sizes.

For a cancelling beta round trip the two contracta are the same raw
term, since the supplied redex is identical. Single substitution of
one beta body's typing gives that term an actual principal typing.
Full typed reflexivity is a headed replacement at either object head.

For a diamond, the landing shape lemma gives two cases. First suppose
both templates are literally \(H\)-headed. Normalize the certified
ORIGINAL body-equality subproof in \(\Gamma[x:A]\). It is a proper
subproof of the primitive middle lambda equality, hence has strictly
smaller height than \(d\). Source single substitution of this
headed proof by \(N:A\) remains headed: its outer reflexivity,
congruence, symmetry and transitivity nodes retain their constructor
heads. The substituted side-premise proofs may enlarge but are not
recursive inputs. This gives a headed \(C_0\cong[N/x]M'\).

The diamond's right guard and annotation equality, as in the commuting
lemma, construct an actual right-body typing in \(\Gamma[x:A]\).
Apply asymmetric equal substitution to that literal headed template.
Its construction property gives one outer \(H\)-congruence for
\([N/x]M'\cong C_1\), with arbitrary internal annotation/body
proofs. Compose these headed outputs. There is no recursive call
on that generated proof or on its new intermediate term.

If both templates are the selected variable, then \(C_0=N\) and
\(C_1=N'\). Normalize the certified ORIGINAL argument equality,
a proper subproof of the middle application congruence and therefore
of \(d\). Its headed output is already the required result. The
body/annotation proofs need not be enlarged or recursively checked
for this branch. The mixed case was proved impossible.

These are all certificate cases. Every recursive call strictly
decreases a natural-number height of an original proof subtree.
All other operations are the already proved finite source proof
constructions. Thus the algorithm terminates and produces actual
headed equality, without supposing all equality paths admit a certificate.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p7.ch88.object-injection}{thm:p07-ch88-object-injection}{Using the new unheaded-to-headed proof, and lambda projection}
\begin{proof}
Normalize the certified product proof. It has exactly the original
context and endpoints, so the prior product-injection construction
\cref{thm:p07-ch88-ordinary-product-injection} applies with the
actual first annotation certificate. Its domain/body equalities are
source derivations. Normalize each certified equality in a generated
function conversion chain; the original endpoints are unchanged.
The proof-valued branch of \cref{thm:p07-ch88-ordinary-root} then
casts the argument, substitutes the extracted body equality and
reapplies the actual final conversion chain. This is a new family
of genuine unheaded proofs admitted by that root result.

For lambda-headed injection flatten only its outer symmetry and
transitivity. At a lambda congruence leaf the actual domain equality,
body equality and body-typing guard provide the body premise in its
first-domain context and that domain's certificate. At typed lambda
reflexivity, source generation gives its body typing; full reflexivity
of that body gives the body equality and its context the domain
certificate. Accumulate domain equalities along the oriented path.
At each leaf transport its original body equality to the fixed first
annotation context using actual source context conversion, then orient
and concatenate it. This is the same finite construction as product
injection, now with the stated lambda guards. A right-domain sorting
certificate is not fabricated: only the actual first domains of the
primitive leaves and the assumed initial domain are used.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

\begin{annexitem}{annex:p7.ch88.object-height-obstruction}{thm:p07-ch88-object-height-obstruction}{A fully typed measure failure, not a nonexistence argument}
\begin{proof}
The context is valid by two logical star extensions and the actual
object certificate \(A:\ast\) for \(a:A\). Projection and
abstraction give \(I_A:[v:A]A\). External induction on \(j\)
using actual application gives \(n_j:A\). Full beta gives
\(n_{j+1}\cong n_j\), and transitivity gives \(n_3\cong a\).
Its proof-level beta guard has body type \(A\), not a logical-context
type; this is an ordinary-full example.

In \(\Gamma[x:A]\), thinning gives \(C:\ast\). Abstraction
over \(p:A\) and application to \(x:A\) give \(D(x):\ast\);
primitive beta gives \(D(x)\cong C\). Both products \(M_0,M_1(x)\)
are typed star by object-product formation, with actual valid domains.
Product congruence using \(C\cong D(x)\) in the base and its
\(y:C\) extension gives a PRODUCT-HEADED body equality
\(M_0\cong M_1(x)\). Lambda congruence has domain equality
\(A\cong A\) from \(A:\ast\), this body equality, and actual
left body typing \(M_0:\ast\). Common abstraction/application give
\(R_0:\ast,R_1:\ast\). Both primitive beta landing guards are
present. Full application congruence uses this function equality
and \(n_3\cong a\), producing the required genuine diamond.
Its product-headed body proof is a base certificate, so it belongs
to the supported family without restricting the proof argument equality.

Exactly \(j\) identity contractions are available in \(n_j\);
each removes one identity application and none duplicates its argument.
Annotations are variables, so \(\eta_\beta(n_j)=j\).
For any such argument \(n_j\), \(D(n_j)\) has height \(j+1\):
reduce the entire argument first, then contract the constant-returning
lambda; no other redexes occur. The product's two copies are separate
syntax fields, hence \(\eta_\beta(M_1(n_j))=2(j+1)\).
Thus the new middle at \(j=3\) has height eight.

In \(R_0\) the argument can take its three steps before the outer
constant-returning beta step, and the body has no redex, so its height
is four. In \(R_1\), the body contains two independent constant-
returning redexes with variable argument \(x\). They may reduce
before or after the outer substitution of the inert variable \(a\);
in either ordering there are precisely three steps. Its contractum
has two steps, while the left contractum is normal. This gives
\(0,4,3,2\) on the original outer path and \(0,8,2\) on the
commuted path. Maximum increases from four to eight and sum from nine
to ten. The local endpoint decrease in the commuting lemma remains
true; only its illicit promotion to a global path measure is refuted.
The supported proof normalizer does not recurse on this larger middle
and terminates by its separately proved original-proof-height measure.
\end{proof}
\end{annexitem}

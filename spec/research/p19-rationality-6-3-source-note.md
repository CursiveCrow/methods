Computing a complementary probability does not logically require an
explicit mental image of a false possibility. The exact posterior versus
intervention query is typed by world labels, not by conditioning on
the impossible factual conjunction of b and not-b.

contrasts do not meet the corrected alpha. Typical counterfactual false-

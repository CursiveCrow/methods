## Mathematical content

The body states and the annex proves:

1. Dimensionless monomials as the kernel of the dimension matrix; rank,
   free-exponent count and basis generation.
2. Complete unit-change invariants on the positive domain, including the
   zero-dimensional product case.
3. The covariant output form and the obstruction when output dimensions lie
   outside the input dimension span.
4. Composition and branching of scaling exponents.
5. Constant logarithmic slope and the failure of a single doubling equation
   to establish a power law.
6. Geometric area scaling and consequences of the stipulated circular-motion,
   projectile, central-force and linear-oscillator models.
7. The unique optimum of opposing powers, with the different energy and
   power minima.
8. Exact birthday occupancy probabilities, logarithmic approximation bounds
   and the general higher-collision coefficient formula.
9. Deterministic rectangular bounds and logarithmic-error bounds for product
   estimates.
10. The selected source recurrence, uniform-sphere, quartic-oscillator and
    no-slip rolling consequences under their declared models.

The source does not present the general orbit-factorization proof or the
complete birthday approximation bound in this exact form. These are explicitly
supplied mathematical reconstructions supporting the selected method, not
claims that the source contains identical proofs.

The count uses rank of the actually represented dimension matrix. Positivity,
zero/sign cases, dimensional coefficients, affine temperature conversions,
single-valued outputs, branch choices and dimensionless model parameters
are explicit. Physical laws are declared premises of examples; dimensional
analysis is not presented as proving those laws.

## Direct corrections and qualifications

- Actual p. 105 has diameter sqrt(5) cm and answer sqrt(5/2) cm. Text
  extraction drops radical glyphs; source-image reading prevented replacing
  these by 5 cm or 5/2 cm.
- The unit-change theorem covers positive quantities and multiplicative unit
  scales. Zero strata, signs and affine origins are not folded into the log
  proof.
- A one-product equation can permit multiple branches or a range.
  The single-constant conclusion requires a single-valued output and the
  stated complete input/model family.
- Omitted dimensionless amplitude is not ruled out by counting dimensions.
  The pendulum coefficient 2 pi is derived for the linearized oscillator,
  while the key-ring measurement remains a calibration illustration.
- Dimensional compatibility does not establish semantic relevance of a
  comparison.
- Source equation 4.46, inspected on p. 118, omits gravity and fluid density
  in its terminal-speed equality after their suppression in a proportionality.
  The active expression is v=sqrt(2mg/(cd rho A)); only justified common
  factors are cancelled in comparisons.
- Equal shape does not guarantee equal drag coefficient at changed Reynolds
  number. The observed cone fall comparison is not treated as a complete
  established terminal-speed time series.
- Energy per distance, induced/profile energy costs and vertical lift versus
  horizontal drag are distinguished. The model's minimum is exact only for
  the stipulated two-power objective. Drag conventions retain reference
  areas and the one-half factor.
- Oddness and monotonicity allow both z and z cubed; they do not prove a
  linear gravitational-deflection law. A nonzero derivative at zero is an
  extra weak-field assumption.
- A single-aperture diffraction scale does not prove all smaller astrometric
  effects unobservable. The source narrative is not used as an independent
  historical-confirmation theorem.
- Birthday pair events are not jointly independent. The exact product,
  controlled log approximation and finite occupancy expression replace an
  unsupported independent-pair interpretation.
- The source oil-estimate conversion requires approximately four litres per
  US gallon; the reversed unit phrase is not carried into the active text.
- A lower uncertainty of an estimate or agreement of two calculations is not
  proof that shared assumptions are right.
- A quartic energy equation alone could admit waiting branches in a
  first-order description. The period theorem specifies the second-order
  restoring equation, eliminating that ambiguity.
- Numerical values about population, transport or historical physical
  illustrations are attributed to the selected source context rather than
  presented as current 2026 observations.

These corrections are in the active statements and derivations. The reader is
not asked to repair an earlier false formula.

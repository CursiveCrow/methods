## Source-specific corrections and empirical boundaries

1. The chapter's Fig3.3.1 caption says nearly100% for AA1/Aac. Actual2012
   Table6 gives81% Aac and88% total valid responses for that item. The active
   body does not infer frequencies from heatmap darkness or reproduce the
   caption's near100% assertion as an exact estimate. White cells include
   low nonzero proportions.
2. The chapter's p230 assertion that PSM cannot produce NVC is too broad.
   Hattori's original p304 Figure8 explicitly has max-heuristic gates
   accepting a passing candidate with probability u and falling through
   remaining candidates otherwise, with final NVC. The active text supplies
   the actual mechanism and preserves the narrower criticism: categorical
   sample tests/gated rejection do not themselves reconstruct a person's
   counterexample-search process. The proved complementary-test fact applies
   to the ungated all-four-test procedure, not the full original algorithm.
3. In the reproduced prototype figure, .04 is rounded. Original Table2
   gives the AI2 overlap mass cx²=.90×.20²=.036; seven-draw miss
   is .964^7=.773640571929416. Those are model probabilities, not human
   response fractions. Prototype compatibility alone does not condition
   samples on satisfying every existential premise.
4. The chapter's EA1 existential import was repaired directly by the
   b-nonemptiness premise and its explicit witness proof. P2's modern
   empty-class semantics is retained. The task figure numbering and nine
   categories are explicit, and NVC is restricted to its eight targets.
5. mReasoner's journal copy explicitly truncates the size distribution;
   the chapter's bare Poisson shorthand does not supply a nonempty model.
   Later fitted ranges/subgroup parameters differ from2016 and are not
   pooled. Empty cells in chapter diagrams are not taken as complete
   negative assignments; complete countermodels are separately declared.
6. 2012 Table 5 has six datasets with nominal counts 20,20,20,20,20,56.
   The first two are the same 20 Columbia students' two sessions, not 156
   independent people. The author-selected weighted comparison is recorded,
   including shared-laboratory/nonindependence limits. Its response hits,
   rejections and 95% intervals are not participants' logical accuracy.
   The prose at-least 16% and Table 8 strict 16% threshold notations differ;
   exact boundary reproduction needs the actual coding, not rounded tables.
7. 2016 simulations reanalyze those 20 students, with exploratory three-group
   clustering and 1512 settings × 100 simulated repetitions of each 64-task set.
   Subgroup r=.74,.82,.90 and individual mean r=.70, range .54–.87, are
   same-data selected fits, not held-out psychological-type diagnoses.
   The source admits an Oba/Acb failure and prospective transfer remains open.
8. 2018 practice reanalysis uses the same 20 people twice about a week apart,
   spontaneous timed conclusions to all 64 tasks, different random orders,
   certainty instructions and unanticipated retest. 58→68% and 19/20 improved
   with reported binomial p<.0001 are observations; no randomized no-retest
   control, no retained trial-order data and no control of between-session
   studying. Model search/weakening parameter changes are fitted, not observed
   process measures. Reported aggregate correlations and individual fit
   percentages are kept separate; no new CI was computed.
9. Hattori's original new experiments use inclusion/prevalence manipulations,

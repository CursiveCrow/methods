1. Independent countable completeness yields closed finite schemata.
2. Rule-by-rule Boolean-valued soundness is proved directly for an

3. Provable-biconditional congruence and every listed Boolean axiom

4. Soundness makes evaluation constant on classes and supplies the

5. Finite ND trees and Boolean soundness prove F_Gamma membership
   exactly equivalent to derivability.
6. The complement-adjoining filter is explicitly proved proper before
   one BPI extension. Its homomorphism gives every truth-lemma clause.
7. The countervaluation proves completeness and finite support; the
   converse scheme supplies exact arbitrary compactness and hence BPI.

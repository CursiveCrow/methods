## Consequential qualifications retained in the body

- Wissenschaft includes social sciences and humanities (note 1). Analytic
  encounters with science and strategic science-promotion campaigns are
  different senses of PUS (note 2).
- The chapter's science/rationality nexus is not scientism; note 3 explicitly
  rejects scientific monopoly on rational propositions. Bayes' mathematical
  identity and Newton's physical-model law have different justification
  standards; the latter's applicability includes inertial/zero-net-force
  conditions. No new mathematical proof or physical observation is inferred
  from community acceptance alone.
- Locally coherent background beliefs do not thereby receive global evidential
  warrant. The homeopathy example is an epistemic illustration, not medical
  advice or an experimental claim established by this intake.
- Limited technical understanding is not merely an information void or proof
  that every disagreement is a deficit. Stable prior models, uncertainty,
  complexity and distributed expertise all matter (note 5).
- Unqualified expert assertion is not deductive entailment. Defeasible reliance
  can nevertheless be warranted without independently reproducing every
  technical derivation. Citizen deference and a scientific community's duty
  to generate/test evidence are distinct (note 6).
- Expertise, field pertinence, integrity, benevolence, perceived trustworthiness,
  actual reliability and action following advice are not collapsed. Own
  interests do not automatically preclude benevolence.
- The source's source-attention, easiness, British 70%, framing, gamers,
  knowledge/polarization and mechanistic-explanation reports retain their
  review-level status. No single sample denominator, universal effect or
  synthesized uncertainty is invented.
- Motivated reasoning is not a diagnosis of a named dissenting person; note 7
  allows disagreement about what counts as evidence, not only deliberate
  rejection. Cultural classification itself is contested.
- Democratic analogy is an epistemic comparison about judging whom to trust,
  not proof of equal competence, legitimate coercion or a policy prescription
  (note 8). Educational source-evaluation approaches remain cited proposals
  at the chapter's scope (note 9).
- METI measures ascriptions to a particular source. It does not validate the
  actual accuracy of a scientist, measure a national public's trust, or prove
  that a scored judgment produces real deference. The 2015 discussion expressly
  leaves actual advice following/information seeking for future work.
- Study 2 confirms a modified rather than completely fixed measurement model:
  two further items were removed and a residual covariance allowed. Correlated
  subscales and low communalities on two integrity items stay visible.
- Study 3 is a within-person vignette comparison, with all six descriptions
  presented in randomized order. It is not six independently recruited groups,
  a treatment of actual neurologists, or a real-world accuracy intervention.

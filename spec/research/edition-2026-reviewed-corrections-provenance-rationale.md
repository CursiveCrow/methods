## Mapping and standing

Each old version-1 record is preserved byte for byte before replacement.
Each version-4 candidate retains the original source authority, locator,
source-unit inventory and audit evidence. It pins the current reader bytes
separately and records every insertion, replacement and deletion using
zero-based half-open line ranges and exact normalized slice hashes.
Unchanged gaps and tails must match completely. Replacement lines receive
no invented original source locator, even when a token resembles old text.

The source remains archived exactly. Candidate source/static validation only
establishes identity, edit replay, membership, route and pending boundaries.
All completeness, independent-source, build, visual and human candidate
fields remain pending. Linked source-unit records stay open/missing with
pending/unclassified audit results and pending content/AI/build/visual fields;
their mechanical static evidence may be rebound only after actual validation.
No source-unit closure, generated-view promotion or user acceptance follows.

three independent manuscript-content review gates for exactly N-c-015.b: the
finite-ranked-tree and weak-monadic-second-order-logic presentation based on the
18 November 2008 release of Hubert Comon et al., *Tree Automata Techniques and
Applications* (TATA).

source definitions, finite-tree automata, determinization, Boolean closure,

## Mathematical and source determinations

The three final reviews independently established these bounded
determinations:

- The source tuple A=(Q,F,Q_f,Delta) and its map to the handbook tuple are
  explicit; nullary and positive-arity transitions, bottom-up runs, final-root
  acceptance, and recognized languages remain correctly typed.
- Determinization retains same-language strength and reachable subset states.
  Boolean closure and the upward marking criterion for emptiness retain their
  source roles and qualifications.
- Weak monadic set variables range over finite word sets. The restricted atomic
  formulas, finite-set tracks, bottom padding, finite position domain, label
  predicates, child relations, and quantifier restrictions are stated before
  they are used.
- The formula-to-automaton direction retains the complete inclusion automaton,
  missing-coordinate extension, union, complement, and projection steps. The
  automaton-to-formula direction retains the state-set partition, final-root
  condition, and correctly directed local transition clause, including the
  nullary case.
- TATA's warning that Theorem 3.3.7 is not precise remains visible; Lemmas
  3.3.4 and 3.3.6 control the exact directions. The closed-sentence convention
  uses the empty alphabet or sole nullary top, with validity reduced to a
  transition into a final state.
- The Exercise 1.4 alphabet and copying language are exact. The supplied
  deterministic state-separation proof establishes reachability of every term
  before using the collision argument and is marked as handbook-owned.
- Finite input trees are not conflated with Rabin's infinite labelled tree.
  Weak and full monadic ranges are distinguished on the infinite successor
  structure while their finite-domain coincidence is stated only where every
  subset is finite.
- Thatcher--Wright remains an abstract-level historical source with an open
  body-dependent acquisition obligation. TATA is not substituted for that
  missing article, and the obligation is stated without assigning a
  hypothetical technical answer.
- The acquired Thomas file remains a May 1996 report precursor rather than a
  verified facsimile of the registered 1997 chapter. Its literal prospective
  handbook title and the eventual publication title remain separate.
- Source statements, handbook examples and proofs, access and licence,
  acquisition and admission, and manuscript and publication status remain
  distinct. The terminology audit found every material distinction defined or
  given exact source standing before reliance.

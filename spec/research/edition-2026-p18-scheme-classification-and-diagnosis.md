## Direct corrections and source qualifications

1. The source's p. 2496 causal-form discussion does not license automatic
   contraposition of defaults or inference from an absent cause to an
   absent effect. The reader's five-way comparison states the necessary
   extra conditions and gives a device countercase.
2. The p. 2507 prose has a reversed genus/species predication phrase.
   The reader text gives the correct direction: predicates universal
   over the genus apply to its species, while a species-specific property
   need not characterize the genus. The source's later Table 8 supports
   the correct inclusion direction.
3. Grennan's list contains nine warrant types, though p. 2514 subsequently
   says eight. The reader map directly gives the nine actually listed,
   separately from the eight claim types.
4. Table 18 is headed goal-based practical reasoning although its contents
   are the slope scheme. The reader text directly identifies it as the
   basic slope scheme. CQ1's endpoint is An, not the isolated printed n.
5. The p. 2528 identification prose calls its conditions three and then
   four while enumerating five. The reader directly supplies five plus
   the sixth value-based condition.
6. Tables 12 and 17 differ: one is a means-to-goal heuristic, the other
   explicitly includes values and necessary-or-sufficient means.
   The earlier handbook necessary-means presentation is not substituted.
7. Figure 8 mixes subtype, composition and argumentative opposition.
   Its arrows are not represented as one formal inclusion relation.
8. The compressed source political slope is an interpretive reconstruction.
   Missing links are not treated as facts established by common knowledge.
   The new example explicitly identifies those missing premises.
9. The p. 2536 informal Dung description is not used as a complete binary
   acceptance rule. The reader uses the existing complete-labeling
   distinction and retains undecided cases.
10. The p. 2546 dependency example reverses the direction in its prose.
    The reader states that Z depends along the X-to-Y-to-Z path on X,
    and distinguishes this from transitive defeasible entailment.
11. The numerical experiment and corpus reports are attributed to the
    2017 article. They are not independently reproduced measurements or
    current platform/dataset claims. Its broad forecast about statistical
    limits is not represented as a theorem.
12. A schematic ontology role for an exception is not an assertion
    that the exception holds. Inherited questions require the actual
    role-preserving subtype definition, not mere membership in a cluster.

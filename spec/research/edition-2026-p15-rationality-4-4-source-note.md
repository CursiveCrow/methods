## Clarifications and correction provenance

1. Probability zero is not logical impossibility. Conditional gains are
   tested on the active conditioning union, not made automatically coherent
   by their zeros outside that union. All conditioning events are possible.
2. First-stage linear feasibility alone is insufficient for conditional
   coherence when some conditions get zero weight. The annex proves the
   recursive restriction and its finite termination, then proves the
   stratum witness principle used by every endpoint example.
3. The full[0,1] noninformation endpoints rely on null-conditioning strata.
   Under ordinary positive-denominator quotient conditioning, p(B)=0 and
   p(B)=1 force0 and1 respectively. The chapter's loose phrase about
   positive premise probabilities is made precise as the strict interior
   0<p(B)<1, with its stated endpoint exception preserved.
4. Tight generic formulas assume no additional logical dependency among
   the schematic atoms. In particular a tautologicalB differs from a
   probability-oneB; known logical implications must not be discarded.
5. Aristotle's two conditions require bothA andnotA possible. Otherwise
   one of the source's conditional expressions is outside its own domain.
6. The Boethius/Abelard validation retains the chapter's narrow-scope and
   threshold reading. It does not fabricate nestedconditional-event
   semantics or a complete connexive calculus. Wide-scope variants are
   separately identified source references, not silently adopted.
7. Changing the conditioning event is not adding constraints on a fixed
   target. Fixed-target feasible-extension sets shrink monotonically.
8. AND uses Frechet constituent bounds, not multiplication of marginals;
   the product in CUT/MP is justified by the conditional premises. The
   constructible zero endpoint is proved, not hidden by an undefined ratio.
9. The source's experimental support is not a proof of normative authority.
   Lower-bound AND errors differ from upper-bound conjunction errors; the
   Linda interpretation objection is retained without blanket denial of
   observed conjunction effects. Pragmatic reading of transitivity as CUT
   is an interpretation hypothesis, not a redefinition of transitivity.
10. Source note 1 prohibits nested simple conditionals. The richer
    conditional-random-quantity claim is explicitly cited and bounded,
    with future psychological validation retained. No unsupplied proof is
    relabeled proved or used to discharge a separately promised full result.

## Exact source qualifications and discrepancies

All zero--one sentence claims explicitly require positive relation
arities and no constants/functions. A nullary independent relation
has probability p in (0,1) already as a noncritical quantifier-free
sentence. Elimination alone cannot establish that unqualified extension.

The held p.25 prints below-half thresholds 1/2−1/m as having measure0,
and identifies their disjunction with Q_half. Both assertions fail.
The new correction uses m>=3 and their conjunction: each conjunct is
noncritical with true content and asymptotic measure1; their conjunction
is exactly Q_half, of limiting probability1/2. This proves the intended
common-class and unrestricted-infinitary obstruction without adopting
the printed signs/disjunction.

The held p.22 lists all bipartite graphs as parametric. Under an
ordinary uncolored single-binary-relation interpretation, this cannot
satisfy Definition5.1: permitted loopless singleton, empty pair and
undirected-edge pair configurations also permit a triangle. The full
counterexample retains that precise vocabulary restriction; no
unspecified enriched/sorted bipartite presentation is invented.
The p.22 arity-only all-size existence criterion also needs a convention
guard: with equality literals and a unary vocabulary, the parametric
constraint forall x y[D(x,y) implies x=y] has singleton models but
none at size2or larger. The new countermodel is explicit; the transfer
theorem retains all-size existence as an actual assumption instead.
Symmetry/irreflexivity are actual parametric constraints, but conditioning
ordered p-bits on symmetry changes edge probability to
p²/(p²+(1−p)²), illustrating why unconditioned critical values cannot
simply be reused.

Source sequence criticality is stricter than pointwise inequality:
a threshold approaching a constant critical mean from below by1/n
still has both liminf differences0. The ordinary existential clause
requires positive liminf or a genuine parameter-witness validity.
The probability concentration bound needs no global bound keeping all
atomic probabilities away from0and1; its required eventual gaps are
derived from the selected syntax tests. The sequence result here retains
independent ground atoms, not arbitrary size-dependent parametric
conditioning. The single-variable threshold1/n denotes one witness at
n>1; its excluded n=1 endpoint and distinct noncriticality test are clear.

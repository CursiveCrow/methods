## Written reasoning and evidence boundaries

The annex contains only two substantive logical results:
`thm:p19-conditional-benchmark` / `annex:p19.conditional-benchmark`, and
`thm:p19-suppression-reconstruction` / `annex:p19.suppression-reconstruction`.
They prove the MP/MT benchmark, AC/DA countervaluations, monotonicity,
the difference between a literal pair of conditionals and a conjunctive
reinterpretation, and the finite contextual-state reconstruction. Empirical
rates and psychological explanations are not given theorem/proof status.

The actual source task contents and response formats are described with
their logical structure; English content is paraphrased, not represented as
a new verbatim stimulus transcription. The library appendix's essay-finishing
wording is distinguished from the essay-writing introductory illustration.
The later Contravening premise negates the consequence, whereas Byrne's
Additional premise has the same consequent. Their numerical contrasts cannot
be pooled as if they were one instrument or estimand.

The reconstructed gate and context states are explicitly hypothetical
handbook interpretations, not recorded participant mental states. A context
expansion admits a valuation that falsifies the earlier unrestricted
material conditional; the chapter directly explains why this cannot refute
deductive MP with premises and meanings held fixed. Both cognitive accounts
require a further account of when interpretations or alternatives change.

Read the actual P19 evidence-methods chapter and its recording, uncertainty,
denominator and comparability standards, plus the existing P02 valuation
and semantic-consequence definitions. The body preserves source-specific
N, exclusions, repeated-item structures, reported uncertainty and limiting
comparisons. Missing raw data, an unresolved correction text, computed
percentage-point differences, and unknown recruitment details remain distinct.
It makes no causal claim about a uniquely isolated mental process and no
universal generalization to ages, cultures, experts or everyday reasoning.

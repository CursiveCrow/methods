## Precision issues identified in the complete source reading

- The source's extra satisfiable-premise condition is a nonvacuous criterion
  for selected right reasoning, not the ordinary definition of classical
  truth-preserving consequence. Both must be stated accurately.
- Possibilities allowed by a premise family are distinct from truth of an
  object-language modal formula at a world. Default expansion of live
  possibilities must not be silently identified with unrestricted modal
  consequence or with a unique empirical representation.
- Logic alone does not provide a probability of confirmation. The swan/
  carnation discussion requires distinguishing logical equivalence from
  confirmation assumptions.
- Three numbers reconstructing a binary JPD automatically give cells that
  sum to one by algebra; coherence additionally requires nonnegative cells.
  An independently elicited fourth cell can instead fail normalization.
- The 2021 future political examples are dated illustrations, not verified
  current assertions or verbatim task items administered in 2015.
- A represented possible outcome need not have positive mass under every
  probability measure. Any equal-weighting assumption must be stated.
- Failure to find a model is not itself a proof of inconsistency. Complete
  finite enumeration can certify it; incomplete psychological search cannot.
- Uncomputability of shortest arbitrary programs does not imply that no
  nontrivial minimality proof is possible. A finite train algorithm needs
  explicit move semantics and a correctness invariant; its instruction
  length is a language-dependent quantity.
- Program-stored causal stories are not medical evidence. Corrections
  belong in active examples and statements; historical provenance belongs
  here, without asking readers to repair an earlier false assertion.
- The 2021 repeated-selection illustration uses triangles-are-white;
  the actual 1970 Experiment I used triangles-are-black. Both versions are
  identified rather than relabeling the original administered material.
- The selected 2021 four-card rule is E-implies-2, not the neighboring
  vowel-implies-even task. The active theorem and full proof retain that
  exact equality rule with unrestricted hidden letter/number faces.
- The 2016 modal study's held copy is advance-online pages 1-15 plus
  its cover, not a final-paginated 1606-1620 PDF. Its logic comparison
  explicitly adds joint-possibility assumptions. Its four possibility
  ratings are not four mutually exclusive JPD-cell ratings.
- The 2015 unique-event disjunction experiment did not show the predicted
  order effect on violations or additive strategies; the fixed-order
  conditional experiment did not test that prediction. These limits are
  written into the body rather than reporting undifferentiated confirmation.
- The 2013 and 2016 papers' measured Lisp description lengths are upper
  bounds in their declared representation, not verified exact shortest
  programs. Loop names alone do not determine computational power.
- The 2016 gesture difference is 13 percentage points (65 versus 52), not
  an unqualified relative 13-percent effect. Its conspicuous muff, carryover
  and speech/gesture coding are part of the evidence boundary.

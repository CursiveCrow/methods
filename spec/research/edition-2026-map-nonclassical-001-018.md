tense, free, deontic and conditional-logic requirements, preserved under the

and component allocation. It does not assert source-body qualification,
mathematical proof acceptance or publication readiness.

## Scope decisions that affect completion work

- N-b-001 retains seven completeness obligations. The existing four-system
  K/KT/S4/S5 proof does not supply KD, KTB or K4. Schema B, KB and the KTB
  system alias B stay distinct.
- N-b-005 requires the actual Thomason and Fine constructions, not merely
  their two existence statements.
- N-b-006 selects Montague's *Pragmatics and Intensional Logic*, Synthese22,
  68–94, DOI10.1007/BF00413599. The canonical *Universal Grammar* record
  stays preserved as a different source. Scott and Montague's original
  semantics are not retroactively credited with every modern E/EM/EC/EN/EMC
  completeness theorem. The identity evidence is the root's prior primary
  publisher reading recorded in the additional-identities file.
- N-b-009 selects Kripke's *Semantical Considerations on Modal Logic*,
  Acta Philosophica Fennica16, 83–94. The propositional *Semantical Analysis I*
  record is distinct. Current Fitting2002 covers a bounded semantic model;
  the Fitting–Mendelsohn1998 book and other original quantified-modal
  presentations remain separately required.
- N-b-010 separates nonexistence, outer denotation and undefined descriptions.
  N-b-011 separates Aumann's agreement theorem from S5/KD45 adequacy and
  distinguishes common-knowledge weak completeness from strong completeness.
- N-b-012 preserves the first DEL edition's 2007 release/2008 copyright
  distinction and allocates the original Baltag–Moss–Solecki action-model
  presentation separately from PAL reduction.
- N-b-013 does not silently attribute the current nonvacuous smooth-preorder
  dyadic semantics to Hansson without the required source comparison.
- N-b-014 binds Kamp's strict operand/order conventions to the separate P9
  source development. N-b-015 does not attribute modern K(@) tableaux to Prior.
- N-b-016 preserves current Lewis VC=C1 nomenclature and separates Stalnaker
  C2, while requiring verification of the naming bridge against the original
  sources. The actual present declaration is
  `thm:p08-vc-completeness`, cited to Lewis1971 and Wen2019. Its limit-free
  sphere semantics must survive subsequent writing.
- N-b-017 keeps the four-atom present triviality statement distinct from
  Lewis1976's complete original argument. N-b-018 does not strengthen CK by
  adding antecedent inclusion, which would validate ID.

The separately existing temporal-dynamic-epistemic annex is not routed by the
current P8 annex root. It is not counted as active proof. Proposed destinations
are finite integration tasks; no empty manuscript files have been created.

Every unknown body or verification result remains an acquisition, writing or
acceptance task. The named extent itself is fixed; no requirement is removed
because its current presentation is only cited, sketched or absent.

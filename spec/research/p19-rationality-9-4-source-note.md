## Corrections required by the actual source reading

- Independence's biconditional cannot include mixing weight zero: it would
  collapse every pair to indifference despite best strictly exceeding worst.
- Problem-T prints discount exponent `t-i`; for impatience with `0<delta<1`
  the intended forward exponent is `i-t`.
- Source proposition 4 omits original Fishburn--Rubinstein domain A0,
  loss-impatience, bidirectional date-shift scope, and the continuous-time,
  sign-sector qualification on uniqueness. The corrected theorem is cited
  to the original, not silently attributed to the chapter's abbreviated text.
- Individual dated-outcome axioms do not by themselves prove additive
  utility over streams. Monetary amount ratios identify discount factors only
  under the separately specified linear utility/no-other-consequences model.
- Choice-overload purchasing does not identify preference incompleteness;
  different response formats alone do not prove a within-context cycle.
- `2^40` counts binary histories, not a necessary lower bound on solution
  operations. NP-completeness does not prove exponential running time for
  every algorithm or every economic problem.
- Cross-species behavioral resemblance does not alone prove a shared
  genetically hardwired prospect-theory representation. Reported market
  anomalies require an information/return/fundamental-value model; the
  chapter's Greenspan causal interpretation is not an identified causal study.

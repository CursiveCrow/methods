Four result/annex pairs have full locally supplied proofs, with suffixes
coding, normalization, range and obstruction under thm:p15-pr-learning-
and annex:p15-pr-learning-. This is a proof-status assertion by the author,
not independent acceptance.

- Coding: concrete pairing/finite-history encoding, primitive-recursive
  inverse and prefix operations, explicit two-stack left/right updates,
  fixed numerical digit protocol, history verification and output adequacy.
  Finite simulation does not assert a universal terminating evaluator.
- Normalization: a compatible serial simulation of all original prefix
  calls in order, at most one completion per tick. Prefix calls cannot
  outrun the available data. Every call completes under actual totality,
  every answer is displayed at a strictly increasing finite completion tick,
  and both convergence directions preserve the identical final code.
  Divergence is preserved as well. The text itself is unchanged.
  This corrects the inadequate largest-fast-completed-prefix shortcut,
  which can skip divergent answers and supplies no reverse implication.
- Range: a primitive-recursive accepting-history predicate and complete
  pairing enumeration, with a fixed a_L in a nonempty target as default.
  The range is exactly the target, without deciding membership or emptiness.
- Obstruction: native I.14–I.22 construction, with source-native G,g_t,
  Z_a,T,U,X_t,P_t,Q_t and predicate-definition/conjunction glyphs retained.
  Typed roles and actual source-level selectors are in the new intake.
  A coded state recurrence proves the text primitive recursive. CaseI
  contradicts infinite range; CaseII gives finite nonempty content and
  rules out a stabilized tester's rejection of an omitted word; CaseIII
  proves all f-values appear by excluding a held position and then checking
  every passed position, and a late Q witness rejects an included word.
  Intermediate hypotheses need not be valid or total testers.

The theorem includes the source naming prerequisite: a target lacking any
tester name is not declared learned through vacuous quantification over
missing primitive-recursive presentations. The explicit countertext
construction applies when the selected infinite target is recursive.
Worked cases include a slow changing-hypothesis serial trace and the
unary-word delayed-candidate construction. Empty pure texts are not needed
for the counterexample; source/tester/generator and host-pause distinctions
remain separate.

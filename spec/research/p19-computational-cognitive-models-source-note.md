## Correction provenance

The active explanation integrates these corrections; sources remain frozen.

- Printed 58 repeats John-as-lover/Mary-as-beloved when describing the
  reversed proposition. Mary-as-lover/John-as-beloved supplies the intended
  contrast; no claim that the two encodings distinguish roles is left false.
- Figure 5.5 caption says 2,3, but diagram and preceding paragraph place
  the circle at 2,2 above the square at 3,2. Reader uses 2,2 coherently.
- Printed 62's prose reverses `contains(coffee,cup)` after assigning
  outside to cup and inside to coffee; Figure 5.6 labels an inside+cup
  RB despite its coffee PO. Reader retains container-first cup-containing-
  coffee throughout, including the nested Jim proposition.
- Printed 63's inside/outside variable convention similarly reverses the
  final contains arguments. Reader uses explicit roles instead of retaining
  the contradictory application.
- The universally quantified passing/collecting example at 54 cannot
  infer all rewards merely from passing. Reader states the context's
  supplied reward premise instead of reproducing an overstrong formula.
- Printed 57's nonlinear-network capacity assertion lacks hypotheses.
  Exact/approximate, domain, activation, width and learning are separated;
  full finite-continuity/rational-step counterexample establishes a concrete
  failure under an explicit common interpretation. No nonexistent universal
  theorem or proof is fabricated.
- Tensor similarity identities at 61 do not entail the impossibility of all
  decoding/generalization. A fully proved orthonormal-role decoder is an
  explicit counterexample. Whole-binding similarity concerns and the
  authors' architectural preference remain stated, not erased.
- The original spatial paper includes agent-relative relation queries and
  new-object/configuration transfer. Reader does not repeat the chapter's
  overbroad "only already-taught answers" as an established fact.

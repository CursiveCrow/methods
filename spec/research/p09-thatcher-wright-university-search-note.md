# Thatcher-Wright 1968: university and public-PDF search

Date: 2026-09-06.

## Result and scope

This second, bounded acquisition pass did **not locate a downloadable
full copy** of Thatcher and Wright's 1968 article or the same-title 1966
IBM report RC-1713. No source file was saved, and
`tmp/pdfs/p09-thatcher-wright-1968` was not created. This reports the
checked routes; it does not prove that no public copy exists.

The target is J. W. Thatcher and J. B. Wright, **Generalized finite
automata theory with an application to a decision problem of second-order
logic**, *Mathematical Systems Theory* **2**(1) (March 1968), **57-81**,
DOI [10.1007/BF01691346](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01691346). The preceding
local and official-record verification is recorded in
`spec/research/p09-thatcher-wright-acquisition-source-note.md`.

The present pass concentrated on university course and author pages,
institutional PDF collections, scholarly bibliographies with attachments,
and exact filename/title/report-number searches. The user subsequently
specified a downloadable copy and declined the paid route. No purchase,
external message, or account signup was performed in this pass. No
shadow-library or paywall-bypass route was used.

## Search coverage

Queries combined the full title or its distinctive initial phrase with
the authors, 1968, `filetype:pdf`, university domains, course/readings
terms, and `RC1713`/`RC-1713`/`RC 1713`. Filename probes included
`thatcher-wright.pdf`, `thatcherwright.pdf`, `TW68.pdf`, `1968.pdf`, and
`thatcher.pdf`. University-specific searches included UCLA, Cornell,
Stanford, Stony Brook, and `.edu`/`.ac.uk` collections; repository searches
included CiteSeer, CORE, NASA NTRS, DTIC, and archival title searches.

The relevant returned attachments were later treatments or references.
Exact article identity was checked before treating any PDF as a possible
acquisition. Search snippets containing the target's bibliographic entry
were not taken as evidence that the attachment contains its body.

## Direct candidate checks

### UCLA: Tim Hunter's chapter and supplement

Both of the following PDFs were successfully fetched into memory and
inspected with a PDF reader:

- [The Chomsky Hierarchy](https://timhunter.humspace.ucla.edu/papers/blackwell-chomsky-hierarchy.pdf),
  Tim Hunter, Department of Linguistics, UCLA, February 2020: **18 PDF
  pages**. Its first page identifies a chapter for the *Blackwell
  Companion to Chomsky*; Thatcher-Wright occurs in its bibliography.
- [Unofficial supplement to Blackwell chapter on the Chomsky Hierarchy](https://www.timhunter.humspace.ucla.edu/papers/blackwell-chomsky-hierarchy-supplement.pdf),
  Tim Hunter, UCLA, 6 July 2020: **14 PDF pages**. A footnote points to
  Thatcher-Wright 1968, pp. 57-59, for the algebraic view of automata; the
  bibliography lists the target.

Neither PDF embeds the 25-page journal article. Both PDFs' annotation
arrays were checked for external URI links; **neither has such links**.
Thus these attachments provide no hidden bibliography download route to
the original. The supplement is a concrete indication of a later scholar
using the opening pages, but it is not a downloadable target copy.

### Saarland seminar via CiteSeer

Programming Systems Lab, Seminar Logical Aspects of XML, Saarland
University, 22 October 2003. It presents later proofs of results attributed
to Buechi and Thatcher-Wright. It is not the 1968 article. A direct body
fetch timed out and the web reader returned an internal error, so its PDF
annotations were not inspected. No original-paper attachment was verified.

These findings do not justify copying definitions, theorem numbering, or
proof architecture into a reconstruction attributed to Thatcher-Wright.

## Author and library leads

The [Berkeley Logic Library](https://logic-library.berkeley.edu/) describes
a physical offprint/dissertation/course-note collection in the Alfred
Tarski Room, 727 Evans Hall. Its entries include earlier Thatcher works
and a **Wright, Jesse B.: Publications** entry. No target-title PDF
attachment was exposed, and the observed inventory did not establish that
the library holds the exact target article. This is an institutional
holdings lead, not a verified download.

Jim Thatcher's maintained [historical personal site](https://jimthatcher.com/about.htm)
had already been checked in the preceding acquisition pass; it describes
his IBM automata research but did not expose a research-paper archive.
The author identity and relationship to Jesse Wright are also stated in
his first-person [ACM SIGCAS interview](https://www.sigcas.org/making-a-difference-award-2009-jim-thatcher-interview/).
These biographical pages were not promoted into source-body evidence, and
the author-about/publisher loops were not repeated as the acquisition
strategy.

The UCLA supplement is the strongest newly verified, specific scholarly
lead because it cites the target's opening pages. There is nevertheless
**no verified direct target-PDF URL remaining among the checked
attachments**. Asking a scholar or institutional collection for a copy
would be an external coordination step, not an acquisition achieved by
this search; no such request was sent.

## Separate catalogue check

The [Semantic Scholar graph endpoint for the exact DOI](https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/DOI:10.1007/BF01691346?fields=title,authors,year,openAccessPdf,url)
returned the exact title, year 1968, authors J. Thatcher and J. B. Wright,
and paper ID `2727b71c9b7c73a9b6b64f0c025aca7a2a93de94`. Its
`openAccessPdf` record had an empty URL, `status: CLOSED`, and a null
license. This is only an aggregator's current discovery result, not a
proof of worldwide unavailability. It adds no downloadable source.

The title-identical [Cambridge result](https://www.cambridge.org/core/product/identifier/S0022481200079597/type/journal_article)
is Dirk Siefkes's **review**, *Journal of Symbolic Logic* **37**(3)
(September 1972), pp. 619-620, DOI 10.2307/2272790. It must not be mistaken
for the 25-page article, even though its heading reproduces the target's
full citation.

## Handoff

The requested exact article remains unacquired after this bounded pass.
No public university compilation containing its full pp. 57-81 was
verified. The older local Thatcher dissertation and the later papers
listed above remain distinct sources. This note supplies the checked
identities and unresolved access leads so later work can start from new
evidence rather than repeat the same candidate attachments.

Theorem7.7's printed search tests only a small polygon value without
relating k to the requested precision. That test alone is unsound.

P_0=0, P_j=f for j>=1 satisfies its strict pairwise clause.

absolute(P_k(s))+2^(-k)<2^(-n), with a full termination/error proof.

the repaired condition to the source.

Native Theorem7.1 builds a zero set from an enumerated open complement

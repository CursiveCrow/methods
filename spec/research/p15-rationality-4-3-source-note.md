## Direct corrections and domain qualifications

- Definition 1's prose reverses A/B relative to its displayed P(B|A).
  The active definition uses the displayed ratio's correct roles.
- Theorem 5's prose names A/B where its formula names A/C screened by
  B. The active theorem follows the formula and gives the full proof.
- The predicted-evidence application at 267 needs P(D)<1, not merely
  positive P(D) and P(H)<1. If D is certain it cannot increment H;
  the active statement supplies the necessary hypothesis directly.
- Lemma 3's logarithmic sum prints an unlogged L summand. The active
  conjunction formula adds LL to LL.
- Note 7 prints tanh of half log LL. The valid identity is
  `KO = tanh(0.5 ln L) = tanh(0.5 LL)` for natural-log LL.
- Lemma 4's strong reading that all argument reversals preserve the
  fixed-target ranking is false. Valid POP-D forward agreement and the
  symmetric Q implementation are retained. The active text gives
  forward D .3/.2 versus reverse D .03/.2 in a realizable model.
- The white-shoe wording at 272 must not make a single specimen entail
  a universal hypothesis. It entails the specimen nonblack/nonraven
  description; transfer of relevance needs an additional refinement model.
- Ratio/log/compositional identities need support/domain guards.
  MCIE excludes dogmatic updated targets; extended endpoint values and
  neutral completions are not a license to evaluate undefined ratios.
- Z is continuous and piecewise affine in the posterior, but its
  slopes at posterior=prior are 1/prior and 1/(1-prior), unequal
  except at prior=1/2. The active theorem and proof therefore separate
  joint-table transparency from global differentiability.

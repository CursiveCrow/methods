## Source/endpoint corrections and scope

The primary p. 3 defines K-minus A as NOT((NOT A) Since True), which is
vacuously true at a first point. Its p. 13 sentence before Lemma 7.3
instead declares it false there. The new exposition follows the displayed
truth clause, consistent with the already defined Hirshfeld–Rabinovich
convention, and uses true in g. Every interior supremum application has
an actual earlier endpoint and is therefore nonvacuous regardless.

The source's discrete recovery is also stated correctly: traverse through
Boolean and U nodes, replacing each encountered entire K-minus subformula
by false without descending into its operand. Such an outermost past-limit
node is evaluated strictly after the current point and has an immediate
predecessor. This does not falsely replace an unguarded K-minus at an
initial point, or claim every nested operand is evaluated in the future.

The GPSS inclusive future quantifiers and the primary's strict convention
are related by explicit equality/strict case splitting for both existential
and universal quantifiers. Current-point atoms are retained.

The scope is all nonempty Dedekind-complete linear orders, including the
reals, finite orders, integers and naturals. Positive assembly, the
temporal-to-first-order direction and guarded future semantics work on
all linear orders. No positive completeness theorem over the rationals is
asserted. Full almost-future completeness and the wider dense-subset
negative result remain distinct presentations.

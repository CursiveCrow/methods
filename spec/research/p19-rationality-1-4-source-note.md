Corrections/limits are integrated where the source's claims occur:

1. **Black swan:** not-all-white, not all-nonwhite; one counterexample does
   not prove most-white. Preserve the revision example without adopting
   its stronger majority inference.
2. **Self-evidence:** Goel's primitive psychological coherence notion is
   not a proof that converse relations are mathematically unprovable.
3. **Indeterminacy:** no ordering follows from the supplied premises;
   that does not mean the actual populations have no definite ordering.
4. **Detection/resolution:** source note4 expressly leaves these
   experimentally unresolved. Correctness alone does not observe all
   proposed stages; error does not prove no detection.
5. **Anatomy:** Figure1.4.1i captionBA44/45 versus p123 textBA45/46 is
   preserved as a source discrepancy, not silently resolved.
6. **Meta-analysis categories:** D10's propositional category is broader
   than conditional tasks; direct contrasts differ from category-specific
   activations. The source's less robust conditional claim remains qualified.
7. **2007 lesion scope:** the main record supports a valid-determinate/
   indeterminate crossover with similar inconsistent-item means, more
   precise than an unrestricted generalized-left-impairment claim.
8. **Necessity/sufficiency and reverse inference:** multiple localization
   patterns constrain a uniform-localization account but do not prove
   exclusive processes or region-alone sufficiency. These qualifications
   are attributed/synthetic inferential boundaries, not invented measured
   findings or normative conclusions.

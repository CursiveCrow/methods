## Completed mathematical components

1. Soundness for arbitrary local premises, with separate induction for
   no-premise theorem proofs; exact D/T/B/4 frame correspondences in both
   directions; consistency using a one-world reflexive frame.
2. Finite support and every deduction-theorem case, including a necessitated
   line whose proof must remain premise-free; the consistency alternative;
   boxed finite consequence by repeated deduction, one necessitation and K.
   The empty list of boxed antecedents is included.
3. Lindenbaum extension beginning with the entire arbitrary premise set,
   consistency of all stages and their union, maximality, deductive closure,
   and each primitive Boolean membership case. The construction is not
   advertised as a consistency-decision algorithm.
4. Canonical accessibility and valuation with a separately justified nonempty
   world set; the failure-of-box existence lemma and both directions of the
   diamond formulation.
5. A complete structural truth induction covering atoms, bottom, negation,
   conjunction, disjunction, implication and box, with diamond derived.
6. KD seriality from diamond truth, KTB reflexivity from T and symmetry from
   B, and K4 transitivity from 4. The symmetry proof spells out the
   double-negation/boxed implication needed by the chosen diamond definition.
7. Separate two-direction adequacy theorems for KD, KTB and K4, each including
   satisfiability of every consistent premise set on the required frames.
8. Canonicity under arbitrary valuations and the two local compactness forms.
   No selection of a model for every finite premise subset is used.
9. Six complete finite model calculations separating missing frame conditions,
   KB from KTB, and local from global necessitation.

## Actual proof obligations and source correction

The body will promise three fully proved results: (1) VOD pure and symmetric
mixed equilibria plus macro transformation and monotonicity; (2) p** joint
improvement, independent-domain Pareto optimality and non-equilibrium; (3)
the corrected group-size behavior of provision at p**. Each must have one
reciprocal annex item. Existing expected-utility and finite-game definitions
are actual prerequisites; this chapter does not re-prove all representation or
Nash-existence machinery.

Correction provenance: Raub p. 620 note 5 says provision increases with group
size when each actor uses p** from p. 615. Substitution yields
`P**(N)=1-(K/(NU))^(N/(N-1))`. For admissible `K/U=1/2`,
`P**(2)=15/16`, whereas `P**(3)=1-(1/6)^(3/2)`, which is smaller, since
`(1/6)^3=1/216 > 1/256=(1/16)^2`. The source’s unconditional assertion is
therefore false in its own stated domain. The repaired active treatment gives
the formula, the exact counterexample, the continuous-parameter derivative
criterion `log(NU/K) < N-1`, and the eventual limit 1. The frozen PDF is not
edited. No unverified claim that Diekmann 1986 itself contains the same error
is made.

The p** Pareto statement is stated over independent mixed profiles, as in the
source game. It is not asserted over correlated assignments. A uniform random
selection of exactly one volunteer has payoff `U-K/N` for every actor and
strictly improves on any fully interior independent profile with p** by
minimizing redundant provision costs while guaranteeing provision. This is an
editorial, fully explained limitation of the comparison domain, not a claim
about observed institutional behavior.

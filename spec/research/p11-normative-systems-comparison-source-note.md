## Corrections and boundaries

- Printed14's suggested satisfiable example says to replace the first formula
  by positive warble(Albert), while retaining the negative third formula.
  That still contradicts itself. The active reconstruction replaces the
  negative third formula by the positive one; its given model is then correct.
  The frozen published bytes are preserved. All three one-premise deletions
  have explicit models, showing that logic alone does not choose a repair.
- The source uses subset-symbol⊂ non-strictly and explicitly allows equality.
  That local role is declared and registered rather than silently changed.
- Semantic completeness of a first-order calculus is not completeness of an
  arithmetic axiom theory or truth in the intended natural-number structure.
  Representing arithmetic in FOL does not invalidate its semantic adequacy.
- Bayes' derivation requires positive conditioning probabilities. Its identity
  is distinguished from interpretation and from a rule prescribing updates
  after a particular kind of observation.
- Contraction/expansion are made explicit for nonempty menus on a finite
  option set, with revealed comparison and transitivity. Utility representation
  is proved at that finite scope; arbitrary ordered domains need more assumptions.
- Expected utility requires a complete/transitive preference over lotteries,
  not merely an ordering of certain prizes plus two loosely worded conditions.
  Positive mixture weight is required for strict replacement; at zero both
  mixtures coincide. Existing full finite-prize proofs are linked.
- Menu inconsistency does not by itself imply willingness to pay for every
  trade or stable repetition after losing wealth. The actual source's two-step
  pump is reconstructed conditionally; a refusal policy supplies a countercase.
- NP-completeness of SAT is not proof that P differs from NP, that every
  instance is hard, or that no human can solve a finite consistency problem.
  SAT, UNSAT and entailment are distinguished. FOL undecidability concerns
  an unrestricted uniform task, not every fragment or presented example.
- Fear of flying despite a low risk estimate and saving/spending conflicts
  are illustrations requiring further representation of emotions, goals and
  costs; neither is automatically a formal contradiction or population study.

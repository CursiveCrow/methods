## Content supplied and source distinctions

The body distinguishes potential/actual explanation, explanatory/evidential
quality, the identification/matching/guiding challenges, candidate generation
from considered-set selection, compatible contributors from rivals, and
agnosticism from mandatory winner selection. The contrastive treatment
retains compatible foils, criticism of exclusion and Lewis's original test,
the corresponding-token proposal, open restrictions, and focusing/fixing
backward counterfactuals. It does not replace this with an SCM theorem.

The 1993 underconsideration treatment reconstructs its ranking/no-privilege
premises, contradictory-pair and background replies, iterative/local versus
global arguments, and the source's express limits: no Humean solution, no
perfect ranking, no defeat of every intermediate skeptic or automatic
truth-over-empirical-adequacy conclusion. The background-to-truth connection
is a substantive philosophical premise, not a fabricated mathematical result.

The 1998 prediction treatment preserves the ad-hoc and testing objections,
theory/theorist distinction, choice of tests and fudging proposals. It does
not collapse these into a slogan that any new observation is stronger than
any accommodated one. New printer, thermal-mechanism and transfer/firmware
cases expose actual comparisons, auxiliaries and undercutting/rebutting
failures. They are stipulated examples, not empirical studies.

Seven full reciprocal formal pairs are supplied, all explicitly editorial:
ibe-printer; ibe-raven-logic; ibe-raven-sampling; ibe-bayes-scope; ibe-bonus;
ibe-contradictories; ibe-selection-fit. These do not claim mathematical proofs
of Lipton's philosophical reliability arguments. Every numerical model adds
its probability/sampling premises before the conclusion.

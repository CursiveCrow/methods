## Corrections and reconstruction departures

1. **Original empty-type encoding.** Original p. 53 Theorem 6 uses binary
   `e(A)` in both clauses. On p. 51 `NP_1` is the union of bounds `2^(k*l)`;
   the original empty-type clause is not NP in binary length. The newly added
   abstract discusses a unary unification, but that is not the encoding in
   the original theorem. The current common `n-c-004.tex` and lot1 note call
   the empty-type clause a unary encoding. Root was notified to repair that
   targeted misattribution; this writer did not edit the shared files.
2. **Original length calculation.** The reconstruction uses the exact
   relation-length sum, rather than the printed p. 54 approximation involving
   the auxiliary guessing exponent. It also uses `2^(l-1) <= n < 2^l`, which
   includes powers of two, rather than reproducing the original p. 51 strict
   lower inequality.
3. **Printed binary increment.** On p. 56 the clause for a current zero has
   an existential lower-one antecedent, while the preservation clause applies
   if a lower position is zero or blank. For the intended row representing
   10, whose reversed digits begin `0,1,0,1`, at the third position both
   antecedents hold: preservation requires next bit 0 and the other clause
   requires next bit 1. The intended 10-to-11 increment therefore does not
   satisfy both printed clauses. At the least significant zero position the
   existential lower-one test is also empty and does not force the needed
   update. The reconstruction replaces the block with the disjoint exhaustive
   carry predicate `C(x,y) = all lower positions have digit 1`, followed by
   the three explicit update cases. This is a justified repair of the intended
   construction, not claimed to be the literal unchanged printed formula.
4. **Typed ranges and boundaries.** The reconstruction explicitly restricts
   state and tape functions to the finite coded state/alphabet lists, and G to
   the three digit/blank codes. Without typed ranges, a spurious row could
   evade every machine-table antecedent. The right tape boundary is guarded
   as well as the source's explicit left guard; exponent slack ensures an
   accepting bounded computation is not excluded. Final time does not wrap
   to initial time. States and tape symbols may share underlying elements,
   but their respective code lists are individually injective. The published
   successor symbol S and similarity-type S retain separate declared roles.
5. **Original and editorial statements.** The source theorem is not replaced
   by the slogan ESO=NP. The unary reformulation is derived separately. The
   pure-set LFP nondefinability proof and input-axis table are labeled editorial
   comparisons; no fixed-point capture theorem is attributed to Fagin 1974.

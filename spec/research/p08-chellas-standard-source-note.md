## Direct source-error repairs and OCR boundaries

1. **Printed Theorem 3.20(3), p.106.** The displayed c1/c2/c3 relation need
   not be symmetric, even on a symmetric transitive source model. Let a be
   isolated and {b,c} a complete two-world cluster; set P0={a,b} and
   Gamma={P0,Box P0}. The truth records (P0,Box P0) are a:(1,1), b:(1,0),
   c:(0,0). The printed relation contains [b]→[a] but excludes the reverse
   because c3 would require b to satisfy Box P0. The active theorem and
   proof instead use **c1/c2/c3/c4**, i.e. the transitive c1/c3 relation
   intersected with its converse. Symmetry, transitivity, every original
   edge and both modal truth constraints are proved in the actual text.
   The source PDF is unchanged. This is a correction, not a quotation of
   the historical printed procedure as if it were valid.
2. **Printed G proof, p.184.** At the step boxing l-times boxed Bi
   antecedents, the source says RK^k. The displayed implication requires
   **RK^l**. The active proof uses the correct l-indexed operation and
   handles k/l/m/n=0 and empty conjunctions explicitly. The source's index
   order is diamond^k box^l → box^m diamond^n, not an OCR-permuted variant.
3. **Euclidean figure 3.7, printed p.108.** The actual filtration set is
   **{P0,Box P0}**, one atom and two sentences. OCR suggested {P0,P1}; the
   figure/text image resolves it. The authored obstruction preserves all
   five worlds, all edges, the four resulting records and the forced-edge
   contradiction. The blank OCR pages 120, 150–151 and 165 were likewise
   read from their primary images rather than treated as absent arguments.

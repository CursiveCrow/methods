## Source inconsistencies and restraint in the current treatment

The Sinayev/Peters source contains some numerical/prose details that should not be promoted into harmonized facts without original analysis evidence. For example, its Study 1 methods report reflection alpha 0.48 on p. 4 while Table 3 reports 0.54 on p. 6; the discussion also gives a numeracy alpha different from Table 3. Some discussion wording about which score's lower reliability limits prediction is awkward relative to the surrounding comparison. These values are not used as exact quantitative claims in the manuscript. The body accurately reports the paper's general concern about low internal consistency and multiple constructs, while leaving reliability-model derivation and reconciliation to a separate treatment. The unchanged original source preserves those readings.

Rounded percentages also do not always sum exactly to 100 in the sources' summary tables. No integer participant outcomes were reverse-engineered from them. The selected arithmetic is explicitly approximate where it starts from rounded percentages.

The model's assumption that an intuitive error terminates processing before calculation, and its classification of non-designated errors as evidence of the first stage, are explicitly interpreted as assumptions. Better fit of two specified latent components is not represented as proof that every person traverses those stages or that two causes were separately manipulated. The source's broad claims concerning superior decisions, financial outcomes and the key mechanism are narrowed to the exact association/evidence questions in the selected unit, with the normative and causal premises stated separately.

The source's claims about familiarity effects in other publications were not accepted as independent findings from those unread papers. The body says that the authors raise familiarity and argue that exposure was unlikely in their own samples. It explicitly distinguishes that argument from an exposure experiment. Further primary follow-ups and commentaries found during search remain outside this three-document corpus rather than being counted as confirming or refuting evidence.

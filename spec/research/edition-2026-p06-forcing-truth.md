## Construction and proof scope

The body has seven reciprocal result/proof pairs: name foundations, recursive
definition, density/monotonicity, atomic truth, full formula truth, ZFC transfer,
and the externally countable semantic comparison (seven primary pairs; the
canonical-name atomic calculation is inside the atomic proof).

The name proof supplies physical-rank approximations on transitive closures,
bounded subname closure, intrinsic ranks, ground/ambient agreement, existence
of the all-ordinal stages, evaluation, canonical names, transitivity, and the
evaluated-rank ordinal bound. It invokes the existing **complete** ordinal
recursion and hierarchy-existence theorem pairs, not the old general-recursion
sketch. Atomic forcing is constructed by nested ordinal truth tables on
rank-bounded sets of names; every recursive call lowers the sorted rank pair.
Every fixed logical formula then obtains its own ground defining formula.
There is no all-formula-code internal truth predicate.

The equality truth direction uses one definable dense set of equality
conditions or explicit tagged disagreement witnesses. Atomic negations are
proved within the same rank induction. Full truth uses the primitive
falsehood/negation/conjunction/existential cases and the explicitly verified
expansions of disjunction, implication and universal quantification; it does
not pick infinitely many conditions to prove a true universal assertion.

ZFC transfer constructs exact pair and union names, Separation subnames,
derives ground Collection from Replacement by least ordinal witness bounds,
builds a collected image upper bound and trims it with already proved
Separation, bounds subset names by the ground power set of
`A_sigma x P`, and builds a ground-ordinal-indexed surjection for Choice.
It proves the actual internal Power Set, not the ambient power set.

The ground is an arbitrary transitive **set** membership structure satisfying
ZFC and the forcing poset is arbitrary set forcing with a greatest condition.
No countability, separativity, ccc or closure restriction is used in the
construction/truth/transfer proofs. A generic filter must be supplied. External
countability is used only to construct a generic through each condition and
to compare with the earlier universal semantic definition. The Cohen
nongeneric-filter example demonstrates why that premise cannot be dropped.

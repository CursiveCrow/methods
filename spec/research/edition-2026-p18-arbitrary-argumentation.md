## Direct corrections and additional proved boundaries

The original Lemma 34 on p. 333 says that an inclusion-minimal defense exists from the assertion that every argument has an attacker. That step is not valid for arbitrary infinite frameworks. The new reader construction instead takes the complete set of attackers of current threats. Every added argument still has an even attack walk to the seed. A conflict would give an odd walk to that same seed and violate the seed's noncontroversy. The increasing union then defends every member at its next finite stage; its transfinite complete closure is proved separately.

The explicit counterexample F_nomindefense is uncontroversial and every argument has an attacker. The threats to {a} are b_n, and only x_(k,0), with k >= n, attack b_n. A defense needs an unbounded set of k values; removing any one such value leaves a defense. Hence no inclusion-minimal defense exists, even under the source proof's actual graph premises. The corrected construction nevertheless has the explicitly calculated stable union {a} together with all even-indexed x_(k,2j).

Lemma 34's nonempty-complete-extension conclusion also needs a nonempty argument set. The new statement supplies that hypothesis. The broader coherence, stable-existence and relative-grounding theorems retain the empty framework correctly: its sole extension is the empty set.

The walk definition includes zero-length even walks, as required by the source's initial seed invariant, and permits repeated vertices. It is not silently replaced by simple-path reachability. All uses of Zorn, well-ordering, transfinite recursion and dependent choice are declared. Finitarity is not conflated with finite A or finite stopping time.

Additional proved boundaries include: non-finitary continuity can hold; even a well-founded framework can require stage omega + 1; an acyclic infinite framework can have an infinite backward attack sequence; limited controversy need not imply relative grounding; and a framework with an attack cycle can nevertheless have coincident grounded/complete/preferred/stable semantics.

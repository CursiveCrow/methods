## What the source-support investigation establishes

The source allows nonpositive observed laws and writes
P(x given Pa(X) minus X)>0 for actions considered. The body retains two
explicit parent-assignment readings: positivity on each observed parent
stratum, and a defined positive conditional on every formal parent tuple.
It does not attribute our earlier strict positivity to the source, and
does not call a structurally defined intervention nonexistent.

The implication from either original-query reading to all needed recursive
denominators is FALSE. The body gives a complete counterexample on
A -> M -> Y with A <-> Y. With U fair, A=U, M=A, and private E of probability
1/4, use either Y=(M XOR U) OR E or Y=E. Their observations agree: diagonal
A=M cells have masses 3/8 at Y=0 and 1/8 at Y=1; all cross cells vanish.
Both A values are supported under both readings. Their effects are 5/8
and 1/4. The missing conditional in the graph formula has positive weight
1/2, so dropping the null observed stratum is not a legitimate zero-term
deletion. This refutes the larger input-only condition, rather than leaving
its sufficiency as an unresolved proof promise.

If the support requirement instead quantifies over every value of every
joint action including N, it implies full positivity immediately by taking
the full action; the converse is proved. All proper action sets need not
imply this: two correlated fair variables with no observed parents satisfy
both readings for every proper action, but have zero off-diagonal masses.
Thus the quantifier cannot be changed silently.

## The finite warranted extension

For each current table and requested district slice, Recover first uses
the exact version-invariance criterion. A first zero created inside the
district forces its causal factor to zero. A first zero in another district
does not alone do so. The proof constructs a single model-compatible
conditional version for all districts, including the zero cases, so every
version-invariant cell has the claimed structural meaning.

The second license is explicit: with J=R minus D and B the current observed
parents of J lying in D, require q_R(j,b)>0 for every b with q_R(b)>0.
No hypothesis on null parent tuples is required. The district product
first implies Q[J](j;b)>0 on observed parent strata. A finite repair
argument then fixes a positive-prior district latent tuple and successively
chooses positive-prior complementary latent tuples to lengthen the natural
J-prefix agreeing with j. Earlier D values stay fixed until each first
J difference, by topological induction. In at most |J| repairs, every
possible district outcome after j occurs naturally with j and positive
mass. This proves the zero mask even at formerly null parent tuples.
The final read-back includes this complete argument. It closes the
operation-level observed-stratum support implication, without silently

## Completeness and the exact remaining question

The body defines a finite licensed-trace domain, indexed by the chosen
procedure, order and reference choices. It requires every extraction table
in the full scalar graph traces at original supported actions to be recovered,
with graph failures terminal and no zero-term or certainty shortcut for this
domain test. This domain contains all positive laws and some sparse laws.
Uniformly over finite alphabets and these licensed laws, no original-query
hedge is equivalent to identification and graph-procedure success. Positive
four-valued witnesses remain within the domain and prove the negative direction.
The enhanced scalar zero-product/certainty rules can resolve further inputs
outside this conservative licensed domain, as the worked examples demonstrate.

Not claimed: that every identified mass at every arbitrary sparse law is
resolved by these local recovery rules, or that general do-calculus
completeness on that larger law-dependent domain follows from the positive
theorem. That broader completeness problem remains separate. The original-
action-support implication has already been refuted, and the fixed-binary/

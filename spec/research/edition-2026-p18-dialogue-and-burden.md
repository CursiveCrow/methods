## Direct corrections and declared additions

1. DDF Appendix L4 bars a prior same-speaker assertion, while Section 4.3
   requires participants to assert the recommended action again during
   Confirm after they endorsed it in Recommend. Applied literally, this
   blocks the nonmovers' confirmation. The reader procedure directly
   permits one phase-qualified repeat endorsement per participant in the
   current confirmation round and retains the ordinary no-repeat rule.
   This is an explicit operational reconstruction, not attributed as an
   already stated exception in the preprint.
2. Appendix L8's store-update line names the mover Pi while the rejecting
   speaker is Pj. Its prose and the private-write rule require updating
   the rejecter's own store. The reader rule directly updates the
   rejecter, without telling the reader to apply the erroneous index.
3. Minor i/j slips in entered-speaker/prior-proposal premises are expressed
   as speaker versus some participant, consistently with source prose.
   No participant is allowed to write another's store.
4. The outer DDF locutions are not advertised as a complete evidence
   evaluator. The example declares its cost-evidence threshold,
   counterclaim support, closing and commitment-import policy explicitly.
5. Walton's broad local-burden grouping is not substituted for
   Prakken–Sartor's production/tactical distinction. A changed tactical
   forecast does not relabel an already discharged production burden.
6. Statements about current legal rules, empirical effectiveness,
   guaranteed consensus and objective optimality are not inferred from
   the sources' normative proposals or historical illustrations.

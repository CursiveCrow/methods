## Written components and argument boundaries

These C01–C05 names describe the authorized new primary contribution;
they are not invented replacements for any canonical queue identity.

- C01: `sec:p15-hume-fork`/`experience`: Hume's actual categories,
  causal inference beyond sense/memory, sensible qualities and conditional
  use of mathematics; demonstration and experiential proof distinguished.
- C02: `sec:p15-hume-uniformity`/`replies`: the two propositions and two
  objections, circularity under his factual-inference account, repetitions,
  secret powers, practice and infancy, and his fallible-search qualification.
- C03: `sec:p15-hume-custom`/`probability`: habit as an explanatory
  principle rather than an ultimate cause; belief versus fiction;
  present evidence; variable effects; analogy and noteH's nine factors.
- C04: `sec:p15-hume-connexion`/`scepticism`: all principal candidates,
  objections and replies about productive power; copy/association
  qualifications; cause explications without a false modern-equivalence
  theorem; practical versus foundational enquiry and mitigated caution.
- C05: `def:p15-hume-normative-boundary`, `sec:p15-hume-reference-use`
  and `def:p15-hume-question-record`: causal/epistemic/practical distinctions,
  explicit examples/limits and justified current-method comparisons.

The demonstrative step is not used as a proof of the separate circularity
step. The desired conclusion is not assumed as a fictional exhaustive
justification axiom. IV.16's allowance of a just inference, IV.21's
agent/philosopher distinction and IV.22's warning against declaring an
argument nonexistent from failed search remain integral. Custom's
prevalence/usefulness is not made a bridge proving truth or ethical ought.
VII's regularity, counterfactual gloss and customary transition are not
asserted generally equivalent to each other or to modern do-semantics.
XII's classification/library rhetoric is not adopted as handbook policy.

Hume's examples are historical philosophical illustrations, not captured
controlled experiments. No N, randomization, effect/uncertainty estimate,
clinical diagnosis, treatment or universal mechanism finding is invented.

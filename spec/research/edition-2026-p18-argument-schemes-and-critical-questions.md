## Corrections and scope safeguards

- Defeasible warrants are not turned into material implications.
- The source's broad discussion of co-occurrence is expressed using
  an actual comparison when an association is claimed; mere simultaneous
  occurrence is not presented as a statistical correlation result.
- The witness's statement is distinguished from the truth of its content
  and from additional inferences concerning identity or responsibility.
- The analogy difference question remains CQ1. The source's nearby
  historical discussion sometimes labels a difference as CQ2; that
  numbering slip is not copied.
- Expert schemes, the Carneades encoding, and Godden–Walton's
  weak/strong question distinction are retained as separate presentations.
- A question that embeds an allegation does not get the allegation
  accepted for free. An unanswered question does not prove the negation
  of the original conclusion.
- No historical medical, legal or scientific source case is turned into
  current advice. The worked cases are fictional.

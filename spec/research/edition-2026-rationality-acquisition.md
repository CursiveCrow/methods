# Edition 2026: free Rationality source acquisition and coverage evidence

Date: 7 September 2026. Scope: acquisition and identity/extent verification for
the new reasoning parts. No whole-book holding, full chapter reading or source
acceptance is claimed.

The [publisher record](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262045070/the-handbook-of-rationality/)
identifies *The Handbook of Rationality*, edited by Markus Knauff and Wolfgang
Spohn, MIT Press, 2021, and expressly links an open-access edition. The book DOI
is 10.7551/mitpress/11252.001.0001. Its print ISBN is 9780262045070; the
downloaded publisher-layout chapter exports identify PDF ISBN 9780262366175.
The publisher shop's EPUB ISBN 9780262361859 is a separate format identifier.

The main MIT Direct book route returned 403 in both web and direct HTTP access.
A search-indexed complete-book mirror returned 404 and was not counted as a
holding. Free author/institutional chapter copies succeeded instead. Their
native publisher title/copyright/download surfaces, chapter headings and page
extent were checked; they are not asserted byte-identical to a freshly fetched
current MIT export. The copies remain separate, unmodified artifacts.

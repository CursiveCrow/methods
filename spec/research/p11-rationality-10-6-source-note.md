## Corrections and limitations

The source at 636 infers expected-utility indifference from equal expected
lives; active text supplies its missing affine-utility qualification and
opposite nonlinear counterexamples. At 636 it calls frame reversal a
transitivity violation; active text distinguishes description invariance
from cycles in one fixed preference relation. At 637, risk comparisons
require actual outcome distributions, not only means/variances/need.
Those corrections are integrated, not left as reader repair instructions.

For the actual SI S1/S2 dependency, the full exact payoff is expressed
equivalently using clearly editorial abbreviations `s_i`/`r_i`. The proof
derives every conditional payoff, mixture and affine coefficient, and
handles its sign/tie/positive-factor conditions. Writing `w/(1-w)` extends
the source's algebraically equivalent `1/((1/w)-1)` to the valid `w=0` endpoint.
The supplement's threshold-to-probability description must also be read
with its stated orientation: larger cues indicate repetition, and
cooperation occurs above the threshold. Thus the normal-tail probability,
not its lower-tail CDF, gives cooperation; active text avoids the
supplement's misleading CDF shorthand. No evolutionary-convergence proof
is attributed to a static best-response calculation.

  \(\epsilon\) substitution occurs.
- R02: Definition 1.1 is stated for every \(0<n\leq\omega\), with \(T_n=n^*\),
  the successors, initial-segment order, and lexicographic order. The
  finite-\(n\) definability qualification and \(T_\omega\) exception are exact.

  ordinary automaton have different tuples. Ordinary acceptance is

  emptiness dependencies used in the sentence decision. Theorem 1.4's two

Round 1 used three fresh agents. A Round 1 reviewer could read only this
charter, the six frozen objects, and the two original-detail render sets. It
could not read:

- another review report;
- `ledger.md` or `transcript/chair.md`;
- any Trakhtenbrot board report or transcript;
- `tmp/p09-rabin-section-draft.tex`;
- the layout extraction as controlling evidence; or
- conversation summaries or messages about another seat.

Reviewers may not contact one another. Each must perform a whole-snapshot audit,
not a spot check. Each report must disclose pages inspected, identity results,
evidence boundary, and relevant limitations. Every finding must identify its
severity, exact frozen manuscript location, controlling printed page, defect,
and minimum sufficient repair.

Assigned report files:

- `transcript/r1-source-fidelity.md`;
- `transcript/r1-mathematical-proof.md`;
- `transcript/r1-adversarial-publication.md`.

A reviewer may write only its assigned report and may not edit a frozen object.
It must end on its final line with exactly one of:

- `VERDICT: ACCEPT`; or
- `VERDICT: REJECT —` followed by exact finding identifiers.

This is book scholarship. No test, automated checker, repository script, TeX
command, interim build, handbook-PDF inspection, or build is authorized.

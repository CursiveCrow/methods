## Hostile-reader and terminology audit

The manuscript closes the main avenues for a hostile scholarly challenge:

- The official 1961 article is the controlling body; the 1959 report supplies
  predecessor evidence only, and the 1962 published errata owns every
  correction. Reader 231--236 and 455--462 state this without silent
  1959/1961/1962 conflation.
- The acceptor tuple, the later transducer tuple, a run, a selected final-state
  set, a total output map, a behavior language, and an \(L_1^1\) formula are
  never collapsed into one object.
- The reader does not promote Theorem 7.3 to an omega-acceptance theorem; it
  retains causal synchronous behavior. It does not elevate Corollary 10.6's
  fixed-machine decision result to an existence decision.
- The erratum is not laundered as original article content: the original
  single-relation sentence is called false, the finite-union/tagging
  construction is labelled published correction, and the corrected reduction
  is only then used.

## Terminology and hostile-reader audit

The reader satisfies the AGENTS rule for each material designation: \(\mathcal
I\) is introduced as an interpreted formal language at 499--510; fictitious
occurrence is semantic independence at 512--516; lower/upper forms have
different dependence; anticipation, finite memory, finite operator, and
\(A\)-operator have separate standing; and portion, homogeneous degree, basis,
and transition are properties/data of stream sets rather than invented
automata. The definitions are necessary, eliminable, nonredundant, and used in
the later results.

The source/translation boundary is particularly sound. The official English
record’s two title renderings are retained rather than normalized (487--497);
the bounded reconstruction explicitly declines complete line-by-line
translation (713--715); later English translation is not silently used; and
the announcement's short statements do not receive the expansion's proofs or
numbering as though they had been printed in 1961.

No blocker or must-fix finding remains within the stated bounded scholarly
translation contract. The unverified specialist-language certification is
disclosed above rather than implied.

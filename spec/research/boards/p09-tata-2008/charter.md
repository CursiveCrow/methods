## Mandatory invariant checklist

The labels `T01`--`T22` below are metalinguistic review identifiers, not names
introduced into the mathematical theory. Every reviewer must dispose of every
item expressly.

- **T01 — identity and version.** The title-page author order, title, official
  HAL route, complete-object hash, 18 November 2008 release date, 263/262-page
  custody description, and distinction from the register's evolving-work 2007
  label must agree across the PDF, bibliography, reader, detailed note, and
  compact record.
- **T02 — rights.** The HAL deposit is authorized public research access, not a
  demonstrated Creative Commons or blanket republication licence. The PDF and
  renders remain research evidence, not handbook assets.
- **T03 — finite/infinite domain.** TATA's operative automata receive finite
  ranked terms. They are not Rabin automata on entire infinite labelled trees.
  The introduction's exclusion of infinite-tree automata must remain visible.
- **T04 — ranked terms and tuple.** A finite ranked alphabet, nullary and
  positive-arity term formation, and finite ordered tree standing must be exact.
  TATA's tuple must be `A=(Q,F,Q_f,Delta)`, including ranked alphabet `F` and
  final set before transitions. The source-to-handbook symbol map must be
  explicit and nonconflicting.
- **T05 — runs and recognition.** The transition rule, bottom-up run, final-root
  acceptance, and recognizable-language definition must be correctly typed for
  all arities, including nullary rules.
- **T06 — determinization.** Deterministic must mean at most one target for a
  fixed symbol and child-state tuple. Theorem 1.1.9 must retain same-language
  strength and reachable subset states, without claiming source details not
  reconstructed.
- **T07 — closure and emptiness.** Theorem 1.3.1's effective Boolean closure and
  Theorem 1.7.4's accessible-state marking route must be exact. Nonemptiness
  occurs exactly when a final state becomes marked. The reader/main theorem
  must not inflate the linear-time result beyond the source's representation
  or proof boundary.
- **T08 — small run ownership.** The finite run on `f(a,b)` must be correct,
  must state why deletion of its root rule leaves no run, and must be marked as
  handbook-owned rather than source-printed.
- **T09 — WSkS semantics and restricted atoms.** First-order variables range
  over words; set variables range over finite word sets. Proposition 3.3.1 and
  the four atomic forms `X subseteq Y`, `Sing(X)`, `X=Yi`, and `X=epsilon`
  must be exact.
- **T10 — finite-set encoding.** The alphabet arities, `0/1/bottom` coordinate
  meanings, and Example 3.3.3 labels for
  `S_1={epsilon,11}`, `S_2=empty`, and `S_3={11,22}` must agree with printed
  pp. 90--91. Nullary bottom padding must not be replaced by an imprecise
  all-zero-track slogan.
- **T11 — bridge to finite-tree MSO.** The finite position set `D`, symbol-label
  subsets, label and child-atom translations, restriction of element
  quantifiers to `D`, and restriction of monadic quantifiers to subsets of `D`
  must be explicit. The ambient prefix tree is a coding structure, not the
  automaton's infinite input.
- **T12 — Lemma 3.3.4.** The restricted-formula-to-automaton direction must
  preserve the complete one-state transition list for `X subseteq Y`, the
  missing-coordinate step for disjunction, union, complement for negation, and
  track projection for existential set quantification.
- **T13 — Lemma 3.3.6.** The converse must quantify one position set per state,
  require a partition of exactly the encoded finite domain, require a final
  root state, and state the local transition clause with the correct implication,
  disjunction of matching transitions, and child-state direction.
- **T14 — theorem qualification and closed sentences.** TATA's statement that
  Theorem 3.3.7 is “not very precise” must be preserved, with Lemmas 3.3.4 and
  3.3.6 controlling the exact directions. For a closed sentence, the alphabet
  is empty or contains only the nullary constant `top`, and validity reduces to
  a rule `top -> q_f` for a final state.
- **T15 — Exercise 1.4.** The exact ranked alphabet
  `{a^(0),f^(2)}` and language `{f(t,t):t in T(F)}` must be retained. The
  handbook-owned deterministic separation proof must justify that every term
  first reaches a state and that two distinct terms cannot reach one state.
- **T16 — weak/full boundary.** Full SkS permits arbitrary, possibly infinite,
  subsets of the successor tree and requires root-down automata on infinite
  inputs with a different complementation argument. Weak and full set
  quantification coincide only on an actually finite structure; that fact does
  not turn the automaton input into an infinite tree.
- **T17 — shared main theorem.** The shared definition at lines 845--874 and
  theorem at lines 876--891 must agree with the TATA presentation on ranked
  trees, bottom-up automata, recognition, MSO correspondence, effective
  operations, and the Rabin nonconflation boundary.
- **T18 — proof and reconstruction boundary.** The reader may give operative
  constructions without claiming to reproduce every state table or every proof
  of Theorems 1.1.9, 1.3.1, and 1.7.4. Every source proof, source exercise,
  handbook example, and handbook proof must retain its actual ownership.
- **T19 — Thatcher--Wright non-substitution.** The article identity and
  abstract-level scope may be reported, but no uninspected algebraic signature,
  recognition relation, generalized Kleene statement, weak logic, theorem,
  example, or proof may be reconstructed from TATA. RC-1713 remains an
  acquisition lead only. The open owed artifact must not name a hypothetical
  answer.
- **T20 — bibliography and cross-record agreement.** Author order, date,
  theorem numbering, page locators, notation, source ownership, bibliography,
  reader, shared main text, detailed note, compact record, and acquisition note
  must agree without a stale status contradiction. In any round that freezes
  the adjacent Thomas evidence, the Thomas record must identify the acquired
  object as the May 1996 report precursor, preserve the report's literal
  prospective title separately from the eventual publication title, and keep
  the registered 1997 chapter body and reconciliation open.
- **T21 — terminology rule.** Every new technical name must have local
  necessity, exact definition or source standing, object/relation/property or
  metalinguistic standing, nonredundancy, and later use. Established source
  terms must still be typed before reliance. An open obligation must be stated
  as an owed artifact rather than assigned speculative terminology.
- **T22 — completion scope.** The frozen records must say that acquisition and
  reconstruction exist but independent review and admission remain open. A
  final `ACCEPT` may admit only `N-c-015.b` manuscript content on these hashes;
  it may not imply `N-c-015.a`, `N-c-015` overall, Part IX, build, visual,
  human, user, book, or publication completion.

## Seat mandates

### Source fidelity and evidence

map, author order, tuple and symbol order, every quoted formula and transition,
numbered-result role, page locator, attribution, proof ownership, and agreement
among all frozen records. Inspect every mandated page. A source mismatch,
unread required page, identity mismatch, silent source repair, or body-dependent
Thatcher--Wright claim is at least a must-fix.

### Mathematical and proof correctness

Independently reconstruct the definitions and every material inference:
determinization typing, bottom-up reachability and emptiness, the small run,
finite-set encoding, both logical directions, the local run clause,
closed-sentence reduction, the Exercise 1.4 separation, finite-tree MSO bridge,
and weak/full boundary. Stress-test nullary symbols, missing runs, partial
transition tables, unreachable states, empty sets, empty free-variable
alphabets, and domain restrictions. A type error, quantifier error, invalid
proof, false equivalence, or theorem-strength inflation is at least a must-fix.

### Adversarial publication and terminology

Try to falsify the reconstruction across finite/infinite, weak/full,
source/handbook, historical/modern, access/licence, proof/outline, and
acquisition/admission boundaries. Audit the five-part terminology rule at each
actual first use, adjacent authority boundaries, bibliography, status language,
and all cross-file agreement. A premature technical name, undefined standing,
hidden source substitution, stale completion claim, or cross-record
contradiction is at least a must-fix.

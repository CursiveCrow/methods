## Evaluation and extracted operations

**Sound evaluation — annex lines 70–106.** For each model A of E the family of term-evaluation kernels is an admissible congruence family. Leastness therefore gives representative independence without presupposing a separate equational-completeness theorem. The evaluation substitution law is valid for every existing assignment. When A is empty and n>0, assertions over A^n are vacuous; when n=0 its assignment set is the singleton zero-power. A signature with a nullary symbol cannot have an empty-carrier algebra, so there is no impossible constant evaluation hidden in this argument.

**Finite products under evaluation — annex lines 94–106.** The target of an interpreted power projection is `M_A(1)=A^1`, not A. The comparison is explicitly `A^n→(A^1)^n`, sending each coordinate to its singleton tuple, with the correctly typed inverse. It is bijective when n=0 and when A is empty. Block concatenation similarly identifies `A^(n+m)` with `A^n×A^m`. Thus preservation follows from actual product cones rather than a silent strictness convention.

**Algebra homomorphisms — annex lines 108–123.** Induction proves preservation of all terms, including nullaries. Its componentwise version is exactly naturality for each tuple arrow. **Recovery — body lines 187–197; annex lines 129–173.** The extracted operation `M(barσ)∘(c_k^M)^(-1)` has domain `M(1)^k` and codomain `M(1)`. The term-induction identity `t^(A_M) c_n^M=M(bar t)` is correct in both the variable and operation cases. The k=0 induction step explicitly uses the unique map to the singleton zero-power. Equality of axiom arrows and surjectivity of c_n then prove each equation under every assignment. A nullary operation forces `M(1)` nonempty through the stated singleton-to-carrier map.

## Naturality, equivalence, and restrictions

**All transformations and their components — annex lines 175–202.** Naturality at the selected projections gives `c_n^N α_n=(α_1)^n c_n^M`; the n=0 case is supplied separately by terminal uniqueness. Naturality at a basic operation gives the algebra-homomorphism equation with correctly placed inverse comparisons. Thus extraction is functorial and no component can vary independently of α_1. Conversely the evaluation functor and comparison isomorphisms transport any algebra homomorphism between extracted algebras to a natural transformation, so the argument covers all homomorphisms, not only isomorphisms.

**Two levels of model naturality — annex lines 204–222.** Equation (4) proves c^M is a natural transformation in the theory object. Equation (3) gives naturality in the model M itself. Both are needed for the equivalence and both are present. Empty target tuples impose no additional condition because the relevant codomains are singleton zero-powers.

**Singleton comparison and triangles — annex lines 224–248.** The algebra `LK(A)` really has carrier A^1. The map `η_A:a↦(a)` and its inverse preserve every operation; the displayed formula for the transported operation on singleton tuples verifies this, including arity zero. Naturality at an algebra homomorphism is the given `h^1 η_A=η_B h`. For the counit `ε_M=(c^M)^(-1)`, the first triangle follows from `K(η_A)_n=c_n^(K(A))`. The second follows because `c_1^M` is exactly singleton formation, so `L(ε_M)` extracts the sole coordinate. These are equalities of the required natural transformations/algebra homomorphisms, not merely objectwise assertions of isomorphism.

**Empty and nonempty restrictions — body lines 207–216; annex lines 250–257.** The singleton comparison remains a bijection for A empty, and zero-powers remain singleton sets. Nonemptiness is preserved and reflected by A↦A^1; the extracted carrier is exactly M(1). Therefore K, L, unit and counit all restrict to the specified full subcategories. The proof does not assert that this restriction includes every Set-valued model.

**Structural-collapse example — body lines 228–239.** In the empty signature, the equation `(2,x_0,x_1)` gives exactly the all-carrier algebras of cardinality at most one. The quotient identifies the two projections `2→1`. It does not create a term in the empty context, so no arrow `0→1` appears. The empty evaluation model is initial because its positive components are empty and its zero component is singleton; its unique componentwise map to any model is natural. The nonempty models have singleton generator carriers. Thus the example's forward pointer to the empty initial model is mathematically consistent.

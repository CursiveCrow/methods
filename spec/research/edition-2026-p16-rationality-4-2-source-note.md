## Corrections and boundary conditions to retain

The publisher's printed Theorem 2 on p. 262 has i>j together with f_i>f_j
and h_i<h_j, contradicting the preceding NRF2/NRF3 monotonicities.
The active theorem is corrected to f_i<f_j and h_i>h_j, with a full
weighted-covariance identity proof and a two-state counterexample to the
uncorrected printed criterion. Its original remains frozen in the PDF.
Conditioning also requires P(F)>0, absent from the printed theorem.

The lack of a logical entailment between F and H does not mathematically
exclude direct probabilistic dependence. The common-parent Y is a motivated
modeling proposal, not a deduction from that lack of entailment.
Collider conditioning does not necessarily create dependence for every
numerical table; the body separates graph-guaranteed independences from
parameter-dependent associations. The two-report models use independence
conditional on H (and, in the shared model, on reliability), not unconditional
independence of the reports. The soft modus-ponens argument states its
actual updating and strictness assumptions instead of claiming an
unqualified increase from every possible partial-premise change.

The proceedings legal study reports 126 participants but its Study 1
ANOVA uses residual df 98 without explaining that analysis-count mismatch.
Its Study 2 guilt contrast is nonsignificant, although credibility changes
are significant. These limitations must remain explicit; no missing
participants or significance are supplied by inference.

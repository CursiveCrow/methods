## Repairs and proof standing

The mathematical proof annex supplies image quantification/conditional
diagonal; every partial equality and description case; the distinction
between rigid and locally coincident terms; full-concept operation
replacement; universal box soundness; both nonempty-domain actual-quantifier
countermodels; exact arbitrary-family representation; S4 soundness and
the singleton-core obstruction; binder replacement; explicit finite-history
countermodels; and the complete two-index counterpart countermodel.

Source-local `V^(I)` means partial functions and differs from `V^I`.
The source `equiv` means concept equality, and source bars/dots/tildes
retain their typed meanings; they are not normalized into unrelated house
roles. Formula substitutions are capture-free. A pointed nonempty I is
declared for the modern frame comparison rather than being misreported
as an explicit source ban on an empty index set.

The source p.161 suggested logical-operation criterion is not accepted
as a proved four-operation classification: permutation invariance alone
also allows constant true and constant false maps, shown explicitly.
The p.168–173 equivocal cross-identification discussion is resolved in
the exposition by retaining its actual hypotheses: rigid equality is
recoverable from universal incidence under injective full state histories;
it is not recovered under a nonseparating property family. General term
concepts have a second function layer, and universal diagonal incidence
need not give equality of denoted histories. Explicit finite countermodels
implement the distinction without adopting the abandoned illustrative defense.

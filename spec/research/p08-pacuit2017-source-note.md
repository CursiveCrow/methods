## Direct corrections and justified additions

1. The source p.63 cardinality remark is not used as an unconditional
   theorem about arbitrary L or finite/empty alphabets. The obstruction
   to minimum EM supplementation instead constructs distinct finite-chain
   theories and a diagonal non-proof set wholly in the complement of the
   chosen proof set. This repairs the p.65 union argument's missing
   disjointness dependency and the stray `EC` subscript there.
2. On p.64 the truth induction does not need the source's restriction
   to consistent formulas. It is given for all formulas, including bottom.
3. “Finite intersections” in the C argument means positive arity.
   The empty intersection is the independent unit, not an implicit C axiom.
4. On p.72 inconsistent descriptions are excluded before choosing any
   Lindenbaum representative. The converse of Lemma 2.18 is proved from
   L-consistency and maximal decisions. It does not infer that every
   L-theorem is a propositional tautology; an EN theorem such as boxed
   truth already shows why that inference would be false.
5. In the p.73–74 boxed label argument, RE gives a theorem whose membership
   in each maximal theory suffices. Lemma 2.18 is not reapplied outside its
   Boolean-observation domain. Finite-canonical truth is proved before
   using it in the counterframe argument.
6. The p.74 non-iterative finite-model result is separated from effective
   decidability: the manuscript requires a finite effective schema list
   over E, as the p.71–72 setup does. No claim of deciding an arbitrary
   nonrecursive axiom set is made.
7. The p.76 sentence attributing the exhaustive bound to exponentially
   many subformulas is repaired. Distinct expanded subformulas are at most
   linear in syntax size; truth-value descriptions/worlds are exponential.
   The bounded-generator encoding gives the stated double-exponential
   exhaustive bound without pretending to prove the cited sharp complexity.
8. On p.78 the root membership assertion is not sufficient as printed.
   The replacement proof uses previously evaluated old-component truth
   sets as root generators and ensures old truth preservation by exact
   inverse-image neighborhoods. E uses both root variants; EC uses their
   positive intersection closure. There is no self-referential root definition.
9. The p.79 contextual box-M and box-E rule displays are not sound for
   arbitrary unchanged contexts. The active text requires separately valid
   theorem-level matrices and gives a unit two-world countermodel for both
   contextual versions. Source C-folding is retained in its sound direction;
   a fully proved positive-conjunction characterization handles EC without
   adding M or a zero-fold intersection.
10. The source regular impossible-world semantics is reconstructed and
    supplemented by a full regular relational canonical proof; this
    editorial extension is not misattributed as a verbatim source proof.
    The augmented strong-completeness exercise likewise has its complete
    relational canonical successor construction, rather than an imported
    unproved K completeness assertion.

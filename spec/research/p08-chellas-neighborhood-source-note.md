## Direct corrections made in the new exposition

The formal statements and proofs were written correctly in the active
body; readers are not instructed to repair an earlier false assertion.
The historical distinctions are recorded here:

- **Printed p.209, interval explanation.** With only exact open intervals
  in the neighborhood family, the box clause means the entire truth set
  is such an interval. Containing an interval is the supplemented clause.
  The source prose's local-persistence reading therefore needs the
  supplementation. The new theorem gives the two exact models and a
  truth set consisting of an interval plus an isolated point, proving
  their difference without conflating them.
- **Theorem 9.19, p.263.** The printed condition is imposed on included
  extra non-proof sets, whereas frame condition (v) constrains omitted
  sets. With no extra sets the printed antecedent is vacuous, but
  the smallest E5 canonical model can fail (v) when N is absent.
  The new proof constructs a two-world E5-model with boxed truth false,
  its maximal true theory, and a diagonal non-proof set using explicitly
  specified identity-model theories. The needed negative extension is
  the whole canonical world set, which is absent at the chosen world.
  The corrected criterion concerns omitted non-proof sets in the final
  neighborhood assignment. Largest-E5 and smallest-ET5 source results
  remain proved with their actual constructive choices.
- **Exercises 7.51/9.47, pp.230, 266--267.** A Boolean subalgebra generated
  by subformula values need not be closed under the old modal operation.
  Agreement on every subalgebra input can consequently be impossible.
  The new finite operation projects the old output to its greatest
  subalgebra lower bound and proves agreement on the represented
  modal outputs that the truth induction actually needs. It proves
  preservation of M/C/N and gives a powerset example whose old modal
  output lies outside the generated subalgebra.

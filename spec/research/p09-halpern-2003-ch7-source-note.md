# Halpern 2003 Chapter 7: source and access note

Retrieved 2026-08-22. Scope: Part IX acquisition gap `N-x-015`, specifically
Joseph Y. Halpern, *Reasoning about Uncertainty*, 2003 hardcover, Chapter 7.
Only publisher, author, named-library, and library-consortium sources were used.
The 2005 revised paperback, the 2017 second edition, and Fagin--Halpern--Megiddo
1990 are recorded only to prevent substitution; none supplies technical text
attributed here to the 2003 chapter.

## Decision

**Acquisition verdict: OPEN - the 2003 Chapter 7 body was not lawfully acquired
or read in this pass.**

The exact 2003 print object and Chapter 7's table-of-contents architecture are
source-identified. No inspected online source exposes printed pp. 239--286 as a
readable 2003 body. The MIT Press page is descriptive only; the Library of
Congress and named-library records are catalog records; Google Books marks the
2003 volume `noview` and not searchable; the currently visible Internet
Archive scan is an access-restricted **2005 paperback**, despite mixed catalog
metadata; its older Open Library-linked 2003 lead is now dark; and the public
library-consortium PDF contains only the title page and contents.

Accordingly, this note does **not** state Chapter 7's exact grammars, semantic
tuples, measurability or additivity assumptions, satisfaction clauses, axiom
systems, theorem numbers, soundness/completeness hypotheses, small-model or
decision results, proof boundaries, or worked examples. Those fields remain
open rather than being reconstructed from the 2005 or 2017 edition or from an
adjacent paper.

### Why “first edition” is safe, but “first printing” is not supplied here

Halpern's [author publication page](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/halpern/abstract.html)
says the book was published by MIT Press in 2003, that a “slightly revised”
paperback followed in 2005, and that a second edition followed in 2017. His
[Cornell CS 6766 course page](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/courses/cs6766/2019fa/)
explicitly calls the earlier book the “first edition” while distinguishing the
2017 second edition. These first-party statements support **2003 first-edition
hardcover**.

The inspected lawful title/contents scan does not include a copyright page or
printer's number line. This note therefore does not identify a particular
printing or impression.

for the following contents-level locators when cited with this qualification;

## Later editions and adjacent papers kept separate

### 2005 paperback

The [author page](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/home/halpern/abstract.html) calls
the 2005 paperback “slightly revised.” The [MIT Press record](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262582599/reasoning-about-uncertainty/)

also place Chapter 7 at p. 239. Shared pagination does not prove page identity,
especially in the face of the author's express revision statement. Its
technical preview was therefore not used to fill any 2003 field.

### 2017 second edition

The [MIT Press second-edition record](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262533805/reasoning-about-uncertainty/)
identifies a distinct 2017 edition and describes new material. The [Google
Books second-edition contents](https://books.google.com/books?id=ih-QDgAAQBAJ)
place Chapter 7 at p. 245 rather than p. 239. Halpern's 2019 course page says the
second edition adds material and corrects typos and minor problems in the first
edition. These facts establish that a silent second-edition substitution is
unsafe; they do not locate particular Chapter 7 changes.

### Fagin--Halpern--Megiddo 1990 and other author articles

The 1990 probability-logic paper and Halpern's author-hosted papers on
likelihood, knowledge and probability, expectation, and related logics may
establish their own languages and theorems. They do not establish which
notation, theorem numbering, hypotheses, proof selection, or examples appear
in the 2003 book. No result from them is attributed in this note to Chapter 7.

## What would close `N-x-015` for Halpern 2003

A source-qualified acquisition must include readable images or an authorized
text of printed pp. 239--286 from ISBN 0-262-08320-5, together with enough
front matter to bind those pages to the 2003 hardcover (at minimum the title
and copyright/ISBN pages). A lawful physical consultation at a named holding
library, a library-supplied chapter scan (Cornell's catalog exposes one
possible request route), a publisher copy, or an author-hosted first-edition
manuscript would suffice.

Until then, Part IX may safely cite the chapter title, section architecture,
and contents-level page starts above. It may not cite Halpern 2003 for a
particular formal grammar, semantic tuple, axiom system, theorem number,
completeness hypothesis, small-model bound, complexity result, proof, or
example. The Halpern 2003 presentation remains **OPEN and blocking at the
technical-body level**.

## Corrections required by the source figures and domains

- Page 531 prose calls the path of (BC,XZ) B-X; Figure 9.1.1 and its caption
  correctly give B-Z. The reconstruction uses B-Z.
- Page 534 prose interchanges the letters M and N relative to its caption.
  Keep M=2/3 U+1/3 D for player 1 and N=1/3 L+2/3 R for player 2.
  The exit action is X in the tree/table, although the caption calls it E.
- The page 534 statement that every finite game has a behavioral SPE is
  qualified by perfect recall. The chapter later explicitly permits imperfect
  recall and absentmindedness; these are not covered by the finite mixed-to-
  behavioral bridge. A finite absentminded counterexample in the annex has
  mixed Nash equilibria but no behavioral Nash equilibrium.
- Page 537 calls (L,DX,L), equivalently (LL,DX), an equilibrium of Figure
  9.1.6a. The visible payoffs make this false: player 2 gains from 0 to 1 by
  changing the reached left-node move D to X. A valid comparison uses
  (L,XX,L), respectively (LL,XX), and retains the memory/role distinction.
- The local interior-mixing comparative-statics claim needs a maintained
  completely mixed, nondegenerate branch. It is not a statement that arbitrary
  payoff changes leave one's own equilibrium mixture unchanged globally.

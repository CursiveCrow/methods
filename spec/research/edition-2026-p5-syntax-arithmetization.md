## Exact mathematical choices and correspondence

1. **Three distinct coding roles.** Syntax uses `C(a,b)=1+pair_N(a,b)` and tagged trees. Annotated ND derivation records use outer tag 15; certified proofs use tag 16. 2. **The primitive-recursive assertion is proved.** Pairing inverse uses a bounded search. Positive cons has strictly smaller head/tail components, so every natural is a finite raw list. Course-of-values recursion is implemented by a primitive-recursively accumulated row list. The canonical-string parser uses the displayed `B(k,q)` recursion and bounded candidate search; it is not justified merely by decidability. No efficiency or polynomial bit-size claim is made.

3. **The operative substitution is preserved.** Numeral substitution never renames an unrelated binder because its replacement term is closed. The arbitrary-term routine must rename whenever the source's third quantifier clause applies, even if the variable being substituted is absent freely. Its preliminary fresh renaming changes only names and tree shape is preserved. Consequently at most n main visits and n renaming traversals of at most n nodes suffice; the stated frame update is PR and a conservative `20(n+1)^2` macro-step bound makes the whole operation PR. This counts fixed PR frame updates, not single Turing-machine transitions or bit operations.

4. **Alpha is syntactic.** Comparison records distinguish free indices and nearest-binder positions. Invariance under the source generators proves one direction; choosing finitely many shared globally fresh binder names and renaming outside-in proves the converse. No semantic equivalence or new formula-equivalence rule is admitted.

5. **All twenty actual rule forms are checked.** The record includes sufficient terms, matrices, indices and discharge labels to check the actual rule instance. Open assumptions are lists of leaf occurrences, not just root formulas or undifferentiated sets. Discharge is nonvacuous and specific to the indicated formula, label and premise branch. The forall-I test uses FV; the exists-E test uses all variable occurrences in the conclusion, the major existential formula and surviving assumptions. Equality elimination permits a matrix with zero occurrences of its substitution variable. Alpha conversion is a separate rule. The only source-display convention translated is the order of the two implication-elimination premises: the body and registry reverse them; the two child positions are explicitly exchanged.

6. **Axiom-instance wording is made exact for this calculus.** ND_FOL has no additional logical-axiom schema. A finite nonlogical theory is a fixed finite list of sentences; membership is a finite equality disjunction. Universal instantiation is carried out by the real forall-E rule with its supplied term, and a supplied sequence of such instantiations is checked by repeated substitution. The theorem now says finite-axiom membership and explicitly specified axiom instantiations. It does not introduce an unspecified schematic closure of a finite axiom set. 7. **The fixed index is a domain index.** `e_T` is an index in the unchanged accepted numbering with domain exactly the governing syntax codes of T. `V(e,a,s)` is the numerical characteristic function of the accepted bounded Comp formula. The structural PR evaluator uses the accepted marked-bounded presentation, whose full first-order expansion has the same truth value. This is separate from uniform evaluation of arbitrary formulas. A proof certificate supplies one accepting history per surviving open axiom leaf; temporary discharged assumptions do not require T membership. No computable bound on the time or size of a successful axiom witness is asserted.

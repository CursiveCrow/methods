## Two printed inconsistencies and their direct corrections

The image of p. 763 really prints a head-selection condition in its reduct
display, while p. 759 selects the rule body. The later semantics and source
cases require the body selector. The active definition therefore uses
T^I = {head: head <- body is a rule and I satisfies body}.
The complete first annex item supplies a discriminating example:
rules p<-q, q<-q, not-p<-not-p at world not-p,q. Head-selection wrongly
gives a unique consistent reduct; body-selection gives p,q,not-p and excludes
the world. This is a demonstrated correction, not a silent source hybrid.

The image of p. 767 declares domains {1,2,3} for its cycling constants,
but the displayed rules/completion use 0,1,2, and p. 770 explicitly declares
the corresponding C+ fluent's domain {0,1,2}. The active cycling example
uses {0,1,2}. The original three-valued nonmonotonicity example on p. 763
legitimately uses {1,2,3} and is unchanged. Retained PDF bytes are unchanged.

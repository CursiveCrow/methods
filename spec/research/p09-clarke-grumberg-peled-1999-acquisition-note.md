# Clarke--Grumberg--Peled, *Model Checking* (1999): acquisition note

**Requested source:** Edmund M. Clarke, Jr., Orna Grumberg, and Doron A.
Peled, *Model Checking*, MIT Press, first edition, 1999, ISBN
0-262-03270-8 / 978-0-262-03270-4.

**Disposition:** NO ANONYMOUSLY READABLE, COMPLETE FIRST-EDITION BODY OR
AUTHOR-ACCEPTED MANUSCRIPT WAS LOCATED ON THE AUDITED PUBLISHER, AUTHOR,
INSTITUTIONAL-REPOSITORY, GOOGLE BOOKS, HATHITRUST, OR INTERNET ARCHIVE
ROUTES. A SEARCHABLE GOOGLE BOOKS PREVIEW, AN INTERNET ARCHIVE
ACCESS-RESTRICTED LENDING SCAN, AND INSTITUTION-SUBSCRIPTION EBOOK RECORDS DO
EXIST. NONE WAS USED AS A COMPLETE BODY.

This is a route-bounded result as of 22 August 2026, not a claim that no copy
exists anywhere. In this note, “public complete body” means that an anonymous
reader can read or download the complete first-edition text through a
publisher-, author-, or institution-controlled route. Catalogue metadata,
search snippets, a partial preview, controlled borrowing, and
institution-authenticated subscription access do not meet that threshold.

of 330 pages, and marks it “Out of print.” The

The formal citation controlled by those records is:

> Clarke, Edmund M., Jr., Orna Grumberg, and Doron A. Peled. *Model
> Checking*. Cambridge, MA: MIT Press, 1999. ISBN 0-262-03270-8.

The edition is not retitled “first edition” on the first-edition product
record. It is identified as the first edition because the
[MIT Press second-edition page](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262038836/model-checking/)
expressly calls the 1999 book the first edition.

### Catalogue discrepancies that must not change the identity

- MIT Press currently renders the third author's surname as `Peleg` on the
  first-edition product page. The Library of Congress responsibility statement,
  Google Books record, Open Library record, and the later MIT Press edition
  identify Doron A. **Peled**. The publisher-page spelling is a current
  metadata defect, not a different authorship.
- MIT Press reports `330 pp.`; the Library of Congress reports `xiv, 314 p.`;
  and [Google Books volume `Nmc4wEaLXFEC`](https://books.google.com/books/about/Model_Checking.html?id=Nmc4wEaLXFEC)
  reports 314 pages. These displays use different extent conventions or contain
  unresolved metadata variation. No complete copy was available to adjudicate
  them by physical inspection.
- Clarke's current CMU publications index places the book under “Books and
  Edited Volumes” and displays `MIT Press, 2000`, whereas MIT Press, the
  Library of Congress, Google Books, and the second-edition publisher text fix
  the first edition to 1999. The source record used here is therefore 1999;
  the author-index display is preserved as a conflicting bibliography display,
  not silently substituted.
- Open Library gives `January 7, 1999` as its edition date. MIT Press's exact
  product date, 20 December 1999, controls the release date in this note.

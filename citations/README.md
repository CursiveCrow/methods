# Methods of Reason citations

**141 complete source files**: **137 PDFs, three HTML specifications and one PostScript manuscript**. Resource size: **481.8 MiB**.

[Metadata spreadsheet](metadata.csv) · [Structured catalogue](manifest.json) · [Source path map](FILENAME-MAP.csv) · [Removal log](REMOVALS.csv) · [Verification](VERIFICATION.json)

The collection retains one preferred complete copy of each identified work. Complete parent volumes replace separately held chapters; complete independent chapters remain where their parent book is not held. Published/final copies are preferred to duplicate drafts. Where only a complete author manuscript is held, its version remains explicit in the filename and metadata.

Previews, incomplete extracts, contents/front-matter files, citation-only exports, acquisition archives, incomplete manual snapshots, redundant source formats and superseded copies were removed. The original source/acquisition files elsewhere in the project remain untouched.

Three complete papers were isolated from mixed packets: Eisinger and Ohlbach (48 pages), Büchi (11 pages), and Julia Robinson (2 pages). Every extracted page was checked against its source for identical text and rendering. The Eisinger/Ohlbach source packet was previously mislabeled as a complete journal issue; its authorship and title are now corrected.

The remaining HTML specifications contain their complete substantive text; their remotely referenced logos are presentation assets. The OWL structural-syntax HTML was removed because its substantive diagram files were absent.
